# Microcosms of the links: ZNPP's power lines, repair crews, substations, crossings and water (2022–2026)

Research date: 29 October 2026. About 30 searches and fetches. Most sources are news outlets that relay IAEA, Ukrenergo, Energoatom or Ukrainian ministry statements. The IAEA's own pages (iaea.org) and the globalsecurity.org mirrors of IAEA Updates mostly blocked direct fetching (HTTP 402/403), so some IAEA wording is known only through search snippets and secondary outlets. Each such case is marked below.

## 1. Repair crews and IAEA-brokered ceasefire repairs (the strongest microcosm material)

### Takeaway
Since October 2025 every reconnection of the ZNPP has needed a negotiated local ceasefire. There have been seven by September 2026. Each follows the same steps: Grossi negotiates, then demining, then repair crews work inside a zone of about 1.5 km radius while IAEA staff watch from both sides of the front, then reconnection, then the line is cut again. This repeating sequence is a small-scale version of the whole "hostage" relationship. The repair sites themselves are documented only roughly: "about 3 km from the site perimeter", "about 1.8 km from the thermal plant's switchyard", "high pylons across the Dnipro". I found no publicly posted video of the crews at work.

### Cited Findings
- **The month-long outage, autumn 2025.** The plant ran on diesel generators from 23 September 2025, when its last external line was severed. Each side blames the other. Repairs began on 18 October 2025 under "local ceasefire zones" that Grossi announced on X. — [Al Jazeera, 18 Oct 2025](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/18/zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-repairs-begin-in-ukraine-as-ceasefire-zones-set)
- **Two phases, per AP citing a European diplomat.** Phase 1 was a 1.5 km-radius ceasefire zone to repair the Dniprovska 750 kV line, where the damage was in Russian-controlled territory. Phase 2 was a second zone for the Ferosplavna-1 330 kV backup line, in Ukrainian-controlled territory. The Russian-appointed plant management credited "close cooperation" between the IAEA and Rosatom. The plant's Telegram channel said the Russian Defence Ministry would play a key role in the safety of the work. Grossi said: "Both sides engaged constructively with the IAEA to enable complex repair plan to proceed." — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/18/zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-repairs-begin-in-ukraine-as-ceasefire-zones-set); see also [Liga/AP summary](https://news.liga.net/en/amp/politics/news/ap-iaea-to-restore-power-to-znpp-in-two-stages-as-planned)
- **Dniprovska restored, 23 October 2025.** The Dniprovska repair was completed on 23 October 2025, after about a month on diesel (via search snippet of IAEA Update 327). — [globalsecurity.org mirror of IAEA Update 327](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2025/11/ukraine-251108-iaea01.htm) (fetch blocked; snippet only)
- **Further damage on Ferosplavna-1.** After off-site power was restored, the IAEA reported more damage on Ferosplavna-1, about 1.8 km from the open switchyard of the Zaporizhzhia thermal power plant (Ukrainian-language search snippet of an Interfax-Ukraine item). — [Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1121313-amp.html) (snippet only). Further reporting says the extra damage was "closer to the plant itself but outside of the initially agreed ceasefire zones", which delayed reconnection. — [mezha.net](https://mezha.net/eng/bukvy/iaea-secures-local-truce-near-zaporizhzhia-npp-for-330-kv-backup-line-repair/) (via search summary)
- **Ferosplavna-1 repaired and reconnected, 8 November 2025.** On 7 November 2025 the IAEA announced a ceasefire to demine and repair the damaged Ferosplavna-1 section, which feeds spent-fuel-pool cooling. — [Ukrainska Pravda, 7 Nov 2025](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/11/07/8006360). Update 327 (8 Nov 2025) says the area was demined the day before, repair began that Saturday morning on a section "about three kilometres from the site perimeter", and the line was reconnected at 19:43 local time. This was the plant's first backup line in about six months. — [Update 327 mirror](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2025/11/ukraine-251108-iaea01.htm) (snippet only); [Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1149222.html) (Ukrainian snippet: reconnected 10 November, "for the first time in six months". The date differs from the IAEA's 8 November, perhaps because it reports a later reconnection after the Dniprovska line was cut again on 14 November).
- **Later ceasefire repairs in 2026.** Another ceasefire repair took place in January 2026. — [Ukrainska Pravda, 18 Jan 2026](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/01/18/8016739) (fetch blocked); [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/amp/post/68288). Ferosplavna-1 was cut on 10 February 2026, a ceasefire followed on 27 February, and the line was reconnected by 5 March 2026. — [UP, 27 Feb 2026](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/02/27/8023119); [UP, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/03/05/8024118). Grossi in February 2026: "Demining activities are ongoing to ensure safe access for the repair teams." — via search summary of [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/amp/post/70915)
- **June 2026: "high pylons across the Dnipro".** The repairs involved "high pylons across the Dnipro River" (the reservoir crossing). The IAEA team at the plant monitored the work on the plant's side of the front line, and a separate IAEA team monitored it on the other side. Demining came first. Six temporary ceasefires had been negotiated since late 2025. Dniprovska was repaired but could not be reconnected because a substation about 100 km north-west of the plant was damaged in May 2026. — [World Nuclear News, 26 Jun 2026](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/substation-damage-stopping-zaporizhzhia-power-line-reconnection-says-iaea). Reuters adds that the work was at two sites: the Dniprovska line and the thermal plant's switchyard. — [Reuters via Internazionale](https://www.internazionale.it/ultime-notizie-reuters/2026/06/26/iaea-says-repairs-completed-on-key-power-line-at-zaporizhzhya-nuclear-plant)
- **The seventh ceasefire, September 2026.** Ferosplavna-1 was disconnected on 20 August 2026 "after military activity on the northern side of the Dnipro River". The seventh local ceasefire began on the weekend of 5–6 September 2026. Diesel stocks were down to "little more than a week of supplies", and cooling pumps were run only intermittently. These were the 26th loss of off-site power overall and the 10th since June. — [WNN, 7 Sep 2026](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/local-ceasefire-in-place-for-zaporizhzhia-power-line-repairs); off-site power restored — [ANS, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.ans.org/news/2026-09-09/article-8377/zaporizhzhia-restores-offsite-power-following-iaeabrokered-local-ceasefire/)
- **Count discrepancy.** WNN gives 19 complete losses of off-site power in June 2026 and 26 in September 2026. — [WNN Jun 2026](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/substation-damage-stopping-zaporizhzhia-power-line-reconnection-says-iaea); [WNN Sep 2026](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/local-ceasefire-in-place-for-zaporizhzhia-power-line-repairs). This is consistent with "10 since June".
- **Repairs before the ceasefires (2022–2025): crews waiting for permission under fire.**
  - Ukrenergo said crews waited more than a day for permission to start, because of constant shelling, before restoring the 330 kV line on 2 October 2024. — [Ukrainska Pravda, 2 Oct 2024](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/10/02/7477879/); [Espreso (UA)](https://espreso.tv/viyna-z-rosiyeyu-energetiki-ukrenergo-vidnovili-liniyu-zhivlennya-zaes)
  - In August 2024 crews had to wait for military permission. — [UP, 23 Aug 2024](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/08/23/7471595/)
  - A 330 kV backup line damaged on 4 April 2024 was repaired. — [UP, 7 Apr 2024](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/04/7/7450094/)
  - In July 2025 the security threat stopped immediate repairs. — [UP, 4 Jul 2025](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/07/04/7520247/)
  - In August 2023 the main 750 kV line was damaged "a few kilometres from the station" and temporarily reconnected on the evening of 10 August. — [Mind.ua, 11 Aug 2023](https://mind.ua/en/news/20261216-energy-workers-reconnect-zaporizhzhia-npp-to-the-main-750-kv-power-line-ministry-of-energy) (the number in the URL is an article ID, not a date)
  - Ukrenergo has said its crews have restored these lines "more than 10 times" (Ukrainian search snippet). — [Ukrinform UA](https://www.ukrinform.ua/rubric-economy/3918910-energetiki-vidnovili-robotu-linii-zivlenna-zaes-minenergo.html)
- **Grid workers killed and wounded nearby.** Zaporizhzhiaoblenergo's director wrote (13 Aug 2024) that two utility employees had been killed and 11 wounded on duty. In October 2022 an attack on an Orikhiv repair brigade killed two and wounded three. A June strike on Ukrenergo's 750 kV substation in Zaporizhzhia region, "linked to the ZNPP", killed one dispatcher and wounded another. — [Interfax-Ukraine column, Stasevskyi](https://interfax.com.ua/news/blog/1006510.html). A Ukrenergo substation dispatcher, Denys Novikov (38), died after a Russian attack on 22 June 2024 "in the southern region". — [UP, 23 Jun 2024](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/06/23/7462139); [UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/the-power-engineer-who-was-killed-by-the-russians-yesterday-in-zaporozhye-has-a-9-year-old-son). The sources disagree on whether he died at the scene or in hospital, and none names the substation.

### Inferences
- **Microcosm candidate A: "The ceasefire for one span".** One repair, for example the Ferosplavna-1 repair of 7–8 November 2025 (demined Friday, repaired Saturday, reconnected 19:43), reproduces the whole war's arithmetic in about 36 hours. It involves a front line, two militaries, an international broker, demining, a repair crew and a clock. For film structure it is a ready-made "held moment" with a known start and end time. It fits the project's existing record of outages and the Ferosplavna-1 crossing. A forensic reconstruction would set the repair window against the line's position (OSM) and the reported zone (1.5 km radius, never mapped publicly).
- The project's own crossing work already treats the ceasefire repairs of June 2026, "high pylons across the Dnipro", as being at the reservoir crossing. That match comes from WNN's wording, not from coordinates.

### Gaps
- I found no exact tower number or coordinates for any repair. Only distances are public ("about 3 km from the site perimeter", "about 1.8 km from the ZTPP switchyard", "a few kilometres from the station").
- I found no video or photograph of the crews at work in the ceasefire windows. Searches of Ukrenergo, DTEK and Energoatom material in English and Ukrainian returned only text news. Ukrenergo's and Energoatom's Telegram and Facebook channels should be checked directly, as should the IAEA's X and Flickr accounts. One summary says a January 2026 IAEA post carried a photo credited to the IAEA's X account; this is unverified.
- Who physically repaired the line on the occupied side (ZNPP / Rosatom staff?) is not stated in any source I could open.

## 2. The Dniprovska 750 kV and Ferosplavna 330 kV substations

### Takeaway
The Dniprovska 750 kV substation (Dnipropetrovsk oblast) is the far end of the ZNPP's main line. It has been struck repeatedly: in October 2022 a strike there blacked out the ZNPP, and in May 2026 damage left the repaired Dniprovska line unusable. That makes it a "heart at the other end of the artery" microcosm. Its exact location is a current target, and I could not find footage of the damage that verifies it.

### Cited Findings
- **October 2022.** Shelling of the Dniprovska substation tripped the 750 kV Dniprovska line and "completely de-energized" the ZNPP. — [Babel, 12 Oct 2022](https://babel.ua/en/news/85525-russia-fired-at-a-substation-in-dnipropetrovsk-region-the-zaporizhzhia-npp-is-completely-de-energized); [Gazeta.ua](https://gazeta.ua/articles/np/_zaporizka-aes-znovu-povnistyu-znestrumlena-okupanti-poshkodili-pidstanciyu/1115453)
- **May 2026.** A substation "about 100 km north-west of the plant" was damaged in May 2026, which blocked reconnection of the repaired Dniprovska line. — [WNN, 26 Jun 2026](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/substation-damage-stopping-zaporizhzhia-power-line-reconnection-says-iaea); [UP, 26 Jun 2026](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/06/26/8041243/). A pro-Russian outlet, citing military correspondents, claimed Geran-2 drones hit "Dneprovskaya" substation on 22 May 2026. — [EADaily, 22 May 2026](https://www.eadaily.com/en/news/2026/05/22/drone-strikes-geranium-2-hit-the-substation-dneprovskaya-military-correspondents) (partisan source, unverified). An aggregator reports a fire at Dniprovska that partly cut the South Ukraine NPP's external power. — [threatcluster.io](https://threatcluster.io/cluster/fire-at-dniprovska-substation-disrupts-nuclear-power-supply--c2e954d0) (aggregator, low reliability)
- **The other end in Zaporizhzhia region.** The Stasevskyi column mentions a June strike on Ukrenergo's 750 kV substation in Zaporizhzhia region, linked to the ZNPP, in which a dispatcher was killed. — [Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/blog/1006510.html) (the substation is not named)
- **How substations work under attack.** Ukrenergo's chairman Vitalii Zaichenko said staff leave a substation after strikes and operators run the system from protected places. A cluster-munition missile scatters submunitions over a substation's yard. — [The Page (UA)](https://thepage.ua/ua/news-energy/chomu-deficit-elektroenergiyi-vinikaye-navit-bez-raketno-dronovih-udariv/amp)

### Inferences
- **Microcosm candidate B: "The other end of the line".** The Dniprovska substation is where the ZNPP's last 750 kV artery lands. When it burns, a line repaired under ceasefire is useless. It may also serve the South Ukraine NPP, which would make it a node shared by two nuclear plants. The aggregator's claim about South Ukraine needs confirmation from the IAEA or Energoatom. Geolocation is possible from OSM (the project's network trace already ends lines at named substations) and from Sentinel-2 or commercial imagery of burn scars. This is satellite-forensic material, not splat material.
- **Ethics: high risk.** The substation is under active attack. Publishing precise damage assessments (which transformers and bays were hit) could inform targeting. Show only what is already public at regional scale, and avoid new damage-assessment imagery.

### Gaps
- No verified photographs or video of damage at Dniprovska or Ferosplavna substations. Ferosplavna substation strikes: no specific reporting found.
- The "100 km north-west" substation is not named in WNN/IAEA. That it is Dniprovska is an inference, supported only by the partisan EADaily claim.

## 3. The IAEA team's rotations across the front line

### Takeaway
The IAEA's support team (ISAMZ) is replaced by crossing the front line, sometimes partly on foot. Its convoys have been attacked: a drone hit an armoured IAEA vehicle on 10 December 2024. Rotations have been delayed or cancelled, and the route itself became political when the IAEA rotated through Russian-held territory. It is a microcosm of hostage negotiation: an exchange at a handover point.

### Cited Findings
- **10 December 2024.** A drone hit an official IAEA vehicle in Zaporizhzhia region during a rotation. Grossi called it "unacceptable". The ANS article carries a photo of the damaged vehicle credited to X/@ZelenskyyUa, plus a Grossi video message. — [ANS, 11 Dec 2024](https://ans.org/news/article-6625/iaea-convoy-hit-by-russian-drone). The armoured vehicle's rear was destroyed and the driver and security officer were unharmed. The drone was of a type that explodes on impact and leaves no debris. — [Arms Control Association, Jan 2025](https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2025-01/news-briefs/drone-strikes-iaea-vehicle-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant). Both sides blame each other, and the IAEA did not attribute the attack (same source).
- **March 2023.** A rotation was completed about a month late. Part of the journey across the front line was made on foot. Rosenergoatom's adviser said mines had to be defused on the route. — [World Nuclear Association/WNN, 3 Mar 2023](https://world-nuclear.org/ukraine-information/wnn-ukraine/wnn-delayed-rotation-of-iaea-experts-at-zaporizhzh)
- **2022 entry route.** In 2022 the first mission entered Russian-controlled territory via Vasylivka, near the contact line. — [Interfax, 31 Aug 2022](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/82710/)
- **February 2025.** A rotation was disrupted, and each side blamed the other. Russia's MoD released a video of the alleged attack. — [Militarnyi](https://militarnyi.com/en/news/russian-troops-disrupt-the-rotation-of-the-iaea-mission-to-znpp/); [TASS (Russian state media)](https://tass.com/politics/1912455/amp); [StopFake](https://www.stopfake.org/en/fake-ukraine-attacked-iaea-officials-with-drones-and-artillery). In its 5 March 2025 update the IAEA said the delayed rotation was finally concluded. — [IAEA via globalsecurity](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2025/03/ukraine-250305-iaea01.htm) (snippet only)
- **Rotation through Russian territory.** One rotation went only through Russian territory, and Ukraine's MFA objected. — [Yahoo/Kyiv Independent syndication](https://www.yahoo.com/news/ukraine-calls-iaea-staff-passing-130514846.html); [IAEA defends route](https://www.yahoo.com/news/iaea-defends-russian-controlled-route-173423981.html)
- **Plant staff under fire too.** On 18 August 2026 a drone exploded at about 06:00 at an Enerhodar bus stop used by ZNPP staff. One was killed, with 15–17 injured by differing counts (20 casualties per WNN). The plant blamed Ukraine. — [UP, 19 Aug 2026](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/08/19/8049240/); [Liga](https://news.liga.net/en/war/news/iaea-drone-explodes-near-znpp-one-killed-15-injured); [WNN, 7 Sep 2026](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/local-ceasefire-in-place-for-zaporizhzhia-power-line-repairs)

### Inferences
- **Microcosm candidate C: "The handover point".** The rotation is the plant's other link: a human one, crossing the same front the wires cross. The December 2024 drone strike has a photo of the vehicle, so it is reconstructable in Forensic Architecture style from the vehicle damage and any geolocatable background. The handover point's location is deliberately unpublished.
- **Ethics: very high risk.** Geolocating the rotation route or handover point could endanger future rotations. Do not publish it. Treat attribution as contested: the IAEA did not attribute the attack.

### Gaps
- Exact place of the December 2024 strike: not published in the sources found. Whether the Zelensky-posted photo can be geolocated: not checked.
- ICRC involvement: not found.

## 4. Diesel generators and fuel

### Takeaway
Diesel is the plant's last link and is numbered in days. The best-documented moment is September 2026, when "little more than a week of supplies" remained and cooling pumps ran intermittently. I found no footage of the generators or of fuel deliveries.

### Cited Findings
- **September 2026.** Diesel was down to "little more than a week of supplies", and cooling pumps ran intermittently. — [WNN, 7 Sep 2026](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/local-ceasefire-in-place-for-zaporizhzhia-power-line-repairs)
- **IAEA warning.** If the diesels fail, the result is a complete blackout and possible fuel melting with a radiation release. The plant had run on diesel since 23 September 2025. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/18/zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-repairs-begin-in-ukraine-as-ceasefire-zones-set)
- **July 2025.** The plant ran on diesel for several hours after a blackout. — [UP, 4 Jul 2025](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/07/04/7520247/)

### Inferences
- Diesel is better used as a sound or time device in the film (a countdown of days) than as its own microcosm. The project's Hum already falls silent during complete losses.

### Gaps
- No footage of the generators or of fuel convoys. Fuel delivery routes are not public. If found, they would be sensitive.

## 5. The crossing towers in the drained reservoir

### Takeaway
The two lines that remain today cross the former reservoir on very tall towers set on caissons. The 330 kV crossing (1977, from the thermal plant) has seven towers: five of 100 m and two of 90 m. The 750 kV crossing (1984, from the nuclear plant) has five: three of 126 m and two of 100 m. Since the reservoir drained, these towers stand on dry or marshy bed. WNN says the June 2026 ceasefire repairs involved "high pylons across the Dnipro". I found no public on-the-ground photo or drone video of the towers in the drained bed.

### Cited Findings
- **The two crossings.** Built in 1977 and 1984 from Enerhodar to near Marhanets. The 1977 line from the ZTPP has five 100 m and two 90 m towers on caissons in the water, prefabricated and then set up in the reservoir. The 1984 750 kV single-circuit line from the ZNPP has three 126 m and two 100 m towers. Wikipedia gives a single crossing coordinate, 47.5648278 N, 34.6453861 E (no per-tower coordinates). It links SkyscraperPage entries for Pylons #22 and #23 of the 750 kV crossing, a Novoklimov blog post and an archived Energobud page. — [Wikipedia: Enerhodar Dnipro Powerline Crossing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enerhodar_Dnipro_Powerline_Crossing); [SkyscraperPage](https://skyscraperpage.com/b63206)
- **June 2026 repairs.** These involved "high pylons across the Dnipro River", monitored by IAEA teams on both banks. — [WNN, 26 Jun 2026](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/substation-damage-stopping-zaporizhzhia-power-line-reconnection-says-iaea)
- **The bed after draining.** The reservoir shrank from 2,061 km² to 509.2 km² by 20 June 2023. — [Yahoo/Kyiv Independent syndication](https://news.yahoo.com/russian-attack-reduces-kakhovka-reservoir-224300054.html). Ukraine's Defense Forces say the bed near Nikopol is hard to cross (swamp, reeds, the river channel). — [mezha.net](https://mezha.net/eng/bukvy/21025539_defense_forces_deny/). Photo essays of the bed exist: Meduza, 2024 ([Meduza "The shallows"](https://plugin-frontend.meduza.io/en/feature/2024/06/06/the-shallows)), Kyiv Post, a year on ([Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/amp/post/33861)) and RFE/RL ([RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/ukraine-receding-dnieper-archaeology-cossack-atlantis/32521289.html)). None of their captions that I could see mention the pylons. An AP file photo (Kateryna Klochko, 9 June 2023) shows the ZNPP behind the shallow reservoir. — credited in [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/18/zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-repairs-begin-in-ukraine-as-ceasefire-zones-set)
- **Project asset.** The project already has a georeferenced Esri World Imagery screenshot (WorldView-2, 23 Aug 2024, Vantor) showing the 330 kV and 750 kV crossing pylons at km 5.2 of the great-meadow corridor, matched to Esri at correlation 0.95. — project file `/Users/patrickrodriguez/dev/sandbox/frame-geo-evidence-pipeline/CLAUDE.md` (Satellite section)

### Inferences
- **Microcosm candidate D: "One tower, 126 m, standing in a former sea".** A single caisson tower of the 750 kV crossing, built to stand in water, now stands on drained bed under a front line. It is a monument to the dam, the plant and the war at once. It is the right scale for a Gaussian-splat-like study, but no orbiting footage was found. The forensically safe route is satellite and OSM (already in the pipeline) plus the archived construction documents from Energobud and Novoklimov for the caisson design.
- **Ethics.** These towers are an active military frontier (the bed is a no-man's-land between Nikopol/Marhanets and Enerhodar). Visiting them on foot is dangerous and was not found to have happened publicly. Tower positions are already in OSM and Wikipedia, so showing them reveals nothing new. Showing which towers were damaged and repaired could aid targeting.

### Gaps
- No public drone video, TikTok or Instagram of the crossing towers in the drained bed was found (searched in Ukrainian and English). Telegram channels of the Nikopol district administration and local Marhanets channels are the likeliest unindexed sources. I found no reports of birds nesting on these towers.
- I did not open the per-tower SkyscraperPage pages or the archived Energobud page.

## 6. Water links: canals, pipelines, the cooling pond's wells

### Takeaway
The dam's destruction (6 June 2023) cut two water links at once. The ZNPP lost its reservoir intake and dug about 10–11 groundwater wells to feed its sprinkler ponds. Kryvyi Rih, Nikopol, Marhanets and Pokrov lost their drinking water, and Ukraine laid new pipelines; by August 2024 all main lines were in operation. The wells are a striking small-scale mirror of the plant's dependence. They are on occupied territory and known only through IAEA text.

### Cited Findings
- **ZNPP's reservoir intake lost.** "The Zaporizhzhia NPP can no longer take water from the Kakhovka reservoir" (related headline). Water utilities in Pokrov and Marhanets stopped, and water was delivered by rail and road. The planned pipelines were Karachunivske–Kryvyi Rih–Pivdenne, Marhanets–Nikopol and Khortytsia–Tomakivka. — [Radio Svoboda, 9 Jun 2023](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/news-dnipropetrovska-oblast-mista-bez-vody/32450984.html)
- **ZNPP wells.**
  - August 2023: the first new well near the sprinkler ponds, at about 20 m³/h. The plan was for 10–12 wells around the ponds' perimeter. — [WNN](https://world-nuclear-news.org/Articles/Groundwater-extraction-begins-at-Zaporizhzhia); [CEEnergyNews](https://ceenergynews.com/nuclear/zaporizhzhia-pumps-water-from-new-groundwater-well-iaea-says/)
  - Later, ten wells gave over 200 m³/h, with an eleventh planned for about 250 m³/h. — [NEI Magazine](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/more-wells-drilled-at-zaporizhia-npp-11160730/)
  - The well counts and dates in the secondary sources conflict, so check the IAEA's own updates (e.g. [iaea.org node 126250](https://iaea.org/node/126250)).
- **Pipelines.**
  - Construction began in July 2023. — [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2023/07/04/ukraine-begins-construction-of-150-km-pipeline-to-restore-water-supply-to-two-regions-following-russias-destruction-of-the-kakhovka-dam/)

### Inferences
- **Microcosm candidate E: "Wells at the cooling pond".** The plant once drank from a sea and now drinks from about 11 boreholes. It mirrors the power story: a link cut and a fragile substitute improvised. The site is visible in satellite imagery (the sprinkler ponds and the cooling pond), and the project's corridor passes are close. Whether the wells themselves show at 10 m is doubtful; they would need very-high-resolution imagery.
- **Microcosm candidate F: "The Marhanets–Nikopol pipe".** The same bank as the lines' landing point (Nikopol/Marhanets). A town that lost its water to the drained reservoir got a 40.9 km pipe under shelling, a lifeline that parallels the power line. Nikopol is shelled daily from Enerhodar ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russia-drone-strikes-one-nikopol-kill-1-injure-1)). The pipeline route is likely documented in construction photos and video from the regional administration, but I did not find them indexed.

## 7. Other "single lifeline" cases near the front

### Takeaway
Utility crews in front-line Zaporizhzhia region restore lines within hours between shelling, and sometimes the same site is hit again soon after repair. Thousands of consumers near Huliaipole, Orikhiv and Vasylivka have been without power since 2022.
