## 1. Bioacoustics and ecology of war: what war noise does to birds and wildlife, and what the drained bed sounds like

### Takeaway
The only rigorous peer-reviewed work on Ukrainian wildlife and war-related disturbance is the 2024 *Current Biology* greater spotted eagle tracking study. Its authors suspect noise and light, but they did not measure sound. The rest is OSINT ecology, interview-based "sound and listening" research, and ecologists' field reports. No acoustic monitoring (AudioMoth or similar) of the Kakhovka bed or the Great Meadow was found. The bed's soundscape is documented only by ecologists' descriptions and TV crews' recordings. Those describe continuous song from hundreds of nightingales (two species) and reed warblers, with the hum of front-line drones in the same recordings.

### Cited Findings
**Peer-reviewed / formal**
- [PR] (snippet) Russell, Franco, Atkinson, Väli & Ashton-Butt, "Active European warzone impacts raptor migration", *Current Biology* 2024. GPS tracks of 19 greater spotted eagles crossing Ukraine in March–April 2022 were compared with tracks from 2018–2021. Deviation was greatest north of 48°N, where military activity was heavier, and one eagle diverted about 250 km. Females took about 246 h to reach breeding grounds against about 193 h before the war. Stopovers in Ukraine fell from about 90% of birds to 32% — [UEA eprint PDF](https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/95248/1/1_s2.0_S0960982224005190_main.pdf); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/05/22/7456977); [Smithsonian](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/imperiled-eagles-are-altering-their-migration-routes-to-avoid-the-war-in-ukraine-180984413/)
- [PR] (snippet) The authors had no direct observation of what the birds reacted to, but "thought noise and light from military activities could have affected their behavior". Some press syndications say 21 eagles where the paper says 19; use the paper's figure — [Wild Polesia summary](https://wildpolesia.org/blog/2024/06/10/birds-eye-view-of-the-warzone-the-armed-conflict-in-ukraine-impacts-seasonal-migration/); [Earth.com](https://www.earth.com/news/migrating-eagles-have-been-rerouted-by-the-war-in-ukraine)

### Inferences
- The only quantified evidence on wildlife behaviour sits far from this site (Polesia eagles, Odesa flamingos). For the Kakhovka bed the strongest claims are ecologists' descriptions, which are qualitative. On screen, "hundreds of nightingales" is the observers' description, not a count.
- That nightingales and drones are heard together is documented by TSN's own recording description. It is the film's strongest sonic juxtaposition, and it is sourced.
- Interviewees report habituation in large waterbirds (cormorants, herons, storks) and nest abandonment in a sensitive colonial species (flamingos). These are anecdotes, and the film should say so if it uses them.
- No one appears to be recording the bed systematically. It is a real gap, and an opportunity a project like this could name, without implying it has filled it.

### Gaps
- No source on frogs, toads or amphibian choruses on the drained bed or in the Great Meadow. Searches in Ukrainian and English found nothing.
- No acoustic monitoring (AudioMoth, Song Meter, BirdNET surveys) on the Kakhovka bed, in the Great Meadow, or near the ZNPP and its cooling pond.
- The Suspilne film's chapter "Природа під звуки війни" is known only from its chapter list. Its sound content, and whether any of it is a pure location recording, is unverified.
- The *Current Biology* eagle paper and the *European Zoological Journal* paper were not read in full.
- No measured sound levels (dB) for anywhere near the site were found.

## 2. Drones as sound: signatures, the acoustic detection networks, and Nikopol residents listening

### Takeaway
Officials describe the Shahed's piston engine as sounding like a moped or chainsaw. The Shahed-101 electric variant is reported to be near-silent. FPV drones are described as a distinct high buzz with seconds of warning. Ukraine's acoustic networks are Sky Fortress (about 9,500–10,000+ phone-and-microphone poles) and Zvook (machine-learning stations trained partly on civilian phone videos). Both are documented in the press, but nothing public says where they are deployed, and this brief does not look. The "Chuika" that Nikopol-area residents and medics carry is not an acoustic device: it is a radio detector of drones' analogue video signal. The Truth Hounds *Killzone* report is the key local source. It documents an average of 459 drone attacks a month on the Nikopol raion (September 2024 – March 2025), and it explicitly treats the sound of a drone's engine as evidence.

**Signatures**

**Truth Hounds, *Killzone: How Russian Drones Are Devastating the River Dnipro's Right Bank* (November 2025)** — [NGO, relevant passages read in full from the PDF](https://dev.truth-hounds.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/killzone-how-russian-drones-are-devastating-the-river-dnipros-right-bank.pdf); [case page](https://dev.truth-hounds.org/en/cases/killzone-how-russian-drones-are-devastating-the-river-dnipros-right-bank)
- Nikopol raion: "the average monthly number of attacks by kamikaze and munitions-drop drones recorded between September 2024 and March 2025 was 459 per month". The highest month was October 2024 (626), the lowest January 2025 (397). About 40% fell on Nikopol city, 188 a month. Victims averaged nine a month, and six civilians were killed in the period. The count comes from the local Telegram channel «Що? Де? Куди?» (58,000+ followers on 15 November 2025).
- KI's chart from the same report: Nikopol drone strikes were zero in June 2023, 290 in June 2024 and 362 in June 2025 — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-drones-target-civilians-dnipro-right-bank/)
- Engine sound as evidence of intent. Controlled kamikaze drones gain speed before impact, "accompanied by a characteristic increase in the intensity of engine sound… such episodes of 'escalating' sound may happen in a series". For fixed-wing drones the sound "can significantly intensify… or… reduce or disappear altogether" before a strike. After loss of control, "the sound of the engine suddenly disappears and is then followed by the sound of an explosion", and "such a strike would not be intentional".
- Method for interviewers: "The audio aspect of the attack is also crucial… Even without information about what the drone looked like, the description of its sound during the attack may indicate the intention to attack. Ask witnesses to recreate the sequence of changes in sound." Different models "create a characteristic sound".
- On hearing drones: "Even a slight noise can make it difficult to hear a drone". Telegram lets locals set "an individual sound signal for a particular channel on the phone". Kherson residents post to a chat when they "notice or hear a drone".
- Witness testimony is from Kherson, not Nikopol: "the drone quickly descended, making a very loud buzzing sound, and hit the gas tank of my car". Another witness: "I heard the drone descend very low, after which I heard a 'switching' sound, similar to the sound of a machine gun shutter".
- Detectors: civilians use Tsukorok, Kseon-L and Vartovyi radio detectors (about USD 100) and interceptor-detectors such as Spuk, Obrii and Chuika, which intercept the drone's video. A 2024 Nikopol case: a resident's TikTok, reposted in the Telegram channel "Nikopol / Marhanets / Enerhodar / Shelling" (15,000 followers), is believed to have led to a drone strike on the flat, injuring an 81-year-old resident. This bears directly on the film's privacy and safety practice.

### Inferences
- The Chuika "listens" to radio, not sound. If the film shows it as an ear, it must say so. The acoustic devices are the state-level networks.
- Zvook was trained to reject "insects, voices… and animals", so in machine listening the bed's birdsong is literally the noise to be filtered out. The film may use this as an observation; it is not a claim about any particular sensor.
- Truth Hounds' sound criteria (escalating engine note = intent; engine cut-out then blast = loss of control) are a sourced, ready-made frame for "sound as evidence" at this site. Its examples come from Kherson; for Nikopol it gives only counts.
- The flight times give the acoustic geometry of the crossing: about 15 s for a Grad from Enerhodar (KI 2022), 20–60 s for artillery from the left bank (Truth Hounds), and "only 8 km by water" (Voxeurop). At about 343 m/s, 8–10 km of open water is roughly 23–29 s of sound delay (my calculation). Across the dry bed now, the path is unchanged but the surface is forest instead of water.

### Gaps
- Whether Sky Fortress or Zvook sensors cover the Nikopol area: deliberately not researched (safety). The film should not imply anything either way.
- No Nikopol-specific testimony on how residents tell outgoing from incoming fire, or Shahed from FPV, by ear was found beyond the KI 2022 piece. CNN July 2026 could not be fetched.
- The figures in the brief ("45 km of nets", "lights-off regime since Dec 2023") were not re-verified in this pass.

## 3. Air-raid sirens and alerts in Nikopol, Marhanets and Enerhodar

### Takeaway

## 4. Sound as evidence at this site: the plant battle, launches from the plant, the dam breach, IAEA earwitness logs

### Takeaway
The best-documented "sound as evidence" here is geophysical, not audible. Ukraine's National Data Centre recorded the Kakhovka dam explosion on seismic and **infrasound** arrays at 23:34:50 UTC on 5 June 2023 (02:34:50 Kyiv time), with a yield of about 914 ± 170 kg TNT. The authors explicitly reject the water's release as the source of the signals. NORSAR independently reported a weaker signal at about 02:35 and a clear one at 02:54. The IAEA's on-site teams serve as continual earwitnesses: their updates log explosions, shelling and gunfire heard from the plant. Whether the 4 March 2022 CCTV stream had audio, and whether any recording of launches from the plant has usable sound, was not established.

### Cited Findings
- [SCI-CONF, full] Liashchuk, Andrushchenko & Kolesnykov (Main Centre of Special Monitoring, State Space Agency of Ukraine; CTBTO PrepCom), "Detection of an explosion at the Kakhovka HPP dam by the Ukrainian geophysical network", poster P2.2-351, CTBTO Science and Technology Conference, 11 September 2025:
  - The event was at 23:34:50 UTC on 5 June 2023, recorded by seismic arrays PS45 (Ukraine) and BURARI (Romania) and stations SORM (Moldova) and LUBAR. It overlapped a Tonga M4.3 teleseism and was separated by filtering.
  - Infrasound arrays KPDA, MAAG and GRDI fixed the epicentre by azimuth. The seismic and infrasound locations fall within 19 km of each other.
  - Seismic energy class K = 5.74 (M ≈ 1), giving 767 kg TNT. Infrasound periods of 0.55–0.63 s gave 714–1,125 kg, a mean of 914 ± 170 kg TNT.
  - "We can reject the idea that the seismic and infrasound signals were caused by a sudden release of water… When water is released from a dam, it creates low signal/noise ratio infrasound signals that last from several hours to several days. In the case of explosions… high signal-to-noise ratio and short-duration infrasonic signals."
  - The abstract page misprints the date as "May 5, 2023"; the poster says 5 June. A search summary had wrongly reported that the authors *attribute* infrasound to the water release; the poster says the opposite.
  — [poster PDF](https://conferences.ctbto.org/event/30/contributions/5667/attachments/2811/6737/P2_2-351_Liashchuk.pdf); [abstract page](https://conferences.ctbto.org/event/30/contributions/5667/)
- [SCI/PRESS] (snippet) NORSAR: "clear signals on Tuesday, June 6, at 2:54 local time", at 46.7776, 33.37; "The signals indicate that there was an explosion. ## 5. Forensic listening precedents (Abu Hamdan, Forensic Architecture, Bellingcat)

### Takeaway
The main precedents are Lawrence Abu Hamdan's *Air Pressure* (atmospheric noise of overflights logged from UN filings and citizen phone videos), the *Earwitness Inventory* (objects as sonic analogies for remembered sounds), the Saydnaya reconstruction (echo-profiling of survivors' memory) and Forensic Architecture's audio ballistics. None has been applied to this site. Bellingcat's Ukraine audio work found here is limited to debunking a clip with lifted explosion sounds.

### Cited Findings
- [Art/research, full] *Air Pressure (A Diary of the Sky)*: an audio-video installation (Future Fields Commission, Fondazione Sandretto Re Rebaudengo and Philadelphia Museum of Art). The script covers May 2020 to May 2021, and the flight data cover 15 years since UN Resolution 1701 (2006): "22,111 flights by fighter jets, helicopters, drones and other unmanned aircrafts". Sources are UN violation filings plus "a campaign of citizen journalism activated by the artist via social media". The artist calls the result "atmospheric violence" and the sustained threat "a long bomb". Companion site: airpressure.info — [ArtDependence](https://artdependence.com/articles/lawrence-abu-hamdans-air-pressure-a-diary-of-the-sky-curated-by-irene-calderoni-and-amanda-sroka)
- [Art] (snippet) Phone videos were gathered via the Arabic hashtag #warintheatmosphere. One listing gives 2,412 incursions for May 2020 – May 2021, a different span from the 22,111 — [La Virreina](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/lavirreina/en/activities/air-pressure/761); [Mousonturm](https://www.mousonturm.de/en/programm/veranstaltungen/4936/air-pressure-a-diary-of-the-sky); [Glasgow International](https://glasgowinternational.org/programme/projects/live-audio-essays-air-pressure)
- [Art] (snippet) *Earwitness Inventory* (2018–): a self-built library of everyday objects (car door, popcorn maker, coins, bread) used as sonic analogies in testimony, made with Saydnaya survivors among others — [FSRR](https://fsrr.org/en/?p=36906)
- [NGO/FA] (snippet) Saydnaya (Amnesty and Forensic Architecture, 2016): prisoners were kept in darkness and forbidden to make any sound, even to whisper, which made them acutely attuned to sound. Abu Hamdan used echo-profiling and reverberation modelling (dripping pipes, sounds heard through vents) to estimate the size of cells and corridors. Assad supporters attacked the methods as "special effects" — [ICA](https://www.ica.art/exhibitions/forensic-architecture-counter-investigations/recent-investigations/torture-and-killing-in-saydnaya-prison); [Amnesty](https://saydnaya.amnesty.org/en/about.html); [MediaCommons](https://mediacommons.org/imr/node/16228)
- [FA] (snippet) Audio ballistics: in the Nakba Day killings (Nawara and Abu Daher), sound-frequency analysis showed live rounds disguised as rubber bullets, which forced a reversal of Israel's denial. Forensic Architecture's Shireen Abu Akleh audio report (2 November 2022) separates the sources and sequence of gunfire by frequency and amplitude — [Pratt archive](https://omekas.prattsi.org/s/surveillance-art/item/4485); [FA audio report PDF](https://content.forensic-architecture.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/2022.11.02_Final-Audio-and-Acoustic-Analysis-SAA-Report.pdf); [Abu Hamdan PhD thesis](https://eprints-gro.gold.ac.uk/23293/1/VIS_thesis_AbuHamdenL_2018.pdf)
- [SCI] (snippet) Multilateration from several unsynchronised recordings (time difference of arrival) can locate a shooter. Echoes, refraction and reverberation confound it — [NIJ](https://nij.ojp.gov/library/publications/audio-forensic-gunshot-analysis-and-multilateration)
- [OSINT] (snippet) Bellingcat (23 February 2022) flagged a manipulated clip whose explosion sounds were lifted from a YouTube video of Finnish military exercises — [Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/news/2022/02/23/documenting-and-debunking-dubious-footage-from-ukraines-frontlines)

### Inferences
- *Air Pressure* maps most closely onto Nikopol's sky: a count of drone attacks (Truth Hounds' 459 a month, compiled from a Telegram channel) plays the role of the UN filings, and residents' phone videos play the role of #warintheatmosphere. Any such use must protect the people filming, given the Truth Hounds case of a TikTok that preceded a strike.
- The *Earwitness Inventory* model fits testimony like "a 'switching' sound, similar to… a machine gun shutter" (Truth Hounds). This is analogy, not reproduction, which keeps the project's rule against generated material.
- Bellingcat's debunk is a caution: war audio is easily swapped. Any location sound used should be traced to an original upload, with its hash recorded as the pipeline does for frames.

### Gaps
- No Forensic Architecture, Bellingcat, Centre for Information Resilience or Earshot audio analysis of the ZNPP, Enerhodar, Nikopol or the dam was found.
- The relation of Earshot (the audio-forensics NGO) to Abu Hamdan was not confirmed in sources read.
