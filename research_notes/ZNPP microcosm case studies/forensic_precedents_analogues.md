# Forensic precedents at the ZNPP / Kakhovka, and analogue "microcosm" cases in the Dnipro valley (2022–2026)

Research notes, 29 October 2026. About 40 searches and fetches. Most findings come through search-result summaries and secondary outlets, with fewer read from the primary page. Where only a secondary report was found, that is said. Coordinates are given only where a source gives them. Anything else is marked "take from OSM".

Correction to the brief: the ZNPP cooling-tower fire was on **11 August 2024**, not 2025 ([WNN](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/iaea-experts-continue-zaporizhzhia-cooling-tower-f); [Meduza, 12 Aug 2024](https://meduza.io/en/news/2024/08/12/moscow-and-kyiv-blame-each-other-after-fire-breaks-out-at-russian-occupied-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant)).

## Q1. Which specific ZNPP / Enerhodar videos and images have been geolocated and analysed, by whom, with what outputs?

### Takeaway
ZNPP footage has been geolocated piecemeal by news outlets (NPR, NYT, CNN), by CIR's Eyes on Russia and the Conflict Intelligence Team (CIT, working with The Insider), and through satellite reviews (Texty/Zaporizhzhia Center for Investigations; Greenpeace Ukraine with McKenzie Intelligence Services; RFE/RL Skhemy with Planet imagery). No 3D model or spatial reconstruction of the plant by an investigative group turned up. The closest thing to a forensic synthesis is Texty's "Nuclear Terror" scrollytelling map (January 2023). Greenpeace/MIS have analysed the student's own line: a damaged 750 kV Dniprovska tower near the plant, September 2025.

### Cited Findings

**4 March 2022: the night battle and seizure (CCTV livestream)**
- NPR analysed the 4+ hour security-camera recording. It found that Russian forces repeatedly fired heavy weapons toward the plant's reactor buildings, and that ordnance hit a high-voltage line outside the plant (the IAEA said two lines were damaged). It placed the camera at the main administrative building by matching buildings seen through the window. The feed had been livestreamed on the plant's YouTube channel from shortly before 23:30 local time. NPR, 11 March 2022 — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/11/1085427380/ukraine-nuclear-power-plant-zaporizhzhia)
- The NYT obtained a video from inside the plant, from a source with ties to local government. It records a warning broadcast over loudspeakers to Russian troops to stop firing. The NYT verified it against archival video and photos of the plant. Known here only from a secondary republication — [Salon/AlterNet, 5 Mar 2022](https://salon.com/2022/03/05/a-glimpse-inside-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant_partner)
- Overt Defense matched the livestreamed fire to satellite imagery and identified the burning building as a training centre/museum outside the reactor area — [Overt Defense, 3 Mar 2022](https://www.overtdefense.com/2022/03/03/zaporizhzhia-pitched-battle-at-europes-largest-nuclear-power-plant)
- The Globe and Mail reported the camera footage as appearing to show flares and weapons fire in front of the administration buildings — [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/video-camera-at-burning-ukrainian-nuclear-power-plant-appears-to-show)

**August–September 2022: militarisation of the site**
- CNN geolocated and authenticated a video (circulating 18 Aug 2022) of at least five Russian military trucks, one marked "Z", with tents and pallets, at the far western edge of the ground floor of the **Unit 1 turbine hall**, about 130 m from the reactor. The filming date is unknown. CNN, 19 Aug 2022 — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2022/08/19/europe/ukraine-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-russian-vehicles-intl-hnk/index.html)
- The IAEA's September 2022 mission confirmed military trucks on the ground floor of the Unit 1 and Unit 2 turbine halls (quoted in the same CNN coverage) — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2022/08/19/europe/ukraine-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-russian-vehicles-intl-hnk/index.html)
- CIR / Eyes on Russia, "Danger Zone" (7 Sep 2022), with coordinates for each item:
  - drone footage filmed by The Insider (2 Aug 2022) of a convoy with anti-aircraft guns and APCs entering near reactor 2 (coordinates in CIR's report);
  - Maxar imagery (29 Aug 2022) of equipment under the cooling pipes at reactor 5 (coordinates in CIR's report);
  - fighting positions at the plant entrance (from an ARD broadcast) (coordinates in CIR's report);
  - MLRS fire on the night of 2–3 Sep, filmed from the Dnipropetrovsk bank facing the plant, with the launch area placed between the NPP and the thermal plant (refuting Rybar's claim);
  - shelling of central Enerhodar near the Shevchenko monument (coordinates in CIR's report). CIR used crater and debris-spray analysis to trace the shell's direction back to the Russian-held south.
  The outputs are annotated figures, not an interactive model. — [CIR](https://info-res.org/post/danger-zone-how-russia-is-risking-nuclear-disaster-at-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant)
- The Insider published the MLRS video. CIT matched the thermal plant's chimneys in frame to a Google panorama, placing the camera on the opposite (Nikopol) bank and the launchers west of the TPP. It said it is impossible to say exactly whether the rockets were launched from the plant site itself — [Espreso](https://global.espreso.tv/russian-military-fire-mlrs-from-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant-the-insider-publishes-video); [Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/09/04/7366003/); [Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/83829-the-insider-publication-showed-how-russian-anti-aircraft-guns-fire-from-the-zaporizhzhia-npp-the-fire-is-adjusted-by-drones)
- StopFake: a Russian video presented as a strike on the NPP actually showed the Zaporizhzhia thermal power plant, about 7 km away. This is a misattribution case useful as a caution — [StopFake](https://www.stopfake.org/?p=136750)
- RFE/RL Skhemy / Maxar: satellite images of 29 Aug 2022 show shelling damage to a roof at the plant — [Espreso](https://global.espreso.tv/major-media-publish-satellite-images-of-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plants-roof-damaged-by-russian-shelling)

**Texty.org.ua + Zaporizhzhia Center for Investigations, "Ядерний терор" (26 January 2023)**
- A scrolling chronology from 4 March to December 2022: the night battle, blackouts, attacks on power lines, IAEA mission, director's kidnapping. It has three maps: the capture; the fire range of Russian weapons from the plant; and the grid around the ZNPP.
- Imagery used: Planet (June 2022, and 29 Oct 2022, showing fortification growth); Maxar 23 Sep 2022 (vehicles under the overpasses between units and special buildings); Maxar 28 Aug 2022 (impact marks on special building 1).
- It states that from 8 August 2022 only the Dniprovska 750 kV line across the reservoir survived, and that from mid-March 2022 Nikopol was shelled from near the plant.
- It relies mostly on attributed sources (Energoatom, IAEA, The Insider, CIT, ISW) rather than its own verification.
- It won a special nomination at "Честь професії".
- [Texty project](https://texty.org.ua/projects/108780/yadernij-teror-yak-rosiya-vzyala-v-zaruchnyky-aes-i-enerhodarciv/); [award note](https://texty.org.ua/fragments/109815/nash-data-proyekt-yadernij-teror-sered-peremozhciv-na-konkursi-chest-profesiyi/)

- Skhemy (RFE/RL) used Planet imagery of 9 June 2023 to show the tributary feeding the cooling pond shrinking after the dam's destruction. Energoatom said the pond level was stable — [Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/95015-skhemy-project-showed-satellite-images-of-the-tributary-to-the-cooling-pond-of-the-znpp)

Greenpeace interprets the month-long outage as deliberate. That is its inference, not something the imagery shows — [Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/news/4460/the-750-kv-power-line-at-zaporizhzhya-nuclear-power-plant-shows-no-signs-of-major-damage-new-satellite-investigation-by-greenpeace/)

  - a 2026 review of a line along the M18 Mariupol–Henichesk, disrupted by strikes.
  [Greenpeace 2026](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/novyny/6346/high-resolution-satellite-imagery-reveals-russias-latest-doomed-attempt-to-connect-the-occupied-zaporizhzhia-npp-to-the-temporary-occupied-and-russian-grid/); [Kyiv Independent on NYT](https://kyivindependent.com/russia-builds-power-lines-to-connect-occupied-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-to-its-grid-nyt-reports/); [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/05/27/world/satellite-images-suggest-russian-plan-restart-seized-ukrainian-nuclear-plant). ### Inferences
- The best-documented footage (4 March CCTV, the Unit 1 turbine hall, the MLRS seen from the Nikopol bank) is a natural "evidence spine" for a film. Each has a known camera position (admin building; turbine hall west end; the Nikopol shore) that the student could rebuild in the 3D scene.
- The MLRS video and the cooling-tower fire videos were filmed **from the Nikopol bank across the reservoir**, the same bank the Dniprovska and Ferosplavna-1 lines land on. That makes the far shore a recurring viewpoint and a strong link to the Great Meadow site.
- The 11 August 2024 fire, the 7 April 2024 drone hits and the September 2025 Dniprovska tower are **open questions**. A careful student investigation could add something there, rather than duplicate settled work.
- The Greenpeace/MIS tower analysis (1.9 km NE of Unit 6) concerns the same line the student draws. It should be credited, and its tower can be checked against the student's OSM towers.

- No Bellingcat, Molfar, GeoConfirmed or Frontelligence Insight product specifically on the ZNPP was found. GeoConfirmed's map likely holds individual pins, but these were not reachable by search.
- CIR's dedicated ZNPP page (info-res.org/eyes-on-russia/zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant) returned 404.
- No independent geolocation of the 11 August 2024 fire footage or the April 2024 drone footage was found.

## Q2. Has Forensic Architecture (or a similar group) worked on the Kakhovka dam or the ZNPP? What do the dam analyses show?

### Takeaway
No Forensic Architecture investigation of the ZNPP or the Kakhovka dam was found. The Kakhovka dam has a dense multi-method record instead: the NYT visual investigation, NORSAR seismic data, GRC's legal team, the Truth Hounds + Project Expedite Justice "SUBMERGED" report (with EOS flood mapping), AP's Oleshky death-toll investigation, and a University of Houston InSAR study. This is ground the student should credit and build on, not redo.

### Cited Findings
- **Forensic Architecture.** Searches found no FA dam or ZNPP project. One search summary says FA's Ukraine listing holds a project with its Kyiv partner, the Center for Spatial Technologies, on the 1 March 2022 strike on the Kyiv TV Tower, plus a Nebelivka archaeology project. The FA Ukraine page did not render, so this is unconfirmed — [FA location page](https://forensic-architecture.org/location/ukraine)
- **NYT visual investigation (published 16–17 June 2023).** Engineers and explosives experts pointed to a charge detonated in a passageway through the dam's concrete base, reached from the machine hall under Russian control. The passage was documented in engineering drawings and confirmed by engineer Ihor Strelets. The NYT said only an inspection after draining could settle the sequence. A separate NYT report (9 June) cited US satellite infrared detection of an explosion. Known here via secondary reports — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/nyt-evidence-suggests-russia-blew-kakhovka-dam); [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/kakhovka-dam-new-york-times-russia-blew-it-up-evidence/32464308.html); [Novaya Gazeta Europe](https://novayagazeta.eu/amp/en/articles/2023/06/17/nyt-explosion-in-technical-tunnel-controlled-by-russian-military-can-be-behind-kakhovka-dam-collapse-en-news); [Illinois MechSE (Glumac's role)](https://mechse.illinois.edu/news/56582)
- **NORSAR seismic.** A signal at 02:54 local time on 6 June 2023, at 46.7776, 33.37, magnitude 1–2, picked up mainly by the BURAR array in Romania about 620 km away; weaker activity at 02:35. It locates the source only to within about 20–30 km and does not attribute — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/06/08/1181078981/seismic-stations-detected-explosion-at-ukrainian-dam); [Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/news/2023/06/09/norwegian-seismologists-say-explosion-was-detected-around-kakhovka-dam-around-time-of-collapse)
- **Global Rights Compliance** (Mobile Justice Team, visited Kherson in June 2023): "highly likely" pre-placed explosives, an "80% and above" determination — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/06/16/russian-forces-likely-behind-ukrainian-dam-attack-investigation-says.html); [Ukranews](https://ukranews.com/en/news/939297-investigation-shows-that-kakhovka-hepp-destruction-by-80-occurred-due-to-explosion-of-pre-laid). GRC also promotes a Kyiv Independent documentary on obstructed evacuations and secret burials — [GRC](https://globalrightscompliance.org/russia-ignored-its-obligations-regarding-the-evacuation-of-civilians-after-kakhovka-dam-destruction-and-secretly-buried-the-dead-documentary-by-the-kyiv-independent/)
- **Truth Hounds + Project Expedite Justice, "SUBMERGED"** (6 June 2024). It attributes the destruction to the Russian 205th Separate Motor Rifle Brigade and argues it is an environmental war crime under the Rome Statute. EOS Data Analytics mapped the flood with optical and radar imagery — [PEJ](https://www.projectexpeditejustice.org/submerged); [Greenpeace UA summary](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/investigations-reports/1341/truth-hounds-new-report-claims-that-the-destruction-of-kakhovka-dam-is-an-environmental-war-crime/); [EOS case study](https://eos.com/blog/kakhovka-dam-flooding-detection-in-ukraine-for-pej/)
- **Legal Action Worldwide + Truth Hounds** complaint to the UN Human Rights Committee for families of 9 dead (6 June 2026) — [LAW press release PDF](https://legalactionworldwide.org/wp-content/uploads/Press-Release_Kakhovka-Case-6-June-2026.pdf)
- **AP investigation (Dec 2023).** At least hundreds dead in occupied Oleshky alone, against Russia's official 59. Death certificates were restricted and medics threatened; records are reportedly hidden. Sourced through anonymous testimony — [RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/ap-investigation-kakhovka-dam-death-toll/32750535.html); [AP best-of](https://leads.svcs.associatedpress.com/best-of-the-week/ap-investigation-russias-cover-up-deaths-caused-by-dam-explosion)
- **University of Houston InSAR (April 2024).** The structure had been sinking since June 2021, suggesting existing damage. The authors say this cannot exclude an explosion — [UH](https://www.uh.edu/news-events/stories/2024/april/04032024-kakhovka-dam-collapse-milillo.php)
- **Other flood mapping:** Wageningen (Planet imagery); DFRLab (flood scenarios); Vox Ukraine (Maxar + Sentinel-2: 11,367 objects submerged) — [WUR](https://www.wur.nl/en/newsarticle/mapping-the-2023-ukraine-floods-after-the-kakhovka-dam-break-using-high-resolution-planet-imagery.htm); [Vox Ukraine](https://voxukraine.org/?p=131185)
- **Bellingcat.** No dedicated dam investigation was found. Aric Toler posted and then deleted a speculative tweet about natural causes — [Zona Media summary](https://en.zona.media/article/2023/06/06/kakhovka-trl)
- **Academic work on the drained bed:** an ISPRS 2024 paper on breach scenarios; a Sentinel-2 study of the reservoir bed's transformation. Ukrhydroenergo reports a centimetre-level 3D model of the reservoir bed — [ISPRS](https://isprs-archives.copernicus.org/articles/XLVIII-3-2024/251/2024/); [Pravda (Ukrhydroenergo)](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/06/06/7515988/)

## Q3. Analogue "microcosm" candidates (Dnipro valley, 2022–2026)

### Takeaway
The strongest microcosms are:
1. **Nikopol**, the far bank: shelled from the plant's grounds, cut off from its water when the reservoir drained, and where the lines land.
2. **The ZNPP cooling pond**, the hostage's own water: shrinking, trenched, re-dammed, all visible in Sentinel-2.
3. **DniproHES**, a dam whose crest is a city's road, struck on 22 March 2024 with a trolleybus hit on the crest; no OSINT geolocation of its videos was found.
4. **The Mykolaiv water main**, a city on a single lifeline cut in occupied ground.
5. **Korsunka/Dnipriany and Oleshky**, occupied villages flooded with no evacuation.

Also usable: the Antonivskyi bridge (the lifeline seen from the other side), Krynky (a cut-off village bridgehead) and Kherson's Korabel island (an island district under the drone "safari").

### Cited Findings

**C1. Nikopol (and Marhanets): the bank across from the plant** (Nikopol about 47.57 N, 34.40 E: take exact from OSM)
- **What, when, where.** Nikopol has been shelled from occupied Enerhodar since mid-March 2022. Ukrainian officials say this came from the plant's grounds "because they know there will be no return fire" (a government claim) — [Ukrinform](https://ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/3604321-russians-shelling-nikopol-district-from-nuclear-power-plant-premises.html). Texty and CIT placed the launch area near the ZNPP — [Texty](https://texty.org.ua/projects/108780/yadernij-teror-yak-rosiya-vzyala-v-zaruchnyky-aes-i-enerhodarciv/). Drones became the main weapon; for example, 12 people were injured in April 2025 — [Rubryka](https://rubryka.com/en/2025/04/10/vijska-rf-atakuvaly-nikopol-z-drona-bagato-postrazhdalyh/).
- After 6 June 2023 the reservoir drained, cutting tap water for about 200,000 people in the Nikopol district, and for Marhanets and Kryvyi Rih. The city set up 41 points for washing water and 17 for drinking water — [CSMonitor](https://csmonitor.com/World/Europe/2023/0816/Mud-and-thirst-Two-Ukraine-cities-cope-with-dam-s-destruction); [WhoWhatWhy](https://whowhatwhy.org/international/caught-between-a-water-emergency-and-a-possible-nuclear-crisis-in-nikopol/); [Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/3727911-water-supply-partially-restored-in-nikopol.html). New mains were built afterwards: Marhanets–Nikopol–Pokrov, Zaporizhzhia–Tomakivka–Marhanets, Inhulets–Pivdenne — [KMU](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/likvidatsiia-naslidkiv-pidryvu-kakhovskoi-hes-zapratsiuvala-persha-nytka-vodohonu-na-dnipropetrovshchyni)
- The UN Commission of Inquiry's October 2025 report covered drone attacks on civilians in Kherson, **Dnipropetrovsk** and Mykolaiv oblasts. It drew on a library of 247 geolocated incidents and found the crime against humanity of forcible transfer — [UN Ukraine](https://ukraine.un.org/en/295172-un-commission-concludes-russian-armed-forces%E2%80%99-drone-attacks-against-civilians-kherson); [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/10/28/world/russia-aims-drone-attacks-civilians-war-crime-un-inquiry-says)
- **How it mirrors the ZNPP.** It is the far end of the lines, and the hostage-taker's target across the water. It is the viewpoint from which the MLRS (Sept 2022) and cooling-tower-fire (Aug 2024) videos were filmed. Its water left with the reservoir: the Great Meadow seen from the town that lost it.
- **Media.** Residents' videos of the plant from the embankment; water-queue reporting; the student's own YouTube collection (Nikopol is already in scope).
- **Splat potential.** Medium. Orbiting phone footage of a water-distribution point or of the embankment looking at the plant may exist.
- **Approach.** Line-of-sight and skyline matching to the TPP chimneys and ZNPP units (as CIT did); shadow chronolocation.
- **Ethics and safety.** Active drone zone. Never reveal filmers' positions or identify civilians; delay publication of fresh footage.
- **Verification status.** The shelling is extensively reported; attribution to the plant site rests on Ukrainian officials plus CIT/CIR geolocation of single videos.

**C2. The ZNPP cooling pond and its intake: the hostage's own water** (pond adjacent to the plant at about 47.51 N, 34.58 E: polygon from OSM)
- **What, when.** The tributary was cut after the dam breach (Skhemy, Planet imagery 9 June 2023) — [Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/95015-skhemy-project-showed-satellite-images-of-the-tributary-to-the-cooling-pond-of-the-znpp). The pond shrank from about 9.6 to about 8.66 million m² (July 2022 to August 2025); a dam was built in the intake channel; there are more than 1,000 m of trenches on the north and west shores (MIS/Greenpeace) — [Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/news/5438/new-level-of-militarization/); [MIS PDF](https://www.greenpeace.org/static/planet4-ukraine-stateless/2026/02/2c4d05a4-Оновлений-Маккензі-eng.pdf)
- **How it mirrors the ZNPP.** The same story at the scale of one pond: the plant's survival depends on a lifeline (water instead of wires) that someone else's war has cut, and the occupier re-engineers it to restart.
- **Media.** Sentinel-2/Landsat (already in the pipeline); Planet via Skhemy; MIS figures.
- **Splat potential.** Low (no access).
- **Approach.** NDWI area time series; shoreline change; trench detection is limited at 10 m (the MIS work used high-resolution imagery).
- **Ethics.** Low risk; remote sensing only.
- **Verification.** The shrinkage can be independently checked by the student. The trench counts are advocacy-commissioned.

**C3. DniproHES (Dnipro HPP), Zaporizhzhia: a dam that is also the city's road** (take coordinates from OSM; the dam crest is between the city's banks above Khortytsia)
- **What, when.** On 22 March 2024 Russia hit the station with 8 missiles (Pravda), including two direct hits, during a mass strike on the grid. The machine hall was wrecked, with a roof over 35 m high to be cleared first; some units have to be made from scratch. There was an oil spill into the Dnipro and at least one person died at the plant — [Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/03/22/7447679); [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2024/03/25/restoration-of-ukraines-dnipro-hydroelectric-power-plant-will-take-years-following-russian-missile-attack/); [OSW](https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2024-03-26/russian-forces-destroy-dnipro-hydroelectric-power-station-day-761). A missile hit a **trolleybus crossing the dam**, and the driver died. Accounts differ on whether there were passengers — [Ukrinform](https://ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/3843190-rocket-hits-trolleybus-with-people-moving-along-dnipro-hydroelectric-power-plant-dam.html); [Liga](https://news.liga.net/en/amp/politics/news/zaporozhe-pogibli-voditel-trolleybusa-otets-i-vosmiletniy-rebenok-bolee-20-ranenyh). Traffic over the dam was halted; Ukrhydroenergo said there was no breach threat — [Ukranews](https://ukranews.com/en/amp/news/993810-there-is-no-threat-of-dam-breach-ukrhydroenergo-informed-about-state-of-dnipro-hpp-after-missile-hit); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/03/22/7447622). It was hit again and in "critical condition" by June 2024 — [Euromaidan Press](https://euromaidanpress.com/2024/06/01/russian-attack-leaves-major-ukrainian-hydroelectric-station-in-critical-condition/)
- **Media.** A video of the missile's flight and impact, apparently filmed from the plant's premises — [Liga](https://news.liga.net/en/amp/politics/video/poyavilas-zapis-momenta-prileta-po-dneproges-letela-krylataya-raketa-h-101-video). A photo of the burning trolleybus on Telegram — [Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/03/22/7447622). No OSINT geolocation of these was found.
- **How it mirrors the ZNPP.** The upstream twin of the Kakhovka dam. Ordinary life (a trolleybus) rides on top of infrastructure that is a target. If it breached, it would drain the very water that remained above the Great Meadow. It is the dam the student's zoom-out already frames.
- **Splat potential.** High for the dam's pre-war form (abundant tourist/drone YouTube footage of the crest and machine hall). Post-strike orbiting footage is unlikely. Officials asked citizens to report people filming the aftermath — [Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2024/03/22/7447622).
- **Approach.** Geolocate the impact video and the trolleybus position on the crest from crest lamp posts and spans. Use Sentinel-2 before/after for the machine-hall roof; sound analysis of the impact video's missile approach.
- **Ethics and safety.** Do not publish new close views of a working dam's vulnerable points; use only already-public footage.
- **Verification.** The strike is well documented; the trolleybus details conflict.

**C4. The Kakhovka HPP and dam crest road, and the flooded occupied villages (Korsunka, Dnipriany, Oleshky)** (NORSAR event at 46.7776, 33.37; villages from OSM)
- **What, when.** Dnipriany and Korsunka near Nova Kakhovka (Russian-occupied) were reported fully underwater, about 3 m in Korsunka. No organised evacuation: residents left on their own or by locals' boats, and coffins were floating in Korsunka — [Pravda, 9 June 2023](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2023/06/09/7406202/); [Meduza](https://meduza.io/amp/en/feature/2023/06/07/the-kakhovka-dam-disaster-unfolds); [Ukrinform](https://ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/3720155-russian-soldiers-climb-trees-mayor-of-nova-kakhovka-tells-how-community-flooded.html). The hidden toll in Oleshky is covered under Q2 ([AP via RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/ap-investigation-kakhovka-dam-death-toll/32750535.html)). REACH mapped the dam damage on 7 June 2023 — [REACH map PDF](https://repository.impact-initiatives.org/document/impact/8d9ecba9/REACH_UKR_Map_DRR_Kherson_Nova_Kakhovka_Damdamage_07JUN2023_A3.pdf)
- **How it mirrors the ZNPP.** The ZNPP's twin hostage, seized the same week and held by the same force, whose threat was carried out. The flood downstream is the other face of the Great Meadow's drainage upstream: one event, two landscapes.
- **Media.** Heavy existing coverage; drone footage of the flood; Maxar; Sentinel-1/2 flood extents.
- **Splat potential.** Low for the villages (occupied). The HPP's ruins were filmed widely on 6 June.
- **Ethics.** The dead and their families, a pending legal case (LAW/Truth Hounds), and residents still under occupation.
- **Verification.** Heavily investigated (Q2). A risk of duplicating others' work, so the student would better use it as context than as the microcosm itself.

**C5. The Mykolaiv water main: a city on a single lifeline**
- **What, when.** About 73 km of pipeline from the Dnipro to Mykolaiv were damaged in April 2022 (about 12 April) inside Russian-held ground. A BBC investigation, with military and UN experts, concluded it was almost certainly deliberately cut with placed explosives and that repair crews were not allowed in. Mykolaiv had no drinking water from the main for years. Estuary water was used for non-drinking supply from May 2022. In 2026 the mayor said drinking water was still absent but expected within a month (unverified) — [Euromaidan Press on BBC](https://euromaidanpress.com/2022/10/26/russia-accused-of-cutting-off-ukraine-water-supply-to-mykolaiv-bbc/); [Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/86126-bbs-investigation-the-russians-blew-up-the-water-pipeline-to-mykolaiv-on-purpose-they-are-not-allowing-repair-crews); [Texty](https://texty.org.ua/articles/107359/water-without-water-how-half-million-people-mykolaiv-live-without-regular-water-supply-and-try-restore-it/); [Mind.ua 2026](https://mind.ua/en/publications/amp/20260006-mayor-of-mykolaiv-in-the-next-month-drinking-water-will-reappear-in-the-city)
- **How it mirrors the ZNPP.** It is the purest "single lifeline" analogue: a linear infrastructure crossing contested ground, cut where its owner cannot reach it, holding half a million people hostage. It mirrors the ZNPP's lines exactly, with water for power.
- **Media.** The BBC footage of the damage; Texty's data piece; the pipeline route (OSM may map it).
- **Splat potential.** Medium (any orbiting footage of a repair site).
- **Approach.** Trace the route; Sentinel-2 scars at the break; chronolocate the repair footage.
- **Ethics.** Low to medium.
- **Verification.** The BBC finding is via secondary reports here. The primary BBC URL was not retrieved.

**C6. Antonivskyi bridge (Kherson): the lifeline seen from the other side**
- Struck by HIMARS around 26 July 2022 and again in August. DFRLab used Planet images (18, 20 and 27 July) to show new holes; by 25 August there were at least 16 holes and a ferry. Russia built a pontoon beside it — [DFRLab](https://dfrlab.org/2022/07/29/russian-war-report-russia-minimizes-ukrainian-damage-of-strategic-bridge-in-kherson/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonivka_Road_Bridge); [TWZ](https://twz.com/ukraine-situation-report-russia-building-pontoon-bridge-into-kherson). It mirrors the ZNPP as a single crossing that sustained an occupation. Good for satellite change detection; low risk.

**C7. Krynky: a cut-off village bridgehead on the left bank**
- A Ukrainian foothold from October 2023 (ISW geolocated the entry footage) to mid-2024. The village (fewer than 1,000 pre-war residents) was "almost completely destroyed" — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/with-krynky-lost-what-did-the-perilous-operation-accomplish/); [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/35948). It mirrors the ZNPP as a place held across water and supplied only by river crossings. Military footage dominates the record; the ethics around soldiers' deaths are heavy. No satellite damage study was found.

**C8. Kherson's Korabel ("Island") district: an island suburb under the drone "safari"**
- Repeatedly left without electricity by shelling; drone strikes on civilians (for example 11–12 July 2026, one killed; 20 August 2026, eight wounded); riverside residents urged to evacuate — [Pravda, July 2026](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/07/12/8043715/); [Korabelov.info](https://korabelov.info/en/?p=607017); [PRM](https://prm.ua/en/one-of-khersons-microdistricts-was-left-without-power-due-to-russian-shelling/). The UN COI found drone attacks on civilians in Kherson to be crimes against humanity (May 2025) — [UN Ukraine](https://ukraine.un.org/en/295172-un-commission-concludes-russian-armed-forces%E2%80%99-drone-attacks-against-civilians-kherson). It mirrors the ZNPP at small scale: an island whose power line is its lifeline, under fire from across the Dnipro. **High safety risk** (active targeting); use only published material.

**C9. Enerhodar and the Zaporizhzhia Thermal Power Plant: the plant's own town and switchyard**
- Seized with the ZNPP on 4 March 2022. The ZTPP 330 kV switchyard is the origin of Ferosplavna-1. The 330 kV ZTPP–Ferosplavna line was cut on 5 September 2022, reported as the last link to Ukraine's grid then — [Ukranews](https://ukranews.com/en/news/880261-last-power-line-that-connected-zaporizhzhia-npp-with-ukraine-s-power-system-turned-off-energoatom); [IRSN Aug 2022 report](https://admin.en.irsn.fr/sites/en/files/2023-09/IRSN_Information-report_Ukraine-Zaporizhzhya-NPP-after-shelling-of-5-6-August-2022_20220808.pdf). CIR placed the MLRS between the NPP and the TPP — [CIR](https://info-res.org/post/danger-zone-how-russia-is-risking-nuclear-disaster-at-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant). The IAEA notes Enerhodar residents, most of them plant staff, losing power and water after drone attacks on the city's substations (undated in the result) — [IAEA](https://www.iaea.org/node/158284). ## Q4. Which forensic techniques suit each case?

### Takeaway
The precedents use a small toolkit:
- skyline and landmark matching to fix camera positions (NPR, CIT, CIR);
- crater and debris-spray direction analysis (CIR);
- satellite change detection with Planet, Maxar or Sentinel (Texty, Skhemy, DFRLab, MIS, Vox);
- seismic signal timing (NORSAR);
- InSAR deformation (Houston);
- flood extent mapping with SAR and optical imagery (EOS, WUR);
- engineering-drawing reconstruction (NYT);
- testimony under anonymity (AP).

The student's pipeline (Sentinel-2/Landsat, OSM, splats from YouTube) maps onto these directly.

### Cited Findings
- Landmark matching through a window to place a camera (NPR, 4 March) — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/11/1085427380/ukraine-nuclear-power-plant-zaporizhzhia). Chimney-to-panorama matching to place a far-bank camera (CIT) — [Espreso](https://global.espreso.tv/russian-military-fire-mlrs-from-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant-the-insider-publishes-video)
- Crater and debris-spray direction, then trajectory back to the launch side — [CIR](https://info-res.org/post/danger-zone-how-russia-is-risking-nuclear-disaster-at-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant)
- Tower-condition and crater search on high-resolution imagery for a line outage (MIS) — [Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/news/4460/the-750-kv-power-line-at-zaporizhzhya-nuclear-power-plant-shows-no-signs-of-major-damage-new-satellite-investigation-by-greenpeace/)
- Planet time series of before/after damage (bridge holes) — [DFRLab](https://dfrlab.org/2022/07/29/russian-war-report-russia-minimizes-ukrainian-damage-of-strategic-bridge-in-kherson/)
- Seismic arrays timing an explosion (± 20–30 km) — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/06/08/1181078981/seismic-stations-detected-explosion-at-ukrainian-dam)
- InSAR settlement before failure — [UH](https://www.uh.edu/news-events/stories/2024/april/04032024-kakhovka-dam-collapse-milillo.php)
- Optical and radar flood mapping — [EOS](https://eos.com/blog/kakhovka-dam-flooding-detection-in-ukraine-for-pej/); [WUR](https://www.wur.nl/en/newsarticle/mapping-the-2023-ukraine-floods-after-the-kakhovka-dam-break-using-high-resolution-planet-imagery.htm)
