# Microcosms inside occupied Enerhodar, at the ZNPP and on the left bank (2022–2026)

Researched 29 October 2026 (web search + page fetches; about 30 tool calls). Every claim below has a link. Where a
page was paraphrased by a fetch tool and not read in full, that is said. Nothing here was downloaded into the library.

Corrections to the brief, from the sources:
- The cooling tower fire was on **11 August 2024**, not 2025 ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/cooling-tower-at-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-requires-demolition-after-fire-iaea-chief-says/); [Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/news/2024/08/12/moscow-and-kyiv-blame-each-other-after-fire-breaks-out-at-russian-occupied-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant)).
- The April 2024 "dome strike" was a drone hitting the **Unit 6** reactor dome on **7 April 2024** ([World Nuclear News](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/grossi-condemns-drone-strikes-at-zaporizhzhia-site); [Arms Control Association](https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2024-05/news/drones-hit-zaporizhzhia-reactor-building)).
- The CCTV of the 4 March 2022 battle was livestreamed on the plant's own YouTube channel (not a camera at the training centre), per NPR ([WYPR/NPR](https://www.wypr.org/2022-03-11/video-analysis-reveals-russian-attack-on-ukrainian-nuclear-plant-veered-near-disaster)).

## 1. Plant staff held hostage: abductions, detentions and the detention buildings

### Takeaway
Detention of ZNPP staff is the clearest literal "hostage" microcosm, and it can be tied to named buildings: the former
Enerhodar police (MVS) building, later reported as **Budivelnykiv Street 17**, which was hit by an explosion on
18 August 2023 (before/after photos exist), and "the Pit" (Яма), a basement on the former National Guard base on the
town's outskirts. No moving-camera footage of either building was found. The best-documented people are the diver
Andriy Honcharuk (died July 2022) and director-general Ihor Murashov (seized 30 September 2022 on the road from the
plant to the town).

### Cited Findings
**Scale and reports**
- Truth Hounds and Greenpeace Ukraine's report *Seizing Power* documents 226 cases of unlawful detention of Enerhodar residents and ZNPP employees, March 2022 to June 2025, with 39 survivor interviews and at least 7 detention sites. The full report is a Google Drive file: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1yLrRNPKerpDFBkDx017da2ZMotlh8dwI/view — [Greenpeace Ukraine](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/news/4290/rosatoms-complicity-in-war-crimes-and-nuclear-risks-at-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant-revealed/)
- Babel's report on it is dated 24 September 2025. It says the sites are "in Energodar and surrounding areas", that "some detainees were forced to sign a cooperation agreement or contract with 'Rosatom'", and that the crackdown intensified after a late-May 2022 assassination attempt on the occupation "mayor" Andriy Shevchyk — [Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/121672-truth-hounds-russians-illegally-detained-over-200-civilians-in-energodar-they-were-tortured-and-some-were-forced-to-work-with-rosatom)
- Kyiv Independent: 78 of those detained were plant staff, at least six people were tortured to death, and Rosatom is linked to pressuring staff into contracts — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russias-rosatom-complicit-in-war-crimes-report-alleges-says-treatment-of-power-plant-staff-risks-nuclear-disaster/)
- Truth Hounds' Roman Koval: "We counted at least seven such places in Enerhodar". The article names no buildings — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/human-rights-activists-count-7-torture-chambers-holding-ukraines-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-staff/)
- Business & Human Rights Centre summary: seven sites in Enerhodar and the surrounding area, two of them smaller, and severe overcrowding — [BHRRC](https://www.business-humanrights.org/en/latest-news/report-seizing-power-rosatoms-complicity-in-occupation-torture-and-nuclear-safety-breaches-at-the-zaporizhzhia-npp/)
- The figures conflict. The mayor's estimate of about 1,000 detained is cited by Truth Hounds ([Truth Hounds](https://truth-hounds.org/en/cases/in-a-nuclear-prison-how-rosatom-turned-europes-largest-nuclear-power-plant-into-a-torture-chamber-and-how-can-the-world-stop-it/); the page returned 403 to the fetch tool, so this comes from a search snippet). A 2025 local article says about two thousand residents passed through Russian torture sites ([1news.zp.ua](https://1news.zp.ua/yak-rosiya-peresliduye-katuye-ta-nezakonno-uvyaznyuye-zhyteliv-enerhodara-i-pratsivnykiv-zaporizkoyi-aes/), via a search summary). The documented count is 226.

**Named buildings**
- **"The Pit" (Яма):** "a basement on the former base of the National Guard of Ukraine on the outskirts of the city". A repair worker held there for more than 70 days describes a windowless underground room with one guarded entrance and wooden crates and boards for beds. The source is a Ukrainian retelling of a Wall Street Journal investigation — [Nikopol News (citing WSJ)](https://nikopol.nikopolnews.net/nikopol/zvirstva-okupantiv-na-zaes/)
- **Former police (MVS) building:** Volodymyr Zhaivoronok was held there 53 days from about 20–22 June 2022, in a cell 3.5 × 3.5 m holding up to 24 people, with a courtroom above it. Interview published 18 April 2025 (Radio Svoboda, Novyny Pryazovia) — [Radio Svoboda](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/novyny-pryazovya-katuvannya-smert-v-poloni-enerhodar-zaes-zhavoronok/33388602.html)
- **Budivelnykiv Street 17 (вул. Будівельників, 17):** an explosion on 18 August 2023. The building housed a police station before the invasion and was then seized by occupation "services". Ivan Fedorov (Melitopol's mayor, on Telegram) said a torture site was there "according to preliminary data", and the local Telegram channel energodar_today24 said the occupation "military police" used it. The article has photos of the blast site beside a pre-war photo of the building, plus Telegram screenshots — [Obozrevatel](https://war.obozrevatel.com/ukr/v-energodari-stavsya-vibuh-u-budivli-de-okupanty-organizuvali-kativnyu-foto.htm); also [Espreso](https://espreso.tv/v-okupovanomu-energodari-progimiv-vibukh-u-budinku-de-ymovirno-rosiyani-katuyut-tsivilnikh), [Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/929605.html)
- The mayor says there were at least ten detention and torture sites, some in the ZNPP's sanitary protection zone and on industrial sites, and in the town in the police department ([search summary of NV](https://nv.ua/ukr/ukraine/events/zaporizka-oblast-yak-ce-zhiti-v-okupaciji-bilya-zaes-rozpovidaye-mer-energodaru-novini-ukrajini-50262456.html); not read in full).
- The General Staff reported a new torture site "in the south-eastern part of Enerhodar" on 17 March 2023, without naming a building — [Novynarnia](https://novynarnia.com/2023/03/17/v-energodari-okupanty-vlashtuvaly-novu-kativnyu/)
- Deputy mayor Ivan Samoidiuk was abducted about 19 March 2022 and held 333 days, mostly in occupied Melitopol (solitary confinement, then a commercial building), until a February 2023 exchange (the article gives 16 and 17 February). He says Enerhodar's police stations were "simply packed", so factories were used as holding sites — [KHPG](https://khpg.org/1608812032); abduction reported in [Ukrainska Pravda, 20 March 2022](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/03/20/7333019/)

**Named plant staff**
- **Andriy Honcharuk**, a diver in the plant's hydro-technical shop: beaten after refusing to dive into the plant's **sprinkler (spray) pond** (бризкальний басейн), and died on 3 July 2022 (mayor Orlov; Energoatom reported the beating on 30 June) — [LB.ua](https://lb.ua/society/2022/07/03/522020_pomer_vodolaz_zaporizkoi_aes_yakogo.html); [Radio Svoboda](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/news-enerhodar-pomer-vodolaz-aes/31927792.html); [TSN](https://tsn.ua/ato/okupanti-do-smerti-zakatuvali-vodolaza-zaes-mer-energodara-2102161.html); Orlov's account (text only, interview 16 December 2024) in [Civil Voices Museum](https://civilvoicesmuseum.org/stories/okupanty-vymagaly-schob-vodolaz-strybnuv-u-bryzkalnyy-baseyn)
- The sources disagree on where Honcharuk was held. Zhaivoronok says a diver named Honcharuk, about 60, died in his cell in the former police building and was later made a Hero of Ukraine ([Radio Svoboda](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/novyny-pryazovya-katuvannya-smert-v-poloni-enerhodar-zaes-zhavoronok/33388602.html)). The WSJ retelling says he was beaten in "the Pit" ([Nikopol News](https://nikopol.nikopolnews.net/nikopol/zvirstva-okupantiv-na-zaes/)).
- **Ihor Murashov**, director-general: stopped by a Russian patrol at about 16:00 on 30 September 2022 "on his way from the station to Enerhodar", taken out of his car and driven away blindfolded (Energoatom's Petro Kotin). Released by 3–4 October after IAEA intervention — [Ukrainska Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/10/1/7369926/); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/10/1/russia-detains-head-of-ukraines-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant); [RTHK](https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1669538-20221004.htm). The WSJ retelling says he was taken to "the Pit" ([Nikopol News](https://nikopol.nikopolnews.net/nikopol/zvirstva-okupantiv-na-zaes/)).
- Also named in the WSJ retelling: contractor Volodymyr Zhaivoronok; IT head Oleh Kostiukov (beaten); repairman Serhii Shvets (shot five times on 23 May, survived) — [Nikopol News](https://nikopol.nikopolnews.net/nikopol/zvirstva-okupantiv-na-zaes/)
- Texty.org.ua and the Zaporizhzhia Investigation Centre made a data project ("Ядерний терор") with maps of the seizure of Enerhodar and the ZNPP, the energy network and weapons ranges, and photos of Murashov after his release. It does not map detention sites — [incentre.zp.ua](https://incentre.zp.ua/energodar-znpp-dataproject/)

### Inferences
- Murashov's seizure happened on the road between the plant and the town. That road links the workplace, the commute and the town, and it can be shown on OSM and satellite imagery. A microcosm built on it would show a road, not a person.
- The sprinkler pond is a strong and unusual detail: one man's refusal to dive into the plant's cooling water, the same water the whole safety case depends on after the dam. The ponds are clearly visible on Sentinel-2 and OSM.
- Budivelnykiv St 17 is the one building with an address, a dated event (18 August 2023) and before/after photographs. Placing it needs an OSM match of the address; I found no published coordinates.

### Gaps
- I found no video of any detention building filmed around it (no splat potential). Footage of the 18 August 2023 explosion may exist on energodar_today24 or other Telegram channels, but none was found or verified.
- The National Guard base ("the Pit") has no address or coordinates in the sources. The Truth Hounds PDF on Google Drive may list sites, but it could not be fetched.
- No original WSJ article URL was retrieved; only Nikopol News' Ukrainian retelling of it.

## 2. The town living on the plant's power and water (Enerhodar, ZTPP)

### Takeaway
Enerhodar repeatedly lost electricity and water together, and the pumps stopped when the power went. That is the
town in miniature mirroring the plant's own loss of off-site power. It is well reported for 2022 and 2024, and in
2025 the plant moved its off-site emergency centre into the town. I found no moving-camera footage of the town's
infrastructure (boiler houses, water pumping station) and nothing specific on the ZTPP's 330 kV switchyard.

### Cited Findings
- 25 August 2022: Enerhodar lost light and water through shelling while the ZNPP was disconnected from the grid for the first time in its history — [Fakty](https://fakty.com.ua/ua/ukraine/20220825-cherez-obstrily-vijsk-rf-energodar-zalyshyvsya-bez-svitla-ta-vody-mer/); [Forbes Ukraine](https://forbes.ua/news/energodar-zalishivsya-bez-svitla-cherez-obstril-rosiyan-zaporizka-aes-prodovzhue-pratsyuvati-25082022-7924); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/08/25/7364739)
- September 2022: "Enerhodar off the grid after powerful explosion" (6 September), and power went off again on 7 September — [Ukrainska Pravda 6 Sep](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/09/06/7366296/); [Ukrainska Pravda 7 Sep](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/09/07/7366473/)
- October 2022: electricity, water and internet were cut, and the town was without power and water for a third day in a row on 9 October — [Fakty](https://fakty.com.ua/en/proisshestvija/20221007-v-energodari-potuzhni-obstrily-znykly-svitlo-voda-ta-internet-mer); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/10/9/7371008/)
- July 2024: Grossi reported another power outage in Enerhodar, where most plant staff live, with a shortage of tap water — [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/tag-enerhodar/)
- An undated IAEA item reports that a drone damaged two substations and Enerhodar was without power for about 16 hours, which stopped the water main pump — [Zaxid/Interfax search summary](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/995529.html) (date not confirmed)
- 23 September 2025: shelling cut the plant's last 750 kV line, the 10th and longest complete loss of off-site power. Seven emergency diesel generators powered the site, and the plant set up a new off-site emergency centre in Enerhodar because the former one was inaccessible — [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2025-10-20/article-7472/update-on-zaporizhzhia/); [Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/news/2025/10/01/ukraine-warns-of-critical-situation-as-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant-left-without-external-power-for-over-a-week); [GlobalSecurity (IAEA 24 Sep 2025)](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2025/09/ukraine-250924-iaea01.htm)
- In June 2023 the IAEA said the site could draw on Enerhodar's water system, a deep excavation at the cargo port, and mobile pumps — [GlobalSecurity (IAEA, June 2023)](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2023/06/ukraine-230606-iaea01.htm) (via search summary)
- An earlier, pre-war (probably February 2021) accident at the Zaporizhzhia TPP also left the town without light and water: the pumps stopped — [The Page](https://thepage.ua/news/na-zaporozhskoj-tes-proizoshla-avariya); [Zaxid](https://zaxid.net/70_tis_lyudey_zalishilisya_bez_svitla_i_vodi_cherez_avariyu_v_energodari_n1513923). (The date is the search tool's inference.)

### Inferences
- "Town and plant fail together" is a one-to-one mirror: the plant's diesel generators stand to the reactors as the town's dry taps stand to its people. The town's water pumping station is the object, though I found no location for it.
- The ZTPP is where Ferosplavna-1 leaves, and its chimneys were the landmark CIT used to geolocate the MLRS video (section 3). The thermal plant links the town, the grid and the forensic method.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on the town's boiler houses or heating in the winters of 2022–26, or on the ZTPP's state (damage, operation) after 2022.
- No date for the 16-hour outage. No 2025–26 reports of the town's own outages during the month-long plant blackout (September–October 2025).

## 3. Military use of the plant: vehicles, firing positions, strikes, and which have geolocated video

### Takeaway
The material is rich and already geolocated. Three pieces are best: CNN's verified video of Z-marked trucks in the
Unit 1 turbine hall (August 2022, later confirmed by the IAEA), the CIR "Danger Zone" set with published coordinates
(convoy at Unit 2, equipment under Unit 5's pipes, fighting position at the entrance, MLRS between the NPP and the
ZTPP), and The Insider/CIT MLRS video geolocated by the ZTPP's chimneys.

### Cited Findings
- **Turbine hall video (Aug 2022):** it circulated on 18 August 2022 and was geolocated and verified by CNN. Trucks marked "Z" stand at the far western end of the ground floor of a turbine hall, about 130 m from the reactor. The filming date is unknown — [TWZ](https://twz.com/russian-military-vehicles-inside-sensitive-building-at-ukraine-nuke-plant); [CNN via KTVZ](https://ktvz.com/news/national-world/cnn-europe-mideast-africa/2022/08/18/russian-vehicles-seen-inside-turbine-hall-at-ukraine-nuclear-plant/); [CNN Breaking Telegram](https://t.me/s/CNN_Breaking/632); [bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/leaked-footage-from-zaporizhzhia-power-plant-reveals-five-russian-military-trucks-in-turbine-room-254053)
- After the September 2022 mission, the IAEA reported "several military trucks on the ground floor of the unit 1 and unit 2 turbine halls" and vehicles under the overpass connecting the units — quoted in [TWZ](https://twz.com/russian-military-vehicles-inside-sensitive-building-at-ukraine-nuke-plant) (search summary)
- **CIR Eyes on Russia, "Danger Zone", 7 September 2022**, with geolocations: convoy entering near reactor 2 (coordinates in CIR's report); equipment under the cooling pipes at reactor 5 (coordinates in CIR's report); Russian fighting position at the plant entrance (coordinates in CIR's report); shelling in Enerhodar centre near the Taras Shevchenko monument (coordinates in CIR's report). Footage sources: The Insider's drone video of the convoy (2 August), Maxar imagery (29 August), Izvestia footage of IAEA inspectors passing Z trucks (1 September), ARD Mediathek video of the fighting position, and Rybar's Telegram post (t.me/rybar/38198). The MLRS launch area was placed between the NPP and the ZTPP, and a 2 September shell on the town was traced to the Russian-held south side — [CIR](https://info-res.org/post/danger-zone-how-russia-is-risking-nuclear-disaster-at-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant)
- **The Insider MLRS video (night of 2–3 Sept 2022):** CIT identified the Zaporizhzhia TPP's chimneys in the frame, placing the camera on the opposite (Nikopol) bank and the launch west of the TPP, where the NPP is — [The Insider](https://theins.press/en/news/254677); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/09/04/7366003/); [Euromaidan Press (CIT)](https://euromaidanpress.com/2022/09/04/russian-occupiers-fire-from-zaporizhzhia-npp-territory-with-anti-aircraft-missiles-the-insider/?swcfpc=1). One outlet cautioned that it is impossible to say exactly whether the rockets are launched from the plant site (search summary).
- The Insider also published video of anti-aircraft guns firing from the ZNPP, with fire adjusted by drones — [Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/83829-the-insider-publication-showed-how-russian-anti-aircraft-guns-fire-from-the-zaporizhzhia-npp-the-fire-is-adjusted-by-drones)
- Bellingcat drone footage (5 August 2022) showed strikes near the cooling ponds and the dry spent-fuel storage casks (174 casks). This is a secondary source, so the Bellingcat original should be checked — [Harvard "Taking Bearings" blog](https://archive.blogs.harvard.edu/kleelerner/zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-perils-part-of-novel-challenges-regarding-nuclear-safety/)
- UK Ministry of Defence imagery of 21 August 2023 shows trucks and APCs about 60 m from a unit; Planet Labs imagery of 5 July 2023 shows objects on the Unit 4 roof. The IAEA found no mines or explosives on the roofs of Units 3 and 4 or in the turbine halls (August 2023) — [Fakty](https://fakty.com.ua/en/ukraine/20230804-eksperty-magate-ne-znajshly-min-chy-vybuhivky-na-dahah-dvoh-reaktoriv-zaes-grossi/amp); [Espreso](https://espreso.tv/suputnik-viyaviv-na-dakhu-4-energobloku-zaes-novi-obekti-radio-svoboda); [Universe Magazine](https://universemagazine.com/suputnykovi-znimky-pokazaly-rosijsku-tehniku-poblyzu-zaes/)
- **Mines / trenches:** HUR's Budanov said the cooling pond was mined (unverified). Greenpeace reported Russian trenches round the cooling pond, including on its dam, focused on the sluice gates, the jetty and the north shore (with Energoatom photos) — [UNIAN](https://www.unian.ua/war/rosiyani-riyut-okopi-bilya-stavku-oholodzhuvacha-zaporizkoji-aes-greenpeace-12711870.html); [Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/918901.html)
- **Unit 6 dome, 7 April 2024:** the IAEA saw minor superficial scorching on the dome roof. Rosatom said a drone first hit near the canteen at 11:38 (three injured), then the cargo port area, then the dome. The attribution is disputed. Rosatom released a photo — [WNN](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/grossi-condemns-drone-strikes-at-zaporizhzhia-site); [ACA](https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2024-05/news/drones-hit-zaporizhzhia-reactor-building); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/04/08/1243344592/attacks-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-ukraine)
- **Cooling tower fire, 11 August 2024:** Zelensky published video of black smoke and flames at the tower's base. The IAEA judged it unlikely that the fire started at the base, and Grossi later said the tower requires demolition. Grossi's inspection of the tower was also filmed — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/cooling-tower-at-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-requires-demolition-after-fire-iaea-chief-says/); [LIGA video](https://news.liga.net/en/politics/video/iaea-chief-grossi-inspects-cooling-tower-at-zaporizhzhia-npp-where-fire-occurred-video); [WNN](https://wnn.world-nuclear.org/articles/iaea-experts-continue-zaporizhzhia-cooling-tower-f)
- Russian state media (CGTN, 27 August 2026) reported a UAV attack on the dry spent-fuel storage. This is a single state-media account and unverified — [CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-08-27/news-1PWZ6fxs8o0/p.html)

### Inferences
- The cooling tower is the best splat or orbit candidate on the site. It was filmed from several sides: Zelensky's video, Grossi's inspection video, and likely Russian and IAEA footage. A burned cooling tower is a strong object for a "plant reduced" story. Any splat would be drawn from third-party footage, said so.
- CIR's four coordinates can be pinned directly on the film's satellite passes, the way the project's insets are pinned to OSM towers.
- The MLRS-by-the-ZTPP-chimneys geolocation shows the forensic method itself: the thermal plant from which Ferosplavna-1 leaves is the landmark used to locate weapons fire.

### Gaps
- No NYT Visual Investigations or Skhemy piece specific to ZNPP firing positions was found. The training centre's later use is not covered.
- I did not confirm Bellingcat's original 5 August 2022 publication URL.

## 4. The cooling pond, the sprinkler ponds, the wells and the shoreline

### Takeaway
After the dam, the plant's cooling came down to its own pond, its sprinkler ponds, and wells drilled beside them.
That is a small, mappable water story inside the big one, and it links to Honcharuk (the diver ordered into the
sprinkler pond). I found no geolocated videos of the shoreline or of fishermen.

### Cited Findings
- By mid-September 2023 the IAEA reported nine new groundwater wells feeding the sprinkler ponds, with about 10–12 planned — [Modern Power Systems](https://www.modernpowersystems.com/news/cooling-system-at-zaporizhzhya-stabilised-but-attacks-in-the-area-continue-11158161/)
- The cooling pond fell about 1 cm a day after the breach and was topped up from the thermal plant's inlet channel — [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/tag-iaea/step-1723481712); [GlobalSecurity (IAEA Aug 2023)](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2023/08/ukraine-230822-iaea01.htm) (via search summary); [Ukranews](https://ukranews.com/ua/news/1023925-u-stavku-oholodzhuvachi-zaporizkoyi-atomnoyi-elektrostantsiyi-zaes-prodovzhuye-znyzhuvatysya)
- Russia's IAEA information circulars give the cooling-pond level as 15.4 m (May 2024) and 15.06 m (July 2024). These are operator figures — [INFCIRC/1206 (ru)](https://www.iaea.org/sites/default/files/publications/documents/infcircs/2024/infcirc1206_rus.pdf); [INFCIRC/1227 (ru)](https://iaea.org/sites/default/files/publications/documents/infcircs/2024/infcirc1227_rus.pdf) (values from search snippets; the PDFs were not opened)
- Trenches on the cooling pond's dam, sluice gates, jetty and north shore — [UNIAN](https://www.unian.ua/war/rosiyani-riyut-okopi-bilya-stavku-oholodzhuvacha-zaporizkoji-aes-greenpeace-12711870.html)

### Inferences
- The cooling pond's shoreline can be measured on every clear Sentinel-2 pass with the pipeline's own waterline method (`satellite/`). Its decline is an observed, sourced microcosm of the reservoir's drainage, inside the plant's fence.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on Enerhodar's fishermen or residents' shoreline videos. No drone or orbit footage of the sprinkler ponds or wells.

## 5. The blockade (March 2022) and the rally (2 April 2022)

### Takeaway
The town's own collective act: thousands of residents stood at the town entrance from 2 March 2022, and the main-square
rally was dispersed by mortar fire on 2 April 2022 (4 injured). Both have photos or short videos, but the original
uploaders are mostly unnamed and the faces are identifiable.

### Cited Findings
- 2 March 2022: residents stood "like a wall of defence at the city entrance", on the road between Enerhodar and the "Dniprovskyi turn" (Дніпровський поворот), for the third time. ### Inferences
- The "Dniprovskyi turn" roadblock sits on the one road into the town, a road the transmission lines also cross. It can be found on OSM. Phone videos there likely move (people filming the crowd), but none was found that orbits a point.

## 6. The 4 March 2022 battle on the plant's own CCTV livestream

### Takeaway
This is the best single forensic artifact: a 4 h 21 min fixed-camera feed livestreamed on the plant's YouTube channel,
already analysed by NPR with CIR. The camera does not move (no splat), but it is time-stamped and its view can be
matched to the site plan.

### Cited Findings
- NPR reviewed 4 h 21 min of security-camera video livestreamed on the plant's YouTube channel from shortly before 23:30 on 3 March. A tank column approached from the south-east; about 1 h 20 min in, a Ukrainian missile disabled a lead tank; a two-hour firefight followed. The training building and the main administrative building were hit (five RPGs), and firefighters arriving about 02:50 were turned back. A transmission line to the main substation was hit (the IAEA said two of four high-voltage lines were damaged). Analysts: Leone Hadavi (CIR), Edwin Lyman, Tom Bielefeld. Footage: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYUT36YGOh8 ; IAEA press conference: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-zsIMfTEUjs — [WYPR/NPR](https://www.wypr.org/2022-03-11/video-analysis-reveals-russian-attack-on-ukrainian-nuclear-plant-veered-near-disaster)
- The fire in the training building was out at 06:20. Grossi said the building hit was a training centre, not part of the reactor — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2022/3/4/russia-ukraine-war-fire-at-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-put-out)
- Texty / ZIC list "a live stream from a camera in the administrative building (4 March 2022)" among their media — [incentre.zp.ua](https://incentre.zp.ua/energodar-znpp-dataproject/)

### Inferences
- The power line hit in the first hour of the occupation is the film's first "cut", directly on the plant's own camera. It ties the microcosm to the project's line record from the start.

### Gaps
- I did not check whether the YouTube video is still online, or its uploader name.

## 8. Synthesis: the strongest candidate microcosms

### Takeaway
Six candidates, ranked by how well they mirror the big story and what can be done forensically with them. The
strongest are A (the diver and the sprinkler pond), B (the director's road) and C (the CCTV night); D–F are good
supporting candidates.

### Cited Findings
- **A. The diver and the sprinkler pond (June–July 2022).** Andriy Honcharuk was ordered to dive into the sprinkler pond, refused, was beaten, and died on 3 July 2022 ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/society/2022/07/03/522020_pomer_vodolaz_zaporizkoi_aes_yakogo.html)). The sprinkler ponds later depended on new wells after the dam ([Modern Power Systems](https://www.modernpowersystems.com/news/cooling-system-at-zaporizhzhya-stabilised-but-attacks-in-the-area-continue-11158161/)). It mirrors the big story through the plant's cooling water and a body held hostage to it. Media: satellite passes of the ponds; no footage of the event. Where he was held is disputed (police building vs "the Pit").
- **B. The road from the plant to the town (30 Sep 2022).** Murashov was seized blindfolded on the commute ([Ukrainska Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/10/1/7369926/)), with the plant-entrance fighting position (coordinates in CIR's report) ([CIR](https://info-res.org/post/danger-zone-how-russia-is-risking-nuclear-disaster-at-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant)). It mirrors the cut line: the plant's head cut off from the town. Media: OSM road plus satellite imagery; the exact spot is not published.
- **C. The night of 3–4 March 2022 on the plant's own camera.** 4 h 21 min of CCTV, livestreamed, analysed by NPR/CIR, with a transmission line hit on camera ([NPR](https://www.wypr.org/2022-03-11/video-analysis-reveals-russian-attack-on-ukrainian-nuclear-plant-veered-near-disaster); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYUT36YGOh8)). Fixed camera: a timeline or sightline reconstruction, not a splat.
- **D. Budivelnykiv Street 17, the former police building (2022–2023).** A detention site with a cell 3.5 × 3.5 m for up to 24 people ([Radio Svoboda](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/novyny-pryazovya-katuvannya-smert-v-poloni-enerhodar-zaes-zhavoronok/33388602.html)), and an explosion on 18 August 2023 with before/after photos ([Obozrevatel](https://war.obozrevatel.com/ukr/v-energodari-stavsya-vibuh-u-budivli-de-okupanty-organizuvali-kativnyu-foto.htm)). It mirrors "the town held hostage" at room scale (a dimensioned cell suits an architectural drawing). The room is reconstructed from testimony, not imaged.
- **E. The turbine hall and the trucks (Aug 2022), and the cooling tower (Aug 2024).** CNN-verified video, confirmed by the IAEA ([TWZ](https://twz.com/russian-military-vehicles-inside-sensitive-building-at-ukraine-nuke-plant)); the burned cooling tower filmed from several angles ([LIGA video](https://news.liga.net/en/politics/video/iaea-chief-grossi-inspects-cooling-tower-at-zaporizhzhia-npp-where-fire-occurred-video)). It mirrors the plant used as a shield. Splat potential: the cooling tower, if footage circling it can be found.
- **F. The wall of people at the town entrance (2 March 2022) and the square (2 April 2022).** A crowd blocking the one road in, and a rally dispersed by mortar fire ([Ukrainska Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/03/2/7327506/); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/04/2/7336565/)). It mirrors people standing in for the severed connection. Media: Orlov's photos, a 1-minute video.

### Inferences
- **Ethics, for every candidate.** Detainees and rally participants are identifiable, and many relatives remain in occupied Enerhodar. Blur or avoid faces, never geolocate private homes, and don't amplify occupation Telegram footage without labelling it as such. Survivor testimony (Zhaivoronok, Samoidiuk) is published, but its use should follow the publishers' framing. Radio Svoboda notes it could not independently verify some testimony.
- **Splat potential is low across the board.** No orbiting footage of a detention building was found. The best bets are the cooling tower and the turbine hall (third-party video). The rest suits stills, satellite passes and drawn reconstructions labelled as such.

### Gaps
- Original WSJ, Bellingcat and Skhemy URLs were not retrieved. The Truth Hounds full report (Google Drive) was not read, and it is the likely source for the other detention site locations.
