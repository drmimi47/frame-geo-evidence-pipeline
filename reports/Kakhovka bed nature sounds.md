# Nightingales reach the bed, never the wires

**No nature recording has been made at the Enerhodar crossing, on the bed between Enerhodar and Marhanets, or anywhere on the occupied south bank since the dam fell on 6 June 2023.** Every public archive the researchers searched (xeno-canto, iNaturalist, Macaulay/eBird, Freesound, radio aporee, Observation.org, university and artists' collections, YouTube, Telegram and the press) gives the same answer. Real, dated, openly licensed recordings exist in two places nearby. The first is one birder's north-bank set around Marhanets, by Ekaterina Kormina. She recorded at about **6–12 km from where the two lines cross the water, and 2–4.5 km from their routes over land**. Four of her post-drainage recordings are on xeno-canto under **CC0**, including XC1016141, the thrush nightingale the storyboard already uses; another 61 on iNaturalist are CC BY-NC. The second is the only recordings made **on the drained bed itself**: 144 iNaturalist clips by the zoologist Oleksii Vasyliuk (**CC BY**), made in May and July 2026 at Mar'ianske and Novovorontsovka, about **53 km downstream**. Ninety-six of them lie inside the bed's outline, and two carry his own notes of an FPV drone and of a starling mimicking a drone being shot down. The bed's published soundscape is one thing only: hundreds of nightingales and reed warblers in the young willow forest in May, heard "mixed with the humming of front-line drones". No one has published or recorded frog choruses, insect song, wind in the willows or water on the bed. Nor is there any recording, or any count since 2012, of the cormorant colony that once nested on the crossing towers. The film can therefore blend real birdsong with its synthesized Hum honestly, but only as a montage of sounds recorded elsewhere, each named by its own place and date. This report gives the candidates, the rules for using them, and who to ask for more.

## The crossing's reach is a silence made by the front line

The bed between the ZNPP and the Marhanets bank is the front line, and that is why there is nothing to hear from it. The river channel is the line of contact. A border-guard video of 18 July 2026 says "the front line today runs along the bed of the former reservoir. Here, opposite the Zaporizhzhia NPP, the 8th Berdiansk border detachment holds the defence" ([Telegram, nikopoldpua/58641](https://t.me/nikopoldpua/58641); [24 Kanal](https://24tv.ua/velikiy-lug-stav-nulovoyu-liniyeyu-bilya-zaporizhzhya-yak-prikordonniki_n3094836/amp)). It is the only item found that places itself at the crossing's reach. It is military footage and unusable. On the south bank, Enerhodar's occupation administration reportedly banned residents from within 500 m of the shore after people were blown up by mines crossing the shallows ([bfm.ru, via TASS](https://www.bfm.ru/news/555345); search snippet only). iNaturalist holds just **11 observations of any kind since 6 June 2023** in the box covering Enerhodar, Kamianka-Dniprovska, Vodiane and Velyka Znamianka, and none with sound ([iNaturalist API](https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations?swlat=47.35&swlng=34.25&nelat=47.55&nelng=34.80&d1=2023-06-06)). No occupation-side nature footage was found, though Rutube blocked access and VK and the occupation Telegram channels were not searched. Even the scientists who work on the bed stay at its edge. Vasyliuk's May 2026 fauna survey could cover only "a narrow strip along the shore — about 200 meters inland" for security reasons ([UAnimals, 2026](https://uanimals.org/en/know-act/environment-war/a-forest-found-nowhere-else-in-ukraine-what-s-happening-at-the-bottom-of-the-kakhovka-reservoir-three-years-after-the-disaster/)).

The archive search confirms the gap. A xeno-canto box query over the whole reach (47.20–47.85 N, 33.30–35.30 E) returns **13 recordings from two recordists** and none on the bed ([xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org/explore?query=box:47.2,33.3,47.85,35.3)). Seven are Kormina's around Marhanets (CC0). Six are Rostyslav Romanyshyn's winter gulls, crows and a Caspian tern in Zaporizhzhia, about 45 km upstream, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 ([XC1050116](https://xeno-canto.org/1050116)). iNaturalist has **267 sound observations** in the same box. Two people made 260 of them, and all but two of the 267 are birds ([iNaturalist API](https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations?sounds=true&swlat=47.20&swlng=33.30&nelat=47.85&nelng=35.30&per_page=200)). Macaulay Library holds 7 audio assets in all of Zaporizhzhia oblast, none on the reservoir. Its Dnipropetrovsk assets since July 2023 all lie north of 47.94 N, and its Kherson assets are all 2017–2021 in the far south ([ML search, UA-23](https://search.macaulaylibrary.org/catalog?regionCode=UA-23&mediaType=audio&sort=obs_date_desc)). Freesound returns nothing for Kakhovka, Nikopol, Enerhodar, Marhanets, Zaporizhzhia or Khortytsia in Latin or Cyrillic script ([Freesound](https://freesound.org/search/?q=Zaporizhzhia)). Radio aporee's map has three points in the whole region, all in Kryvyi Rih ([aporee](https://aporee.org/maps/?loc=47.5,34.4,8)). No passive acoustic monitoring (AudioMoth, BirdNET) of the bed has been published.

**A note on distances.** The three research notes measured "distance to the crossing" three ways: to the edge of a box drawn round it, to a point mid-crossing, and to the config's crossing centre. That is why Kormina's recordings appear as 2.5–8.4 km in one note and 10–17 km in the others. This report re-measured every candidate on the OpenStreetMap line geometry the 3D survey uses. It gives two figures: the distance to the lines' spans over the water of 5 June 2023 (the crossing proper, 3.9–8.4 km north of the plant), and, near the north bank, the distance to the lines' routes over land. On land the Ferosplavna circuits run west along Marhanets's southern edge towards Nikopol, and Dniprovska runs north. Private recordists' coordinates are rounded to two decimals before measuring, so each figure is good to about ±0.7 km. The figures are the researchers' own calculation, not a source's. One more comparison matters for the mix. The project's footprint estimate puts the lines' corona audible at most about **1.4 km** away, even on a still wet night. Every recording below was made beyond that, so none could contain the lines' own sound, and any blend of birds and Hum is a montage, never "what it sounds like there".

Kormina's four CC0 files are the most useful material, and the only material free of every condition. They were made after the drainage, on the shore of the same reach, within a few kilometres of where the lines run over land, and CC0 waives every right ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)). Her xeno-canto uploads were all made on one day, 14 July 2025, from her own archive. She is the same person as iNaturalist's "uabird", so the two sets are one recordist's work, and some files probably appear in both. Where the same file appears under both licences, the CC0 copy on xeno-canto can be used freely. Her iNaturalist record has something xeno-canto lacks: the reach's year in sound, month by month from June 2023 to August 2026. Thrush nightingales sing in late April to mid June, a scops owl calls at 01:56 in June, and Savi's warblers reel in April and August. Waders such as the stilt, Temminck's stint and spotted redshank arrive in July 2026, at points north of the town that suggest shallow wet ground. Winter brings redwings, a horned lark, dunnocks and Bohemian waxwings ([iNaturalist API](https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations?sounds=true&swlat=47.20&swlng=33.30&nelat=47.85&nelng=35.30&per_page=200)). One spot recurs often in her record. It is given here only as "near Marhanets", and it must never be drawn as a point. The storyboard's soundscape still already bins these recordings into 1 km cells for that reason.

Vasyliuk's set is the evidence-side answer to "what does the bed itself sound like". It was made on the drained floor, it is dated and georeferenced, its recordist is a named zoologist of the Ukrainian Nature Conservation Group, and its licence allows any use with credit. It is also clearly not the crossing. It lies about 53 km downstream on the same right bank, in the same willow-and-reed habitat. The files are short identification clips, not ambiences, and they were recorded very quietly, so raising them to mix level will lift their noise floor. A level check of nine files by the researchers, not a listen, found stretches where more than 70% of the energy lay below 300 Hz. It also found loud low-frequency events: in the avocet clip, about 45 s in and 35 dB over the file's median, and in the paddyfield warbler clip, about 44 s in and 48 dB over it. These could be wind, handling, vehicles or distant explosions. One file in the set has a drone noted, and the place was a frontline area under drone attack, so the whole 13–14 May set has to be listened through before any of it is blended.

### Two counts of Vasyliuk's bed recordings, reconciled

One note counts **56 at Mar'ianske inside the bed outline and 88 round Novovorontsovka**. The other says **96 of 144 on the bed**. Both are right, and they describe the same 144 files. The researchers re-ran the point-in-polygon test on the saved iNaturalist replies against OSM relation 1600087 ([OSM](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/1600087)). All 56 Mar'ianske recordings, every one made on 14 May, lie inside the outline, 5–300 m in from its edge. Of the 88 at Novovorontsovka, **40 lie inside** (33 from 13 May, 7 from 14 July; up to 370 m in), and 48 lie outside in the village, up to about 1.1 km out. That gives 56 + 40 = **96**, the same 96 that iNaturalist's own place "Former Kakhovka Reservoir" (id 216144) returns, ID for ID ([place 216144 sounds](https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations?sounds=true&place_id=216144)). The first note's "on the outline's edge (0.05 km)" gave the Novovorontsovka cluster's nearest distance without splitting it into inside and outside. Both drone-flagged files lie inside the outline: the FPV nightingale at Mar'ianske and the starling at Novovorontsovka.

## The bed's published soundscape is nightingales by the hundred, and drones

Every published description of the bed's sound comes from one source: the UNCG expedition of May 2026 near Novovorontsovka, on the right bank in Kherson oblast. It describes birdsong. Vasyliuk: "I didn't think I could hear so many at once", and at night the singing "turns into a noise, or an echo" ([TSN, 25 May 2026](https://tsn.ua/exclusive/dykyy-lis-ta-solovyi-shcho-vidbuvayetsia-na-mistsi-znyshchenoho-rosiianamy-kakhovskoho-vodoshovyshcha-3088937.html)). Texty relays him: "there is continuous singing in the forest; a huge number of nightingales and reed warblers live there now", and "birds became the true masters of the willow thickets" ([Texty, 1 Jul 2026](https://texty.org.ua/fragments/117772/xto-zaselyv-dno-kaxovskoho-vodosxovyshcha-zooloh-opysav-tvaryn-novoho-lisu/)). The botanist Anna Kuzemko said ornithologists identified two species, the thrush and the common nightingale: "one nightingale or two is nice; hundreds is simply striking". TSN's crew heard them "mixed with the humming of front-line drones" ([TSN, 7 Jun 2026](https://tsn.ua/exclusive/rivno-try-roky-pislia-pidryvu-kakhovskoyi-hes-iak-vyhliadaye-unikalnyy-velykyy-luh-3100838.html)). Its English text calls the team's recording "a blend of hundreds of nightingales singing ... and the distant hum of drones" ([TSN English](https://tsn.ua/en/exclusive/kakhovka-hpp-blown-up-three-years-ago-what-unique-great-meadow-looks-like-now-3100838.html)). "Hundreds" is the scientists' word. Intent's headline says "thousands" ([Intent, 24 May 2026](https://intent.press/en/news/ecology/2026/thousands-of-nightingales-sing-at-the-bottom-of-the-kakhovka-reservoir-and-7-meter-trees-grow/)). A phrase seen in one search summary, "a single wall of noise", appears in no page the researchers read and must not be quoted. Upstream at Khortytsia, the Irish Times heard "small birds twitter among the young willows", with "occasional muffled booms of artillery fire" from the south ([Irish Times, 21 Nov 2025](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2025/11/21/to-flood-this-place-again-would-be-a-crime-ukraines-great-meadow-springs-back-to-life-after-destruction-of-dam)).

Everything else the film might want to hear is undocumented. No page read mentions frogs or toads calling on the bed. Marsh frogs, green toads and one common spadefoot appear in 30 iNaturalist photo records in the reach since the breach, and Vasyliuk photographed green toads inside the bed outline on 14 July 2026 ([381439040](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/381439040)). But no frog has been recorded there: the reach's only amphibian sound is Kormina's fire-bellied toad of April 2023, before the drainage. Insects are described, not heard. Many insects live on the trees and bushes ([Vgoru, 24 May 2026](https://vgoru.org/novini/cujes-prosto-sotni-solovyiv-shho-vidbuvajetsia-na-dni-kolisnyogo-kaxovskogo-vodosxovishha)), there are many spiders, and bees and butterflies are "practically absent" ([Texty](https://texty.org.ua/fragments/117772/xto-zaselyv-dno-kaxovskoho-vodosxovyshcha-zooloh-opysav-tvaryn-novoho-lisu/)). Twenty-six orthopteran species, including the great green bush-cricket, have been photographed in the reach, but none recorded ([iNaturalist species counts](https://api.inaturalist.org/v1/observations/species_counts?nelat=47.80&nelng=35.30&swlat=47.20&swlng=33.70&taxon_id=47651&d1=2023-06-06)). No published description of wind in the willows exists. The closest are a NovaPriroda short titled "the ears of grass sing" and a 2017 clip of wind off the full reservoir at Velyka Znamianka. Both are unheard and under a standard licence, and the 2017 one is also outside the collection's publication years. No recording of water on the bed, or of the full reservoir at the crossing before June 2023, was found.

Season and hour can be matched to dated recordings, not to a soundscape study. May is the one month whose bed sound is sourced. June and July keep orioles, cuckoos and reed warblers, while nightingales fall silent by July (a Moscow source, not Ukrainian: [mos.ru](https://www.mos.ru/news/item/74027073/), snippet). Late summer brings waders to wet ground, and winter is near silent, with thrushes and waxwings in town. Scops owls give the night a single repeated note from April to July: Shvets recorded one at 22:56 on 25 April 2026, Kormina one at 01:56 on 22 June 2026 ([374114900](https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/374114900)). The bed's water also changes by year, which matters for any wet-ground sound. The spring 2024 floods covered much of it, while in spring 2026 Maniuk said there was too little water in the plavni ([UNIAN](https://www.unian.ua/ecology/velikiy-lug-shcho-vidbuvayetsya-iz-teritoriyeyu-pislya-pidrivu-rosiyanami-kahovskoji-ges-13501719.html), snippet).

## The cormorant towers have no sound and no count since 2012

The one documented link between animals and these lines is mute in every archive. Sidorenko and Siokhin surveyed the Enerhodar crossings in 2012. They counted **1,240 great cormorant nests on six towers**: 320, 280, 200 and 100 on four towers of the 330 kV crossing, and 190 and 150 on two of the 750 kV crossing. Caspian gulls nested there too, and the towers near the right bank were not reached ([Sidorenko & Siokhin 2016, *Biosystems Diversity*](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/nesting-of-great-cormorants-phalacrocorax-carbo-on-man-made-structures-in-ukraine)). Most nests sat on horizontal members at 20–25 m or lower, below the conductors. Fishery inspectors said the birds had nested there every year for at least a decade, and a 1990 inspection found no droppings, so the colony probably postdates 1990. Nothing about it has been published since the drainage. The nearest claim is unattributed narration that exposed shores opened nests to predators and people, "so cormorants moved downstream en masse" ([Telegraf, 6 Oct 2026](https://news.telegraf.com.ua/ukr/obshhestvo/5951748-9-metrovi-dereva-i-naybilshi-u-evropi-zmii-shcho-vidbuvaetsya-na-dni-kakhovskoho-vodoskhovishcha)). It names no tower. Searches for 2023–2026 reports of birds nesting on the crossing towers returned nothing.

Nothing was recorded under or beside the Dniprovska or Ferosplavna-1 towers on the bed, by anyone. The nearest civilian clip after the breach is Chervonohryhorivka hromada's "dam No. 5" montage of 12 June – 14 July 2023 ([YouTube mQdj_rrfgCA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQdj_rrfgCA)). It is a 0:52 clip of unknown sound, filmed from about 9.4 km from the spans. On the south bank, the last shore recording is a fishing video from the Enerhodar shore uploaded on 14 May 2023, before the breach ([YouTube t1900jmlMVU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t1900jmlMVU)). Any recording at the towers would put someone in the border zone opposite the plant. Even if one surfaced, the safety rule would let it be placed only as a stretch of the crossing, never at a tower.

## Who holds more, and what to ask them

The best person to ask is the recordist whose files are already open. **Oleksii Vasyliuk** heads the board of the Ukrainian Nature Conservation Group. He can be reached through iNaturalist messages or UNCG's press centre and channels ([UNCG contacts](https://uncg.org.ua/en/contacts/); [iNaturalist vasyliuk1](https://www.inaturalist.org/people/vasyliuk1)). CC BY needs only credit, but an ask could bring longer raw takes from 13–14 May 2026, especially the night chorus, and their times of day. It could also settle whether TSN's nightingale-and-drone audio is UNCG's or TSN's, whether it has drone-free stretches, and whether the expedition planned for autumn 2026 recorded anything. **Anna Kuzemko** (Kholodny Institute of Botany; UNCG) was on the same trip ([TSN](https://tsn.ua/exclusive/rivno-try-roky-pislia-pidryvu-kakhovskoyi-hes-iak-vyhliadaye-unikalnyy-velykyy-luh-3100838.html)). **Ekaterina Kormina** ([iNaturalist uabird](https://www.inaturalist.org/people/uabird); xeno-canto) is the person to ask for permission beyond non-commercial use of her iNaturalist files, and for longer takes near the river: her XC1016032 note describes "many warblers near the river in the reeds". She is also the only recordist who might hold a frog chorus from the north bank. **Oksana Shvets** ([iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/people/shvets_oksana)) holds the Sholokhove recordings.

Broadcasters and reserves hold the location sound. **Suspilne Dnipro** (author Polina Palamarchuk, ecologist Vadym Maniuk, camera Daniil Nikolaienko) holds the raw field audio behind its bed films on the Nikopol bank ([Detector Media](https://stv.detector.media/suspilna-korporatsiya/read/9383/2026-05-27-na-suspilnomu-vidbudetsya-premiera-dokumentalnogo-filmu-velykyy-lug-nezvidana-zona/)). The **Khortytsia National Reserve** (Mykhailo Mulenko, head of nature protection) has a field team on the upper bed ([Byline Times](https://bylinetimes.com/2024/10/10/ukraine-kakhovka-disaster-dnipro-river/); [iNaturalist khortytsianpd](https://www.inaturalist.org/people/khortytsianpd)). **TSN/1+1** holds the May 2026 nightingale recording. The anonymous owner of **@NovaPriroda_UA** holds about 108 long, mostly wordless walks at Mar'ianske, filmed from June 2023 to June 2026. Two sound artists have worked from field recordings on the reservoir's upper and lower reaches, not the crossing. Valerie Karpan's *Hydraulic Border Intervals* (Berlin, 2026) was developed through research on Khortytsia ([museumsportal-berlin](https://www.museumsportal-berlin.de/en/events/valerie-karpan-hydraulic-border-intervals/), snippet). Zhanna Kadyrova's *The Forest* recorded the trees for a year, though whether it has sound was not established ([Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/01/23/8017585)). The authors of the 2012 cormorant survey were at a Melitopol institute, and Melitopol has been occupied since 2022. Their current affiliation is unknown.

## Recommendations: a montage that names its places

The film can use what exists honestly if it treats every real sound the way it treats a satellite pass: dated, sourced, placed where it was made, and never moved to where the film needs it. Eight rules follow from the evidence. Each is a proposal for the user to decide.

| # | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Keep XC1016141 as the base and add Kormina's other three CC0 files** (XC1016138, XC1016134, XC1016032). Credit them even though CC0 does not require it, for example: "Thrush nightingale · near Marhanets, north bank · 16 April 2025 · Ekaterina Kormina · xeno-canto XC1016141 · CC0 · recorded about 9 km from where the lines cross, not on the bed" | Nearest, after the drainage, free of conditions; the caption carries place, date and distance |
| R2 | **Use Vasyliuk's Mar'ianske chorus for "the forest the lines cross"**, labelled "recorded on the same drained bed, at Mar'ianske, about 53 km downstream · 14 May 2026 · Oleksii Vasyliuk · iNaturalist · CC BY". Exclude 361467377 and 361134120, listen to every other file at full gain with a spectrogram, and record each verdict | The only real sound from the bed; CC BY allows it; two files are flagged by their own recordist |
| R3 | **Match the birds to the record's date, and only with recordings made after 6 June 2023.** Nightingales and warblers for a May date, orioles and cuckoos in June and July, waders in late summer, near silence with a redwing or waxwing in winter, and a scops owl for night. Show each clip's own date beside the record's, and use Kormina's 2022 – May 2023 files only for a "before" moment, labelled so | The season is sourced through dated recordings; mixing a May chorus into a January date would invent a soundscape |
| R4 | **Keep the birds independent of the lines.** The biophony holds steady through every cut and complete loss while the synthesized Hum falls silent. Never swell or duck the birds with the lines' state | No source says the birds respond to the lines; the contrast is the point, and any coupling would imply a causation nobody observed |
| R5 | **Say plainly, in `film.json` `sound` and in the statement, that the birds and the Hum were never heard together.** Every recording was made beyond the ~1.4 km at which the lines could be heard at most, the Hum is synthesized, and the blend is a montage | Keeps "material recorded elsewhere is said to be" and "synthesized is said to be" in one sentence |
| R6 | **Add no frogs, singing insects, wind in the willows or water as "the bed's" sound.** If the user wants one, take a licensed recording from elsewhere and label it with its own country and date, or leave it out. Kormina's cicada (CC BY-NC) is the one real insect from the reach, near Marhanets, July 2024 | None has been recorded or described on the bed; the marsh frogs and bush-crickets are only photographed |
| R7 | **Screen and trace every file before it enters the mix.** Listen for drones (including starling mimicry), artillery, explosions, sirens, engines and voices. Keep the original download with its sha256 and source URL, as the pipeline does for frames. Never use TSN's nightingale-and-drone recording unless the user reverses the no-fighting rule | Drones are audible or noted in the bed's best-known recordings; war audio is easily swapped |
| R8 | **Protect the recordists and the site.** Draw Kormina's and Shvets's recordings only in binned cells, at least 1 km, and better about 3 km in keeping with the safety rule. Never call any spot a home, and never place a sound at a tower. Give the cormorant colony as dated text only ("2012: 1,240 cormorant nests on six of these towers · Sidorenko & Siokhin 2016 · no count since") with no calls added | One person's points round a frontline town can locate them; no recording or post-2012 count of the colony exists |

On licences, CC0 and CC BY are ready to use. CC BY-NC (Kormina's and Shvets's iNaturalist files, and the jackal) suits festival and academic screenings and needs the recordist's permission for anything else ([CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) for the NC terms). Romanyshyn's CC BY-NC-SA files are best avoided, because share-alike would reach into the film's soundtrack. Macaulay's assets carry the library's own terms, and standard-licence YouTube and Telegram material needs its rights-holder's permission. Pre-2022 uploads, such as the 2017 Velyka Znamianka wind clip or the 2020 CC BY shore video PvIIz69dqs0, would also need the user's decision under the collection's publication rule.

## Conclusion

The search turns the film's sound question into a map of access. The nearest real birdsong was recorded a few kilometres from where the lines run over land on the Ukrainian-held bank. The only sound from the bed itself comes from a strip 200 m deep, 53 km away. The 4.5 km of old water, now willow and channel, that the two lines actually span is acoustically blank: front line on one side, a mined shore under a 500 m ban on the other. That silence is a finding the film can state rather than fill. It also puts the Hum in a stronger position than expected. Nothing anyone recorded near the crossing could contain the lines' voice, so the synthesized Hum is not standing in for a recording the film failed to get. It is the only way that voice can be heard at all.

The practical consequence is that the real sound in Powerlines should be few clips, carefully named. One CC0 nightingale from Marhanets and a listened-through handful of Vasyliuk's Mar'ianske chorus, each with its place, date and distance, will hold up better than a lush, unattributed bed of "nature". The most valuable next step is a message to Vasyliuk: his longer May 2026 takes, a drone-free night chorus and any autumn recordings would give the film the bed's own sound with its provenance intact. Everything the archives lack, the frogs, the wind in the willows and the cormorants on the towers, should stay as text, or as silence the film says is silence.
