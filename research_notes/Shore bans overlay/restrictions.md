# Shore bans and civilian restrictions round the ZNPP, 24 Feb 2022 to 5 Nov 2026

Research notes for a map overlay (Powerlines). Compiled 5 November 2026. Every item gives who, when, what, the
extent *as the source gives it*, the URL, and whether it is first-hand (the order or the issuing body's own post)
or reported. "Snippet only" = seen only in a search-engine summary, page not opened. Wording: "occupied" for
Russian-held places; the ZNPP is in cold shutdown since 13 April 2024. No military positions, mines' or crews'
positions are given; extents only as the sources give them for civilians.

Tags: **[1st]** first-hand (issuer's post or order, opened); **[rep]** reported by media, page opened;
**[snip]** search snippet only; **[x]** page could not be opened.

## 1. The Enerhodar 500 m shore ban (occupied south bank)

### 1.1 What was verified

- **The bfm.ru item exists** (https://www.bfm.ru/news/555345), dated **1 August 2024, 22:56**, headline
  «В Энергодаре призывают не подходить к Каховскому водохранилищу из-за мин». It cites TASS, which cites the
  administration's official Telegram channel, and links the TASS original. **[rep]**
- **The TASS original was opened** (https://tass.ru/obschestvo/21510687; opened in a browser, WebFetch got an
  empty page): «В Энергодаре призвали жителей не подходить к Каховскому водохранилищу из-за мин», TASS website
  staff, **01 августа 2024, 15:21** (Moscow time), dateline МЕЛИТОПОЛЬ. **[rep]** Full text of the two key paragraphs:

  > МЕЛИТОПОЛЬ, 1 августа. /ТАСС/. Администрация Энергодара призвала жителей города не приближаться к
  > Каховскому водохранилищу и не пытаться перейти его в обмелевших местах из-за угрозы подрыва на мине. Об этом
  > говорится в официальном Telegram-канале городской администрации.
  >
  > "Участились случаи подрыва мирного населения с летальным исходом при попытках перейти по обмелевшим участкам
  > Каховского водохранилища на противопехотных минах, а также хаотически разбросанных в результате обмеления
  > водохранилища на всем побережье речных минах и других взрывоопасных предметах. В связи с этим администрация
  > <...> в очередной раз напоминает о запрете приближения к берегу Каховского водохранилища ближе 500 метров и
  > тем более попытках его перехода", - говорится в сообщении.

  Translation: "Enerhodar's administration urged the city's residents not to approach the Kakhovka reservoir and
  not to try to cross it at shallow places because of the threat of mines. This is said in the city
  administration's official Telegram channel. 'Cases of civilians being killed by mines have become more frequent
  when trying to cross the shallowed parts of the Kakhovka reservoir: anti-personnel mines, and also river mines and
  other explosive objects scattered chaotically along the whole shore as the reservoir shallowed. The
  administration <...> therefore once again reminds [residents] of the ban on approaching the shore of the Kakhovka
  reservoir closer than 500 metres, and all the more on attempts to cross it.'"

  TASS's third paragraph repeats the Russian claim that Ukraine struck the Kakhovka dam on 6 June 2023 (the
  Russian narrative; not for the film).

### 1.2 Answers to the questions

- **Who issued it:** TASS says "the administration" (администрация, with an ellipsis where its full name was cut:
  probably "Энергодарского городского округа"). **No official is named, and no decree, number or legal act.** (The
  current Russian-installed head, Maksim Pukhov, started in October 2024, after this post: Kommersant,
  https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/8248160 [snip].)
- **When:** the reminder is dated 1 August 2024. **It is a reminder of an existing ban** («в очередной раз
  напоминает», "once again reminds"): the ban was issued earlier, and **its original date was not found.**
  The earliest related item found: 2 November 2022, Aleksandr Volga (then the occupation-installed head) asked
  residents not to visit the shoreline and the forest because of unexploded ordnance and "Lepestok" mines: «хочу
  предупредить вас об опасности посещения береговой линии и леса» ("I want to warn you of the danger of visiting
  the shoreline and the forest"); a request, no distance (https://mkam.business-gazeta.ru/news/570034 [rep], citing
  t.me/AlexanderVolgaEnergodar/50, a channel that no longer exists [x]; regnum.ru/news/3740700 [x] 403).
- **The administration's own post was not found.** The channel is **t.me/ener_admin** ("Администрация
  Энергодарского городского округа", linked from the administration's site https://energodar.gosuslugi.ru/). Its
  public web preview was read for every post of 30 July–5 August 2024 (ids 7178–7266): none is about the
  reservoir. Telegram search of the channel for «подрыва мирного населения», «Каховского водохранилища»,
  «берегу», «прибрежной», «500 метров» finds no such post. Either it was deleted, or TASS quoted another channel
  (the administration also has VK https://vk.com/energodar_adm and OK, not checked: VK needs a login). **So the
  quote rests on TASS alone.**
- **What "the shore" means:** the source says only «берег Каховского водохранилища», "the shore of the Kakhovka
  reservoir". It does not say whether it means the 2023+ water edge, the old (pre-breach) shoreline, or the cooling
  pond (the cooling pond is not mentioned at all). Read with "попытки перейти по обмелевшим участкам" (attempts to
  cross the shallowed parts) and "на всем побережье" (along the whole coast), the text treats the drained area as
  the reservoir and warns against walking across it. **Undefined in the source: do not draw it as a precise
  500 m buffer of any one line without saying which line we chose.**
- **Area covered:** not stated. The issuer is the Enerhodar urban okrug administration, so at most its territory
  (in the Russian-installed structure, the city of Enerhodar plus its okrug). **Kamianka-Dniprovska is a separate
  municipal okrug and is not mentioned.**
- **Penalties:** none stated.
- **Still in force?** Unknown. No later reminder, and no lifting, was found (searches of t.me/ener_admin, Pukhov's
  t.me/Pukhov_M and Balitsky's t.me/BalitskyEV). Treat it as "reported in force on 1 August 2024".
- **Independent / Ukrainian reporting of the 500 m ban:** none found (searches in Ukrainian, Russian and English;
  Ukrainska Pravda, Suspilne, RIA Melitopol, Meduza, Mediazona, IAEA). The Meduza/Reuters feature on Enerhodar
  (30 Aug 2025, https://meduza.io/feature/2025/08/30/gorod-prizrak-upravlyaemyy-nasiliem-i-strahom) does not
  mention it. The IAEA has not mentioned it in anything found.
- **The mine incidents behind it:** the post gives **no dates and no number**, only "cases ... have become more
  frequent". No report of a specific civilian killed crossing the drained bed near Enerhodar in 2024 was found in
  Russian or Ukrainian media. Related, not the same: Dmytro Orlov (Ukraine's mayor of Enerhodar, in exile) said on
  13 April 2023 that «Замінована була виключно територія узбережжя, Каховського водосховища та територія по
  периметру Запорізької атомної станції» ("only the coast, the Kakhovka reservoir and the ZNPP perimeter had been
  mined", later fields and forest belts too) (https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/news-minuvannia-enerhodar/32361902.html
  [rep]); the IAEA saw mines on 23 July 2023 in the buffer zone between the site's inner and outer perimeter
  barriers (Grossi's statement of 24 July 2023, reported e.g. https://www.nucnet.org/news/iaea-confirms-mines-in-close-proximity-to-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-station-7-2-2023 [snip]): the plant site, not civilians.
  Pukhov reported 6 people wounded on 3 August 2026 by drone-dropped mines on roads into the city
  (https://www.interfax.ru/russia/1107163 [rep]): roads, not the shore.
- **Confidence:** that the administration (as quoted by TASS) referred to a ban on coming within 500 m of the
  reservoir shore on 1 August 2024: **high** (TASS opened; bfm.ru agrees). The ban's issue date, legal form,
  exact area and present status: **unknown**.

### 1.3 Related, found while checking (occupied Zaporizhzhia oblast)

  **18 January 2026, 15:20** **[1st]** (forwarded to t.me/ener_admin/13588 on 19 January 2026): «В городе Токмаке, а
  также в храмах, находящихся на расстоянии менее 15 км от линии боевого соприкосновения либо от береговой линии
  Каховского водохранилища, праздничные церковные службы будут проводиться без прихожан» ("in Tokmak, and in
  churches less than 15 km from the line of contact or from the shoreline of the Kakhovka reservoir, the festive
  services will be held without parishioners"). A one-day measure on gatherings, not a movement ban, but it is the
  occupation authority's own use of a 15 km band from the Kakhovka shoreline. Again "the shoreline" is undefined.
- **Seasonal fishing restrictions, whole oblast.** t.me/ener_admin/11217, 15 April 2025 **[1st]** (an
  information post): from 1 April 2025 amateur fishing restricted and commercial fishing banned; «Ловля рыбы во всех
  реках и водоёмах области» banned except from the shore with one rod; «Использование плавсредств» (use of boats)
  banned. No end date or legal act given (this is the usual spawning-season rule; not a security zone).
- See section 2 for curfews, passes and the May 2023 "relocation".

## 5. Caveats for the overlay

1. The Enerhodar 500 m ban is known only from TASS (and bfm.ru copying it). The administration's own post is not
   in its channel today. Credit it as "Enerhodar occupation administration, as reported by TASS, 1 August 2024",
   and say it was a reminder of an earlier ban whose date is not known.
2. "The shore" is undefined in every source (Enerhodar 500 m, Balitsky 15 km). After June 2023 the water edge moved
   kilometres; any drawn buffer needs the line it is measured from chosen and said (e.g. the pre-breach reservoir
   shoreline, or the water edge on a dated pass). Drawing it from a line of our own choosing is an interpretation.
3. Area covered by the Enerhodar ban is not stated: at most the Enerhodar okrug; nothing for Kamianka-Dniprovska.
4. Mine incidents: no dates or numbers anywhere; do not caption a count.
5. Russian sources (TASS, Balitsky) carry their own narrative (e.g. TASS on the dam); quote the restriction only.
6. Sections 1 and 1.3 were checked first-hand in this session (TASS page, Telegram posts opened). Sections 2 and 3
   come from two parallel searches; their quotes are as those searches reported them and should be re-opened
   before any goes on screen (especially the 2026 items: Hanzha's evacuation orders, Bazyliuk's appeal).
7. Comparable extents for the map: occupied side, "500 m from the reservoir shore" (Enerhodar, reported) and "15 km
   from the Kakhovka shoreline" for gatherings (Balitsky, first-hand); Ukrainian side, "the water surface and the
   shoreline strip" (Nikopol, order № 34, no width) and named villages and streets for evacuation. Nothing on either
   bank defines its shore line.
