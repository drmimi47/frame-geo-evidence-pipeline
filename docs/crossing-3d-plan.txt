======================================================================================================
ARCHITECTURE: RENDER ONCE, SET THE TIMING AFTERWARDS
======================================================================================================
Blender renders passes; a Python compositor applies the record, so pacing, record and captions change without a new
Blender render.
- satellite/scene3d/export.py (our Python): a scene.json spec and assets in the zoom-out's plane: FABDEM heights (reuse
  the fetch in scripts/scene/terrain.py; true to size, never exaggerated), the Sentinel-2 textures with the 2D film's
  crops and gain (imagery/crossing for 2025, a new crossing_2021 crop), the OSM lines (network.json) and tower
  positions, water levels by date, the camera path.
- scene3d/build.py (runs inside Blender): builds the scene; renders per frame a beauty pass (terrain, water, towers,
  unlit wires), an emission mask per line (AOV or view layer: Dniprovska, Ferosplavna-1, Ferosplavna-2, the others'
  stubs near the plant) and depth; exports camera matrices per frame (camera.json).
- scene3d/compose.py: per frame, each line's glow from Record.states in the 2D film's STATE_RGB / STATE_W / STATE_GLOW
  and fades (on and off read the same in both), power-direction arrows along lit wires, the top-left date, count,
  legend and captions, labels projected from OSM coordinates through camera.json (reuse zoomout._Overlay, _blend,
  record.Record).
- Sound: hum.score gets per-voice pan curves (arrays, not constants): each line's pan follows its screen x from
  camera.json, a little level for distance. One score for the whole merged film, so the Hum is continuous across the cut.
- The match cut: the 2D film's last frame (zoomout._view(i): half-width, centre) is the 3D sequence's first: a
  perspective camera with a very long lens, high up (near-orthographic), on the same textures; then it dollies in while
  the lens shortens (the ground width kept) and tilts. Checked by the pixel difference of the two frames.
- zoomout gets a `handoff` option: after the zone's close hold the camera eases to the handoff framing (the plant and
  both lines leaving north) and the 2D render stops there; the pull-back with the pulse, the dissolve and the 2D push in
  are replaced by the 3D sequence.
- Output: satellite/networks/znpp/scenes/<id>/ (scene.json, renders, film.mp4, film.json: camera per second, sources,
  statements: modelled, derived, synthesized). CLI: `evidence satellite crossing3d --pass animatic|timing|final`.
  Config: a `crossing3d:` block under `networks: znpp` in config/conductive-seam.yaml.
