# Eighteen small sites mirror the hostage plant

The strongest microcosms of the occupied Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant (ZNPP) are not far-off analogues. They sit within about 15 km of the two crossing lines. They are: **the plant's own cooling pond**, a reservoir inside the drained reservoir, now fed by about eleven boreholes and partly re-dammed where inspectors may not go; **the far bank at Nikopol**, from which the Grad launches beside the reactors were filmed in September 2022 and geolocated by the thermal plant's chimneys, and where a pensioner watches the six domes from his ninth-floor balcony; and **Pokrovske**, where a Cossack church foundation and a drowned cemetery came out of the water within days of the dam's destruction. A fourth candidate, the **"ceasefire for one span"** (seven IAEA-brokered local truces between October 2025 and September 2026, each to repair a line that is then cut again), is the war's arithmetic compressed into about 36 hours. It belongs inside the film's existing record of cuts and reconnections rather than in a sequence of its own. Eighteen near-site candidates are ranked below, together with regional analogues for context. For almost all of them the researchers confirmed **no orbiting footage suitable for a Gaussian splat**. The best chances are Ukrenergo's 2015 aerial film of the crossing towers, the burned cooling tower filmed from several sides, beached Cossack dugout boats at Khortytsia, and pre-war footage of DniproHES. The student's proposed site on the north bank near Marhanets is a zig-zag fire trench with two U-shaped dug-in emplacements. Esri's dated archive shows them **absent on 1 October 2021 and present, freshly dug, on 29 August 2022**, and a fire burned the steppe beside them **between 16 and 18 August 2024** (Sentinel-2). They lie a few hundred metres from where the last lines come ashore, on the bank where the IAEA "preliminarily assessed" Ferosplavna-1's August 2026 damage to be. No source ties the earthworks to the lines. As a microcosm the site is strong, but it can be shown only as dated satellite stills, and publishing it precisely could put people in danger.

## How the candidates were weighed, and how far to trust them

Seven researchers worked separately on seven areas: the plant and Enerhodar; Nikopol and Marhanets; the drained bed; the lines, repairs and water; domestic and building scale; forensic precedents; and the student's trench site. Several found the same cases, and those are merged here into single entries. Three facts in the original brief were corrected independently by more than one researcher. The cooling-tower fire was on **11 August 2024, not 2025** ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/cooling-tower-at-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-requires-demolition-after-fire-iaea-chief-says/); [Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/news/2024/08/12/moscow-and-kyiv-blame-each-other-after-fire-breaks-out-at-russian-occupied-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant)). The April 2024 strike was a drone on the **Unit 6** reactor dome on **7 April 2024** ([World Nuclear News](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/grossi-condemns-drone-strikes-at-zaporizhzhia-site)). The 4 March 2022 security camera was on the **main administrative building**, which NPR established by matching buildings seen through its window, and its feed was livestreamed on the plant's own YouTube channel ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2022/03/11/1085427380/ukraine-nuclear-power-plant-zaporizhzhia)). A Euronews item of 12 August 2025, "Fire detected near Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant", also exists but was not read, so a separate 2025 fire is unconfirmed ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2025/08/12/fire-detected-near-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant-in-ukraine-energy-ministry-says)).

The evidence comes in four grades, and the report says which applies wherever it matters. **Primary data checked by the researchers themselves** is the most solid: Esri's imagery metadata and Sentinel-2 burn ratios for the trench site, and YouTube metadata (uploader, date, length, views) read with yt-dlp on 29 October 2026 for the drained-bed videos. **Pages read in full** come next. Then come **claims known only from search-result summaries**, because many IAEA pages and mirrors returned HTTP 402/403, as did the Truth Hounds reports; CNN's July 2026 Nikopol piece returned 451 and NPR's August 2022 feature timed out. Last are **advocacy-commissioned or partisan sources**: Greenpeace/McKenzie Intelligence Services, Energoatom's and HUR's claims, and Russian state media. Telegram, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook could not be searched, and the researchers agree that this is where most raw local footage lives.

Each candidate was rated on four axes: how closely it mirrors the plant held hostage (**mirror**), what public media exists (**media**), whether the project's own tools can produce evidence from it (**workability**), and the danger to people of showing it (**risk**). The scope rule also matters in practice. Only YouTube videos published 2022–2026 that pass the Ukraine gate may enter the collection. Several of the best "before" sources were uploaded earlier: Ukrenergo's 2015 aerial and a 2019 clip of the Kamianka–Nikopol crossing. TikTok and Telegram are outside the pipeline altogether. Using any of them needs a decision with the user, not a workaround.

## Three candidates carry the plant's whole story at small scale

### 1. The cooling pond: a reservoir held inside the drained reservoir

Four researchers reached the cooling pond independently, which says something about how central it is. When the Kakhovka dam was destroyed on 6 June 2023, the ZNPP lost its reservoir intake. Its survival came down to its own cooling pond, its twelve sprinkler ponds, and groundwater wells drilled beside them. The first well came in **August 2023 at about 20 m³/h** ([WNN](https://world-nuclear-news.org/Articles/Groundwater-extraction-begins-at-Zaporizhzhia)). Ten wells later gave over 200 m³/h, with an eleventh planned ([NEI Magazine](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/more-wells-drilled-at-zaporizhia-npp-11160730/)). By early September 2025 the IAEA reported the pond **down more than 3.2 m since the dam's destruction**, nearly 60 cm in three months. A dam had been built to isolate one channel, fed by mobile pumps, to supply the main transformers, and the IAEA was refused access to it ([Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1102185.html); [IAEA via GlobalSecurity](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2025/08/ukraine-250828-iaea01.htm), both via search summaries). Greenpeace Ukraine's commissioned analysts, McKenzie Intelligence Services, measured the pond shrinking from **about 9.6 to about 8.66 million m² between July 2022 and August 2025**. They also counted more than 1,000 m of trenches on its north and west shores by June 2024 ([Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/news/5438/new-level-of-militarization/); [MIS update, Feb 2026](https://www.greenpeace.org/static/planet4-ukraine-stateless/2026/02/2c4d05a4-Оновлений-Маккензі-eng.pdf)). Skhemy (RFE/RL) had already shown the tributary feeding the pond shrinking on Planet imagery of 9 June 2023 ([Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/95015-skhemy-project-showed-satellite-images-of-the-tributary-to-the-cooling-pond-of-the-znpp)). Russia's own circulars to the IAEA give the pond's level as 15.4 m in May 2024 and 15.06 m in July 2024. These are operator figures, read from search snippets ([INFCIRC/1227](https://iaea.org/sites/default/files/publications/documents/infcircs/2024/infcirc1227_rus.pdf)).

The pond has a creature and a human thread. After the cold shutdown the pond cooled to +13 °C and fish died in large numbers ([Ukrainska Pravda, 3 Nov 2022](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/11/03/7374802/), a single Ukrainian report known only from a search summary, never confirmed by the IAEA). The plant's diver **Andriy Honcharuk** was beaten after refusing an order to dive into the sprinkler pond, and died on 3 July 2022 ([LB.ua](https://lb.ua/society/2022/07/03/522020_pomer_vodolaz_zaporizkoi_aes_yakogo.html); [Radio Svoboda](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/news-enerhodar-pomer-vodolaz-aes/31927792.html)). Texty's account puts the fatal torture on 29 June and describes him as forced into the cooling water, so its dates and details differ from these reports ([Texty](https://texty.org.ua/projects/109061/nuclear-terror-how-russia-took-nuclear-plant-and-people-enerhodar-hostage/)).

The mirror is exact. The plant's survival hangs on a lifeline someone else's war has cut, and the occupier re-engineers it behind a fence where inspectors may not look. The ethical risk is low because everything is remote sensing. Above all, the student can **produce new evidence rather than quote it**. The pipeline's waterline method can measure the pond's shoreline on every clear Sentinel-2 pass from 2022 to 2026 and check it against MIS's figures. The ponds are plainly visible at 10 m. The wells and the trenches are not, because MIS used very-high-resolution imagery. A splat is impossible: there is no access and no footage. Verification: the pond's decline is IAEA-reported, the area figures are advocacy-commissioned, and the student's own time series would be the independent check.

### 2. The far bank: Grad launches beside the reactors, filmed from where the lines land

Three researchers found the same video. On the night of 2–3 September 2022 a camera on the Nikopol bank filmed Russian multiple rocket launchers firing near a ZNPP power unit. The Conflict Intelligence Team (CIT) matched the **Zaporizhzhia thermal plant's chimneys** in the frame to a Google panorama. That placed the camera on the opposite (right) bank, pointing south, with the launch site west of the thermal plant, where the nuclear plant is. CIT said it could not tell whether the launchers were inside the plant's fence ([The Insider, 3 Sep 2022](https://theins.ru/news/254676); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/09/04/7366003/)). The Centre for Information Resilience's "Danger Zone" (7 September 2022) placed the same launch area between the nuclear and thermal plants, rebutting the pro-Russian channel Rybar. It also published coordinates for a convoy entering near reactor 2 (coordinates in CIR's report), equipment under reactor 5's cooling pipes (coordinates in CIR's report), a fighting position at the plant entrance (coordinates in CIR's report), and shelling near Enerhodar's Shevchenko monument traced by crater analysis to the Russian-held side (coordinates in CIR's report) ([CIR](https://info-res.org/post/danger-zone-how-russia-is-risking-nuclear-disaster-at-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant), read in full). Energoatom claimed in July and December 2022 that launchers stood on the grounds and near Unit 6 by the spent-fuel store ([Focus.ua](https://focus.ua/uk/voennye-novosti/522350-vs-rf-ustanovili-na-zaporozhskoy-aes-raketnye-kompleksy-i-obstrelivayut-nikopol-video); [The Insider, 8 Dec 2022](https://theins.press/en/news/257683)). These are the operator's claims. CNN separately verified video of at least five Z-marked trucks at the far western end of the **Unit 1 turbine hall, about 130 m from the reactor** ([CNN, 19 Aug 2022](https://www.cnn.com/2022/08/19/europe/ukraine-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-russian-vehicles-intl-hnk/index.html)). The IAEA later confirmed trucks in the turbine halls of Units 1 and 2.

This is the hostage story's central mechanism, the plant used as a shield ("they know there will be no return fire," as Ukrainian officials put it: [Ukrinform](https://ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/3604321-russians-shelling-nikopol-district-from-nuclear-power-plant-premises.html)), seen from the very bank where Dniprovska and Ferosplavna-1 come ashore. It is also forensic method in its plainest form: the thermal plant from which Ferosplavna-1 leaves is the landmark that located the weapons fire. The student can re-solve CIT's bearing against the project's georeferenced passes and pin CIR's four coordinates the way the film's inset is pinned to OSM towers. The video comes from a fixed camera, so it can give stills and a sightline, not a splat. The original upload (Telegram or YouTube) was not found; The Insider embedded the video on its own page. Risk is medium: never reveal a filmer's position. Verification: geolocated by two independent groups; the fence question stays open and must be said on screen.

### 3. Pokrovske: what one energy project drowned, the war on another laid bare

Within days of the breach, the water near **Pokrovske** (Nikopol district, right bank) fell back up to 500 m. It uncovered the foundation of a stone church and a cemetery with open graves and human remains, beside munitions that residents were told to avoid ([TSN, 9 Jun 2023](https://tsn.ua/exclusive/z-kahovskogo-vodoshovischa-voda-pishla-na-500-metriv-u-navkolishnih-selah-visihayut-krinici-2347003.html)). Pokrovske stands on the site of the last Zaporozhian Sich (Nova Sich, 1734–1775) and was partly washed away in the 1950s ([Vikna, 19 May 2024](https://vikna.tv/dlia-tebe/kahovske-vodoshovyshhe-znahidky-istoriyi-na-dni-kolyshnoyi-vodojmy/)). Radio Svoboda reported metal detectorists active there and no named researchers ([Radio Svoboda, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/pidryv-kakhovskoyi-hes-povernennya-velykoho-lukhu-arkheolohichni-skarby/32487603.html)). The main moving image, read directly with yt-dlp, is "На дні Каховського водосховища ЗНАЙШЛИ ЛЮДСЬКІ РЕШТКИ" by **Телеканал Конкурент Україна, YouTube, 12 June 2023, 0:55, about 114,000 views**. Its description credits local Telegram channels ([YouTube xjWRL3dKlp8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjWRL3dKlp8)). Ukrainska Pravda Life ran it as the church "that Shevchenko drew" ([UP Life, 15 Jun 2023](https://life.pravda.com.ua/culture/2023/06/15/254860/), headline only, 403). Sources disagree on whether it was a Sich church or built after the Sich was abolished ([OrthoChristian](https://orthochristian.com/154290.html); [Focus](https://focus.ua/uk/ukraine/572947-na-dni-kahovskogo-vodoshovishcha-znayshli-starovinnu-kozacku-cerkvu-shcho-pro-neji-vidomo-video)), and that dispute must be settled before any caption.

The mirror works from a different angle than the plant's. A Soviet energy project, the Kakhovka hydroelectric station, flooded about 90 villages and resettled about 37,000 people ([UWEC/UNCG](https://uwecworkgroup.info/is-it-time-to-restore-velykyi-luh/)). The war's destruction of that same infrastructure then exposed the dead it had covered. It belongs to the Great Meadow chapter and needs no access. It can be geolocated by matching the church footprint against the project's 1941 sheet and the June 2023 Sentinel-2 passes. Pokrovske lies west of Nikopol and may fall outside the great-meadow corridor's box, whose west edge is 34.26° E, but the zoom-out's 60 m layer covers it. A splat is unlikely from short handheld clips. A ruined foundation would be a good subject if longer orbiting Telegram footage turns up. The ethical weight is high. Do not show remains gratuitously, and keep coordinates coarse in public, because publicity draws looters. Verification: the finds are reported by several outlets; the church's identity is disputed; the original Telegram posts were not identified.

## The rest of the field divides into supporting scenes and context

### 4. The ceasefire for one span

Since October 2025 every reconnection has needed a negotiated local truce. The sequence runs: Grossi negotiates; deminers clear the ground; crews work in a zone of about 1.5 km radius while IAEA teams watch from both sides; the line is reconnected; and later it is cut again ([Al Jazeera, 18 Oct 2025](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/18/zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-repairs-begin-in-ukraine-as-ceasefire-zones-set)). The cleanest single case is Ferosplavna-1 in November 2025. The section **"about three kilometres from the site perimeter"** was demined on 7 November and repaired on the morning of the 8th, and the line was reconnected at **19:43** local time, the plant's first backup line in about six months ([Ukrainska Pravda, 7 Nov 2025](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/11/07/8006360); IAEA Update 327 via a [GlobalSecurity mirror](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2025/11/ukraine-251108-iaea01.htm), snippet only; Interfax-Ukraine gives 10 November, perhaps a later reconnection). Two further cuts fall on the north bank. In the night of 2–3 June 2026, Energoatom said a drone launched from the opposite bank hit the **"Nikopolska" substation**, blacking out the plant ([Focus.ua](https://focus.ua/uk/voennye-novosti/756416-situaciya-na-zaes-rosiyani-obirvali-liniyu-ferosplavna-1-z-nikopolya-detali)). The project's OSM trace ends Ferosplavna-1 at the Ferosplavna substation, so that needs checking. On 20 August 2026 the line was cut "after military activity on the northern side", and the seventh truce followed with **"little more than a week" of diesel** left ([WNN, 7 Sep 2026](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/local-ceasefire-in-place-for-zaporizhzhia-power-line-repairs)). The June 2026 repairs involved the **"high pylons across the Dnipro"** ([WNN, 26 Jun 2026](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/substation-damage-stopping-zaporizhzhia-power-line-reconnection-says-iaea)). WNN counted 19 complete losses of off-site power in late June 2026 and 26 by September, "the 10th since June", which the record's own count should be reconciled against. No photo or video of crews at work was found. The mirror is perfect, but this is the film's own subject, so it works best as a held moment inside the existing record rather than as a separate microcosm. Never mark which towers were repaired.

### 5. The ninth-floor balcony and the Nikopol sightlines

Two researchers found the same witness. **Mykhailo Kling, 72**, runs to his panoramic ninth-floor balcony to watch launches, with "the eerie shapes of the Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant reactors jutting out from the opposite bank". He says the Russians sometimes fire "from behind them". His wife, Lyubov Harmash, has a mother and sister in occupied Vodiane (text and photos by Francis Farrell, [Kyiv Independent, 25 Aug 2022](https://kyivindependent.com/life-on-the-front-line-of-russias-new-nuclear-brinkmanship/), read in full). Other sightlines are on record. NPR's Tamara Korolkova, 70, has watched the plant from her flat for decades ([NPR, 16 Aug 2022](https://www.npr.org/2022/08/16/1117511626/ukraine-russia-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-town-fears-meltdown), summary only). ABC's "Nadezhda" sees it from the end of her street, and ABC photographed it from a bus stop and saw "electricity poles on the horizon" across the dry bed ([ABC, 16 Jul 2023](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-07-16/ukraine-war-nikopol-residents-without-water-fear-nuclear-risk/102596430)). Radio Svoboda published a March 2024 photo of the plant and Enerhodar from Nikopol ([Radio Svoboda, 30 Apr 2024](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/novyny-pryazovya-nikopol-bez-kakhovskoho-vodoskhovyshcha-viyna/32925034.html)). The forensic form here is a **viewshed from a single window**, about 11–12 km across water and then sand, rebuilt from satellite imagery and OSM building heights, and checked against the photographs by matching the skyline of the six domes. These are private elderly people, so work only from the published photographs with credit, never identify the flat, and treat any portrait as needing consent.

### 6. The night of 3–4 March 2022 on the plant's own camera

NPR, with CIR's Leone Hadavi and the physicists Edwin Lyman and Tom Bielefeld, analysed **4 h 21 min** of the administrative building's camera feed, livestreamed from shortly before 23:30. A tank column came from the south-east; about 1 h 20 min in, a lead tank was disabled; a two-hour firefight followed; firefighters arriving about 02:50 were turned back; and a transmission line to the main substation was hit. The IAEA said two of the four high-voltage lines were damaged ([WYPR/NPR](https://www.wypr.org/2022-03-11/video-analysis-reveals-russian-attack-on-ukrainian-nuclear-plant-veered-near-disaster); footage [YouTube fYUT36YGOh8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYUT36YGOh8), whose uploader and online status were not checked). Overt Defense matched the burning building to the training centre outside the reactor area ([Overt Defense](https://www.overtdefense.com/2022/03/03/zaporizhzhia-pitched-battle-at-europes-largest-nuclear-power-plant)). For this film it is the **first cut on camera**, the record's opening event filmed by the plant itself. A fixed camera gives a timeline and sightline, not a splat. Risk is low, and the analysis is well established, so credit NPR and CIR and do not redo them.

### 7. Plant staff held hostage: the diver, the director's road, the police building

This is the literal hostage microcosm. Truth Hounds and Greenpeace's *Seizing Power* documents **226 unlawful detentions** of Enerhodar residents and ZNPP staff between March 2022 and June 2025, at **at least seven sites** ([Greenpeace Ukraine](https://www.greenpeace.org/ukraine/en/news/4290/rosatoms-complicity-in-war-crimes-and-nuclear-risks-at-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power-plant-revealed/)). According to the Kyiv Independent, 78 of those detained were plant staff ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russias-rosatom-complicit-in-war-crimes-report-alleges-says-treatment-of-power-plant-staff-risks-nuclear-disaster/)). The mayor's estimate of about 1,000 is higher. The one building with an address, a dated event and before-and-after photographs is **Budivelnykiv Street 17**, the former police building, hit by an explosion on 18 August 2023 ([Obozrevatel](https://war.obozrevatel.com/ukr/v-energodari-stavsya-vibuh-u-budivli-de-okupanty-organizuvali-kativnyu-foto.htm)). Its cell measured **3.5 × 3.5 m for up to 24 people**, according to Volodymyr Zhaivoronok, held there 53 days ([Radio Svoboda, 18 Apr 2025](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/novyny-pryazovya-katuvannya-smert-v-poloni-enerhodar-zaes-zhavoronok/33388602.html)). That is a dimensioned room an architecture student could draw, labelled as reconstructed from testimony. The director-general **Ihor Murashov** was taken blindfolded from his car at about 16:00 on 30 September 2022 "on his way from the station to Enerhodar" ([Ukrainska Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/10/1/7369926/)). That makes the commute road a mappable object, the plant's head cut off from its town, though the exact spot is unpublished. Where Honcharuk was held is disputed: the police building according to Zhaivoronok, "the Pit" in a former National Guard base according to a Ukrainian retelling of a WSJ investigation ([Nikopol News](https://nikopol.nikopolnews.net/nikopol/zvirstva-okupantiv-na-zaes/)). Risk is very high, since many relatives remain under occupation. Blur faces, never place private homes, and follow the publishers' framing of testimony.

### 8. The crossing towers and their cormorants

The two crossings are the 330 kV line from the thermal plant (1977) and the 750 kV line from the nuclear plant (1984). Their towers stand up to **126 m** on caissons floated out and sunk in the reservoir, and they now stand on drained bed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enerhodar_Dnipro_Powerline_Crossing); [ru.wikipedia](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%8B_%D0%9B%D0%AD%D0%9F_%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B7_%D0%9A%D0%B0%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B5_%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%BE%D1%85%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%89%D0%B5)). The tower counts and heights conflict between sources. A 2012 survey found great cormorants nesting on four towers of the 330 kV crossing (**up to 320 nests on one**) and two of the 750 kV crossing ([Sidorenko & Siokhin 2016](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/nesting-of-great-cormorants-phalacrocorax-carbo-on-man-made-structures-in-ukraine)): life living on the lines that cool the reactors, stranded in the air when the water fell. Whether the colonies survived after 2023 is unknown; guano whitening on the caissons in high-resolution imagery might tell. The best aerial footage found of any candidate is **NPC Ukrenergo, "Краса електроенергетики. Опори, ЛЕП та перехід через Каховське водосховище", YouTube, 11 September 2015, 8:38**, a bird's-eye film of the towers standing in water ([YouTube yd6nkeCuomc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yd6nkeCuomc), metadata read directly). It could support a splat of the steel above the water and serves as a "before" reference for the Blender towers. It was uploaded before 2022, so ingesting it needs the user's decision. This candidate largely overlaps the main film rather than mirroring it. No post-2023 footage of the towers in the bed was found. Tower positions are already public, but never mark damaged ones.

### 9. The Kamianka–Nikopol crossing: the historic line across the same water

At the narrows opposite and just east of the plant, a crossing has been used since the 17th century and was controlled by the Mykytynska Sich. In 1943 the Germans built a bridge here, of which one pier remains, and a ferry ran after it. **Orliv island**, submerged in 1954, has been resurfacing since 2023 (description of [YouTube Ujuo7po7An0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ujuo7po7An0), Майя П`ятьКоней, uploaded 1 June 2019, 2:55, filmed pre-war; [Vikna](https://vikna.tv/dlia-tebe/kahovske-vodoshovyshhe-znahidky-istoriyi-na-dni-kolyshnoyi-vodojmy/)). Bank erosion at Mykytyn Rih has exposed old graves. It is the closest historical counterpart to the power lines (Cossack crossing, occupation bridge, ferry, pylons, no-man's-land), and it needs no access. The island's emergence can be shown directly in the project's own Sentinel-2 passes, which would verify the secondary claim. A related clip, "Запорожска АЭС и обмелевшее Каховское водохранилище вид с Никополя" (Denis Vedernikov, 14 June 2023, 0:23, "from Telegram channels"), shows the plant over the shallows ([YouTube VYvKO6WzexU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VYvKO6WzexU)). The ZNPP itself stands on the levelled Ivanivski kuchugury dunes of the old meadow shore ([Vikna](https://vikna.tv/dlia-tebe/kahovske-vodoshovyshhe-znahidky-istoriyi-na-dni-kolyshnoyi-vodojmy/)), which makes a quiet opening fact.

### 10. Nikopol's water, seen from one window

After 6 June 2023 the drained reservoir cut tap water for about 200,000 people in the Nikopol district ([CSMonitor](https://csmonitor.com/World/Europe/2023/0816/Mud-and-thirst-Two-Ukraine-cities-cope-with-dam-s-destruction)). The city and the plant had drunk from the same reservoir and lost it on the same day. France 24 Observers gathered the residents' record ([France 24, 9 Aug 2023](https://observers.france24.com/en/europe/20230809-authorities-and-residents-reel-from-dramatic-water-loss-after-ukraine-dam-attack), read in full): the journalist **Arthur Afonin** ("Nikopol City") photographed the water falling from his home, **8–25 June 2023**; TikToks show the former banks with the ZNPP in the distance ([@nastya_perevertailo, 22 Jul 2023](https://www.tiktok.com/@nastya_perevertailo/video/7258732712108887302)), dead fish ([@innadotsenko39](http://www.tiktok.com/@innadotsenko39/video/7242637052955692293)), and a former firefighter clearing explosives from the bed ([@alexandrkorobeynikov](https://www.tiktok.com/@alexandrkorobeynikov/video/7260164800443976965)). On YouTube, Tasha Katina's "Нікополь. Берег стрімко оновлюється." (9 June 2023, 1:22) is a handheld clip of the receding shore ([YouTube GrneVoioNkQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GrneVoioNkQ)). The replacement was a single mappable line: the **40.9 km Marhanets–Nikopol–Pokrov main**, with all mains reported running by August 2024 ([KMU](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/likvidatsiia-naslidkiv-pidryvu-kakhovskoi-hes-zapratsiuvala-persha-nytka-vodohonu-na-dnipropetrovshchyni)). Afonin's dated stills fit the film's dated-pass logic and can be cross-dated against Sentinel-2. The TikToks are unverified until downloaded, and they sit outside the YouTube pipeline.

### 11. The "Chance for Life" shelter: three hundred dogs on one water source

The domestic-scale researcher rated this the most precise creature-scale mirror. Olena Voshun's Nikopol shelter holds **about 300 dogs in 130 enclosures**, beside the city's sewage treatment works, with no centralised water. After the breach it lived on its basin reserves, needing about 300 L a day, then on deliveries and rainwater, and later on the city's new link to the old riverbed. Shelling debris has fallen on the grounds, and her home has been hit four times ([LB.ua, 21 Oct 2023](https://lb.ua/society/2023/10/21/580380_ostriv_sobak_dekotrim_lyudyam.html), read in full). The sequence of reserves, then outside supply, then a new connection matches the plant's diesel days, which is why it mirrors so closely. Being "next to the sewage works" makes it placeable on OSM. Voshun publicises her work on Facebook, so consent is tractable, and a walkaround video of the enclosures, if one exists, is a possible splat source (not checked). A Suspilne report says a drone destroyed the house she was fitting out for sick dogs; its year is not shown and the page returned 403 ([Suspilne Dnipro](https://suspilne.media/dnipro/1017925-misce-moei-mrii-u-nikopoli-dron-armii-rf-zrujnuvav-budinok-zoovolonterki/)).

### 12. Nikopol under nets: the zone the plant never got, built by the city

CNN's reporting has residents asking for nets over the pavements too, and handheld "Chuika" detectors at about $500 that miss fibre-optic drones ([CNN via Egypt Independent](https://www.egyptindependent.com/only-fools-are-not-afraid-ukrainians-adapt-to-life-under-constant-russian-drone-attacks/), read as a republished copy). On 26 June 2026 a drone hit a minibus under a net on the road into Nikopol, killing 2 and injuring at least 12 ([Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-drone-targets-civilian-minibus-in-nikopol-killing-2-injuring-at-least-12/)). On 23 April 2025 an FPV drone hit a bus of Marhanets mining-plant workers, killing 9 ([Telegraf](https://news.telegraf.com.ua/ukr/ukraina/2025-04-23/5906601-dron-atakuvav-avtobus-yakiy-viz-tsivilnikh-na-robotu-vsi-detali-pro-vibukh-v-margantsi)). Truth Hounds' *Killzone* counts an average of **459 drone attacks a month** in Nikopol raion from September 2024 to March 2025 ([Truth Hounds](https://truth-hounds.org/en/cases/killzone-how-russian-drones-are-devastating-the-river-dnipros-right-bank/), via summaries). The insight comes from the Nikopol researcher. The IAEA's protection zone was proposed and never agreed, and the film already holds on it as a dashed circle. The city built its own zone instead: a lattice of cables strung over streets, visually kin to the power lines. Footage of a drive under the nets moves forward, which the project's Scene Viewer work found gives too little to reconstruct. Use the bus strikes only as located events, never as images of victims.

### 13. The cooling tower fire, and the dome hit

On **11 August 2024** black smoke rose from the Unit 1 cooling tower. Zelensky posted video, Nikopol's district head cited reports of burned tyres, and Rosatom blamed Ukrainian drones. The IAEA found heavy scorching inside but "not been able to draw definitive conclusions", was refused access to the second tower, and Grossi later said the tower requires demolition ([WNN](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/iaea-experts-continue-zaporizhzhia-cooling-tower-f); [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/cooling-tower-at-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-requires-demolition-after-fire-iaea-chief-says/)). His inspection inside the tower was filmed ([LIGA video](https://news.liga.net/en/politics/video/iaea-chief-grossi-inspects-cooling-tower-at-zaporizhzhia-npp-where-fire-occurred-video)). On 7 April 2024 drones scorched the top of the Unit 6 dome; the IAEA did not attribute the strike ([Arms Control Association](https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2024-05/news/drones-hit-zaporizhzhia-reactor-building)). The tower is the **best on-site splat candidate**. It was filmed from several sides, many of them from Nikopol, by Zelensky's video, the IAEA's inspection and likely Russian footage. Any model would be built from third-party footage and must say so. No independent geolocation of the burning-tower videos was found, so this is an **open question where a careful student investigation could add something** rather than duplicate settled work.

Self-seeded willows and poplars on the bed reached about **2 m in summer 2024, about 7 m in 2025 and about 9 m by spring 2026**. ### 15–18. The town, the factory, the dam and the far substation

**Enerhodar fails with its plant.** On 25 August 2022 the town lost light and water through shelling while the ZNPP was disconnected from the grid for the first time in its history ([Ukrainska Pravda](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2022/08/25/7364739)). Outages followed in September and October 2022 (the "Luch" substation) and in July 2024. In September 2025 the plant moved its off-site emergency centre into the town ([ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/2025-10-20/article-7472/update-on-zaporizhzhia/)). Dry taps are to residents what diesel generators are to reactors, an inverted one-to-one mirror. But the town is occupied: no new footage, no identifiable residents, and its night-light layer (VIIRS) is on hold in this project.

**The ferroalloy plant.** On 7 November 2023 the **Nikopol Ferroalloy Plant**, the factory Ferosplavna-1 is named after, stopped its furnaces because shelling of the energy system risked its power and water ([Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-economy/3783845-nikopol-ferroalloy-plant-suspends-production-due-to-russian-shelling.html)). Interfax noted that experts suspected its owner's interests played a part, so the motive is contested ([Interfax-Ukraine](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/948380.html)). It is a factory waiting on power, opposite a power plant waiting on one line.

**DniproHES.** On 22 March 2024 missiles wrecked the machine hall of DniproHES, the station at the corridor's eastern end. One hit a **trolleybus crossing the dam's crest**, killing the driver ([Ukrinform](https://ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/3843190-rocket-hits-trolleybus-with-people-moving-along-dnipro-hydroelectric-power-plant-dam.html)). Accounts of passengers conflict. No OSINT geolocation of the impact video ([Liga](https://news.liga.net/en/amp/politics/video/poyavilas-zapis-momenta-prileta-po-dneproges-letela-krylataya-raketa-h-101-video)) was found, and abundant pre-war tourist and drone footage makes it a good splat candidate for the dam's form. Do not publish new close views of a working dam.

**Dniprovska substation and the IAEA's crossings.** Shelling of the Dniprovska substation, at the far end of the 750 kV line, de-energised the ZNPP in October 2022 ([Babel](https://babel.ua/en/news/85525-russia-fired-at-a-substation-in-dnipropetrovsk-region-the-zaporizhzhia-npp-is-completely-de-energized)). A substation "about 100 km north-west" damaged in May 2026 kept the repaired Dniprovska line unusable ([WNN](https://world-nuclear-news.org/articles/substation-damage-stopping-zaporizhzhia-power-line-reconnection-says-iaea)); that it is Dniprovska rests only on a partisan claim. The IAEA's rotations cross the same front as the wires, once partly on foot, and a drone hit an IAEA vehicle on 10 December 2024 ([Arms Control Association](https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2025-01/news-briefs/drone-strikes-iaea-vehicle-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant)). Both mirror the story strongly, and both are **active targets or protected routes**: show them only at regional scale, as already published.

Several smaller cases can serve as cutaways rather than chapters. The Zaporizhzhia regional children's hospital keeps dialysis and intensive care running on generators and **43 kW of donated solar panels** ([Brno Daily](https://brnodaily.com/?p=57105)). A Nikopol invincibility point runs three generators for up to 120 people ([Suspilne](https://suspilne.media/dnipro/594691-mabut-zima-bude-ca-tazca-ak-pracuut-punkti-nezlamnosti-v-nikopoli-reportaz/), undated). Zaporizhzhia's underground schools are buildings designed to survive the threat ([Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/05/21/7513341)). Regional analogues are better as context than as microcosms: Mykolaiv's water main, cut in occupied ground in April 2022, a single lifeline that held half a million people hostage ([Euromaidan Press on BBC](https://euromaidanpress.com/2022/10/26/russia-accused-of-cutting-off-ukraine-water-supply-to-mykolaiv-bbc/)); the Antonivskyi bridge; Krynky; Kherson's Korabel district; and the flooded villages below the Kakhovka dam, which are already exhaustively investigated.

## The trench near Marhanets is a 2022 shore defence beside the lines, not a guard on them

### What it is and when it appeared

The student's site sits on the Ukrainian-held right bank, in Marhanets urban hromada near Maryivka (342 people in 2022; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marivka)), about 10 km from the occupied plant. The project's OSM survey puts the north-shore crossing tower about **283 m south** of the trench. The pattern is a zig-zag (traversed) fire trench with two horseshoe-shaped dug-in emplacements behind it, a standard field-fortification layout. The trench-site researcher read Esri's own metadata for every Wayback release at the point. **These dates are verified from primary data**:

| Esri release | Capture (Esri metadata) | Trench and emplacements |
|---|---|---|
| 2021-05-19 | 4 May 2019, WorldView-3, 0.31 m | Absent (grassland, a small tilled plot) |
| 2022-04-27 | **1 Oct 2021**, GeoEye-1, 0.46 m | **Absent** |
| 2023-02-23 | **29 Aug 2022**, WorldView-3, 0.31 m | **Present**: bright fresh spoil, vehicle tracks |
| 2024-02-08 | 27 Jun 2023, WorldView-2, 0.5 m | Present; grassing over |
| 2025-04-24 to 2026-09-24 | 23 Aug 2024, WorldView-2, 0.5 m | Present; fresh black burn scar to the south |

Source: [Esri Wayback configuration](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/config.maptiles.arcgis.com/waybackconfig.json) and its per-release metadata layers. The works were dug **after Russia seized Enerhodar on 4 March 2022 and no later than 29 August 2022**, almost certainly in spring–summer 2022, judging by the fresh spoil. They were therefore built while the reservoir was full, facing open water and Enerhodar across it. They are not a fortification of the drained bed from 2023–24. The 23 August 2024 capture is the same WorldView-2 scene the project's crossing-pylon inset already uses. Because the 2021 and 2022 captures are more than ten months apart, construction cannot be narrowed further from Esri alone. Sentinel-2 passes from March to August 2022 might show when the 20–25 m bright spoil appeared, but that was not run.

### Its relationship to the lines, the burn and the houses

**Nothing public ties these earthworks to the power lines.** They stand near the towers because both occupy the same strip of high bank opposite Enerhodar. In general, positions like this guard a riverbank against crossings or landings. Official denials and unverified channel claims are the only public material on sabotage crossings near Nikopol ([Focus, 8 Feb 2024](https://focus.ua/voennye-novosti/625720-rossiyane-vyryli-tonnel-dlya-perebroski-drg-v-nikopol-v-rva-oprovergli-zayavleniya-smi)). Reported Russian use of the drained bed is concentrated far upstream ([RFE/RL, 26 Dec 2025](https://www.rferl.org/a/russia-attacks-drained-reservoir-dam-destroyed-ukraine/33630023.html)). The works predate Dnipropetrovsk oblast's 2024 contractor fortification programme ([ArmyInform](https://armyinform.com.ua/2024/03/30/na-dnipropetrovshhyni-vzhe-vykopaly-42-km-protytankovyh-roviv-i-vstanovyly-11-tysyach-tetraedriv/)). Who built them is unknown.

The **burn** is a datable event of its own. The researcher computed the median Normalized Burn Ratio of the burned patch against an unburned control on every Sentinel-2 pass of summer 2024. The difference sat at normal seasonal values (−0.060 on 16 August), **dropped to −0.188 on 18 August 2024**, held through September and began recovering by 30 September ([Planetary Computer, Sentinel-2 L2A](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/dataset/sentinel-2-l2a)). The fire therefore happened **between the passes of 16 and 18 August 2024**, and the scar, about 1 km across and reaching toward the old shore, is fresh in Esri's capture five days later. **Its cause is not established.** Shelling, drones, heat and farm burning can all ignite dry steppe, and no oblast or district report names a fire here on those dates. The hromada is shelled from the occupied bank, and the record backs that. Up to 40 rockets were fired on 25 July 2022, reportedly from Enerhodar's residential quarters ([Fakty](https://fakty.com.ua/ru/ukraine/20220725-rf-obstrilyala-nikopol-ta-marganecz-prosto-zi-spalnogo-rajonu-energodara/)), and a night strike killed 13 in Marhanets on 10 August 2022 ([Vikna](https://vikna.tv/video/ukrayina/obstril-marganczyu-sogodni-naslidky-nichnogo-udaru-rosiyan/amp/)). On 12 September 2023 shelling damaged seven private houses, three gas pipelines and **three local power lines** ([Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.ua/rubric-ato/3760112-rosiani-obstrilali-marganec-poskodzeni-tri-gazogoni-ta-linii-elektroperedac.html)). The safe caption is "a fire burned this ground between 16 and 18 August 2024 (Sentinel-2)", followed separately by "the hromada is shelled from the occupied bank", with sources. The **houses** north-west of the trench are a dense grid of small plots and cottages that looks like a dacha or garden cooperative. Its name is unconfirmed. They show no obvious burn in the 2024 capture and look less tended than in 2022. That is the researcher's reading of the imagery, not a damage assessment.

The tie to the film's story comes from the IAEA. On 20 August 2026 Ferosplavna-1, then the plant's only line, was disconnected at 00:44, and **"the location of the damage to the line was preliminarily assessed to be on the northern bank of the Dnipro River"** ([CGTN](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-08-20/news-1PLl5ZvZRoQ/p.html); [WNN, 7 Sep 2026](https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/local-ceasefire-in-place-for-zaporizhzhia-power-line-repairs)). That is the trench's bank. But the break could be anywhere along Ferosplavna-1's tens of kilometres north of the river, so **do not say or imply it was at this site**. One search summary wrongly called the north bank the plant's side; the ZNPP is on the south (left) bank.

### What it can and cannot be in the film

As a microcosm the site is strong for the **shore** rather than for the trench as a military object. On one strip of steppe and garden plots the plant's last lines come ashore. In the war's first months it was dug into a defensive line facing the occupied plant across water. A year later the water drained away in front of it. In August 2024 the ground beside it burned, and in August 2026 the last line was cut "on the northern bank". The lifeline, the front line and civilian ground share about 500 m. The material is **satellite stills only**: a five-date Esri stack (2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024) and Sentinel-2 every few days, presented as dated stills and slow dissolves, which the pipeline already makes. **A splat is not feasible.** No ground or drone footage of the trench, the north-shore towers or the view from here to the plant was found. Geolocation anchors are the paved road, the grid of dacha lanes, the trench outline and the two rights-of-way, whose tower shadows show in the 2022 and 2023 captures. Chronolocation rests on the October 2021–August 2022 bracket, the August 2024 burn and the June 2023 drainage.

The safety rules from the trench note bind the film. **Do not publish the coordinates, a pin, or a frame that places the trench to within metres**; describe it as "on the north bank near Marhanets, where the lines come ashore", and keep the coordinates in a private log for the professor. **Use only dated archival imagery from 2024 or earlier**, credited on screen, and never seek or claim knowledge of current use. **Make no operational interpretation**: say "a trench and two dug-in emplacements of a common type", with nothing on weapons, units or fields of fire. Do not mark damaged or repaired towers. Never go there or ask anyone to film there: it is within artillery and drone range, and the bed is mined. Consult the professor, and ideally someone with Ukrainian security-reporting experience, before anything is shared. Blurring or generalising the location in a public cut is allowed; generating imagery is not.

**Verified from primary data:** absent on 1 October 2021, present on 29 August 2022, still present on 27 June 2023 and 23 August 2024, and the burn between 16 and 18 August 2024. **Reported:** the shelling of the hromada, the IAEA's "northern bank" assessment and the sabotage denials. **Not verified:** who built the works and why, any link to the towers, the cause of the fire, the cooperative's name, and any ground media. The student's Google screenshot may be a different capture from Esri's; Google Earth Pro's status bar shows its date.

## No candidate has confirmed splat footage, and the richest sources still need hand-searching

The researchers agree: **no orbiting footage suitable for a Gaussian splat was confirmed for any candidate**, and every listed video still needs watching frame by frame. The project's own Scene Viewer work sets the bar. Only takes where the camera turns around a place have worked, and walking or driving straight ahead gives too little. By that standard the best chances, in order, are these. First, **Ukrenergo's 2015 aerial** of the crossing towers, which uses moving drone views, though only the steel above the water would reconstruct and the upload predates the collection's scope. Second, the **burned cooling tower**, filmed from several sides by different parties. Third, the **beached dugout boats at Khortytsia**: a 7 m oak monoxyl estimated at 300–500 years old ([Radio Svoboda](https://www.radiosvoboda.org/a/pidryv-kakhovskoyi-hes-povernennya-velykoho-lukhu-arkheolohichni-skarby/32487603.html)), and the Cossack boat in PRESSING's 53:45 film (6 July 2023, ~3.5M views; [YouTube aqyGE7aS6pg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqyGE7aS6pg), chapter at 26:38). These are the most splat-friendly objects found, but they lie about 60 km upstream. Fourth, **DniproHES** from pre-war tourist and drone footage. Weaker possibilities are walking shots in the Suspilne forest films, where foliage is noisy, and a walkaround of the dog shelter, if Voshun's Facebook has one. Any splat from third-party footage is a reconstruction and must say whose footage it came from.

Several items still need checking by hand. One film already covers Nikopol's human side: Anastasiia Bonadyha's **_Apocalypse Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow, and the Day After Tomorrow_** (FAMU, Prague, 2025, 20 min), which follows Father Bohdan, who stays in Nikopol after the dam. The object- and creature-centred microcosms (the pond, the sightline, the graves, the dogs' water) are the ones that differ from it, and its director is a natural contact.

## Recommendation: the pond, the far bank and the drowned church

The three strongest candidates each serve a different chapter of the outline, and each connects to work the student has already done.

**The cooling pond** belongs to the plant chapter. It is the only candidate where the student's own pipeline produces the evidence. A Sentinel-2 waterline time series of the pond from 2022 to 2026, drawn with the same tone curve and dissolves as the corridor film, checked against MIS's area figures and the IAEA's 3.2 m drop, shows the "reservoir within the reservoir" shrinking while the record runs. Honcharuk's refusal to dive into the sprinkler pond is its single human line, used as text only.

**The far bank** belongs to the crossing chapter. The MLRS video's camera stood on the shore where Dniprovska and Ferosplavna-1 land. Re-solving CIT's chimney bearing on the georeferenced passes, then setting Kling's balcony viewshed beside it, turns the Blender flight's destination into a witness position. The shore is where the plant was seen firing and where its last line was later cut "on the northern bank". The trench site can appear here only as the generalised shore with its dated satellite stack and the August 2024 burn, never as a pinned position.

**Pokrovske** belongs to the Great Meadow chapter. The church foundation, matched to the 1941 sheet and the June 2023 passes, shows what the reservoir covered, and Orliv island's resurfacing at the Kamianka–Nikopol crossing can be shown in the same passes.

The **ceasefire for one span** should not be a fourth sequence. It should be the record's longest held moment: one repair of about 36 hours, demined Friday and reconnected at 19:43 on Saturday, with the Hum returning on one voice. The **4 March CCTV** fits the same way as the record's first cut, filmed by the plant itself.

## Conclusion

The research changes where the film's evidence comes from. The strongest microcosms are not places the student must film or license. They are places the student's existing instruments can measure: a pond's shoreline, a camera bearing, a church's footprint, a burn's date. That suits Forensic Architecture's method better than footage would. The same tools that draw the lines can show, at small scale, the dependence that holds the plant hostage: water through boreholes, a shield the other bank cannot fire at, and a landscape whose dead were first drowned and then exposed by energy infrastructure.

The trench site's lesson reaches beyond it. Nearly everything near the lines is also near a front, so in this film rigour means resolution chosen on purpose as much as accuracy. Generalising a location, refusing to mark a damaged tower, and stating that a fire's cause is unknown are forensic decisions in their own right, and the film should show them openly. Its strongest claims will be the ones it shows it declined to make.
