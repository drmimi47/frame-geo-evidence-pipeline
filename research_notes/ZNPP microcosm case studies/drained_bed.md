# Microcosms on the drained bed of the former Kakhovka reservoir (Great Meadow / Velykyi Luh, near the ZNPP), June 2023–2026

Research notes, 29 October 2026. YouTube metadata (channel, upload date, length, views) was read directly from YouTube with yt-dlp on 29 October 2026. Where only a news description of a video exists, that is said. No coordinates below come from a source unless one is cited. Positions are given as place names to be geolocated against Sentinel-2 / OSM / the 1941 sheet in the project's own pipeline.

## What surfaced when the water fell (flooded villages, Sich sites, archaeology, wrecks, WWII remains, old crossings)

### Takeaway
The best-documented single site near the ZNPP is Pokrovske (Nikopol district, right bank). Within days of the breach, the foundation of a stone church and a cemetery with open graves and human remains emerged there, on the site of the last Zaporozhian Sich (Nova/Pidpilnenska Sich). It was filmed by locals and spread through Telegram. Around it are more sites, most less documented: the Kamianka–Nikopol crossing (a 1943 bridge pier, the ferry, Mykytyn Rih Sich, the resurfacing Orliv island), Cossack dugout boats at Khortytsia, and WWII wrecks. The ZNPP itself stands on the levelled dunes (kuchugury) of the old Great Meadow shore.

**Kamianka–Nikopol crossing, Mykytyn Rih, Orliv island (opposite and just east of the ZNPP)**
- In 1943 the Germans, using local labour, built a bridge from Kamianka to Nikopol. It was destroyed when the reservoir was made, leaving one pier. A ferry ran at the narrowest point, where the Nikopol and Kamianka capes face each other, and the crossing had been used here since the 17th century. The Mykytynska Sich controlled it. — description of the YouTube video "трагічно блукають уламки забуття на переправі Кам'янка - Нікополь", channel Майя П`ятьКоней, uploaded 1 June 2019 (filmed April 2019, pre-war), 2:55, 131 views: [YouTube Ujuo7po7An0](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ujuo7po7An0)
- Orliv Ostriv (Eagle Island) formed when the Dnipro shifted channel around 1845–46, was submerged in 1954, and has been gradually resurfacing since 2023. Mykytynska Sich sits on Cape Mykytyn Rih near Orliv island, about 750 m below the Nikopol pier on the right bank. Bank erosion there had exposed old graves with buttons, icons and crosses (no date given). — [Vikna, 19 May 2024](https://vikna.tv/dlia-tebe/kahovske-vodoshovyshhe-znahidky-istoriyi-na-dni-kolyshnoyi-vodojmy/)

### Inferences
- **Pokrovske is the strongest "hidden/revealed" microcosm.** A Cossack community's church and its dead were drowned by a Soviet energy project (the Kakhovka HPP) and exposed by the destruction of that same energy infrastructure in war. It mirrors the ZNPP story: energy infrastructure that remakes a landscape, and a reversal that exposes what it covered. The ethical weight (human remains, looting) is high.
- The **Kamianka–Nikopol crossing** is the closest historical "line across the water" to the plant's power lines: Cossack crossing, then a 1943 occupation bridge, then a ferry, then the pylons, then today's no-man's-land. It sits within sight of the ZNPP.
- That the plant stands on levelled kuchugury of the old meadow makes a quiet opening fact: the plant is itself built on the Great Meadow.

### Gaps
- No coordinates for the Pokrovske church/cemetery in any source I fetched. They need geolocation from the local Telegram videos against Sentinel-2 (June 2023) and the 1941 sheet's church symbol.
- I found no named archaeologist or official survey of the Pokrovske site after 2023. Institute of Archaeology NASU announcements were not found.
- No post-2023 field documentation of Chortomlyk/Kapulivka, Bazavluk or Mykytyn Rih surfaced in my searches. Searches on TikTok, Instagram and Telegram were not possible with my tools.
- The original Telegram posts behind the Pokrovske clips (uploader, exact date) were not identified.

## The new forest (the "new Great Meadow"), its ecologists, and the restore-vs-keep fight

### Takeaway
A self-seeded willow and poplar forest has grown on the bed: about 2 m in summer 2024, about 7 m in 2025, about 9 m by spring 2026. Anna Kuzemko and Oleksii Vasyliuk of the Ukrainian Nature Conservation Group (UNCG), and the UWEC Work Group (Eugene Simonov), document it under drone threat and argue against rebuilding the dam. It is filmed by Suspilne Dnipro, TSN and independent channels (RID). The same forest now hides infantry crossing the bed.

### Cited Findings
- Summer 2024: willows and poplars up to about 2 m. 2025: up to about 7 m. Latest expedition (spring 2026): the largest trees about 9 m. Scientists document it several times a year at the risk of Russian drones. Ukrainian soldiers guard the whole right bank against sabotage-group crossings. Drone noise is audible in the field recordings; nightingales of two species are present. — [TSN, Ihor Berezhanskyi, 7 June 2026](https://tsn.ua/exclusive/rivno-try-roky-pislia-pidryvu-kakhovskoyi-hes-iak-vyhliadaye-unikalnyy-velykyy-luh-3100838.html)

### Inferences
- **"What grows in the absence"** is the forest's microcosm: an evacuated, mined, drone-watched zone becoming wild. It parallels Chornobyl (another nuclear-adjacent exclusion), and it is a split image. The scientists' cover for nature is the soldiers' cover for infiltration (see the next section).
- The tree heights (2 → 7 → 9 m, 2024–26) are a dated, sourced series. The film could show them as a growth measure against the pylons' 90–126 m.

## The bed as front line / no man's land (crossings, sabotage groups, mines, drones, fires)

### Takeaway
Since late 2025 the overgrown bed has become an infiltration route for small Russian infantry groups toward Zaporizhzhia, and Ukrainian HUR units hunt them there. One concrete human story is documented: a Russian soldier lost for five days in the willow forest, photographed by UNITED24. Near the ZNPP, Ukraine says Russia is setting up observation posts on the bed and denies that sabotage groups reached Nikopol. Fires recur on the bed.

### Cited Findings
- A Russian soldier ("Ivan") wandered five days on the bed in late 2025 after a drone attack, sheltering under an anti-drone cloak. He was then captured, wounded, by the Bratstvo Battalion (Timur Special Unit, HUR), given first aid and handed to medics. Bratstvo escorted captured Russians about 27 km across the former bed. Terrain: dense willow forest, marsh, reeds, mines and UXO, constant drone surveillance. Photos by Mykyta Shandyba. The capture point is **not named.** UNITED24 says it could not verify parts of his account. — [UNITED24 Media, 17 September 2026](https://united24media.com/inside-ukraine/photoreports/russian-soldier-got-lost-for-5-days-at-the-bottom-of-the-kakhovka-reservoir-until-ukrainian-special-forces-found-him-22608); related video [YouTube RL-fwrrlPOg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RL-fwrrlPOg) (not checked)

## The pylons on caissons crossing the bed

### Takeaway
Two crossings run from Enerhodar's plants to the Nikopol bank. One is the 330 kV ZTPP–Ferosplavna line (1977): 7 towers, 5 standing in the reservoir, 90 m high (the central one 100 m), on concrete foundations about 30 m across, 750–800 m apart. The other is the 750 kV ZNPP–Dniprovska line (1984): 5 towers, 3 in the reservoir, 126 m intermediate towers on foundations about 40 m across, 1.2 km apart, floated out on hollow caissons built in a dock. Their towers hosted large cormorant colonies. I found no post-2023 footage of people at their bases.

### Cited Findings
- All the specifications in the takeaway (heights, weights 375/350 t, spans, the floating "наплывной" method) come from Russian Wikipedia, which cites Kalinina et al. 2021 and a 2011 blog. — [ru.wikipedia, Переходы ЛЭП через Каховское водохранилище](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%8B_%D0%9B%D0%AD%D0%9F_%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B7_%D0%9A%D0%B0%D1%85%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B5_%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%BE%D1%85%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%B8%D1%89%D0%B5). ## Channels and pools: the reforming Dnipro and the ZNPP's cooling water

### Takeaway
The plant's own cooling pond is a small, measured replica of the lost reservoir. It is cut off from the drained reservoir and fed from 11 groundwater wells drilled after the breach, and its level fell more than 3.2 m by September 2025. Plant staff built an isolation dam inside it, which the IAEA has not been allowed to visit.

### Cited Findings
- By early September 2025 the IAEA reported the cooling pond had dropped more than 3.2 m since the dam's destruction, nearly 60 cm in the last three months. A dam was built to isolate one channel of the pond, fed by mobile pumps, to supply the main transformers. The IAEA was refused access to it. Ten of 11 post-breach groundwater wells were working, and 12 sprinkler ponds are fed from them. A project to pump Dnipro water into the pond is planned. — [Interfax-Ukraine](https://interfax.com.ua/news/general/1102185.html); [IAEA via GlobalSecurity, 28 August 2025](https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/ukraine/2025/08/ukraine-250828-iaea01.htm); [NEI Magazine, "New dam aids cooling water conservation at Zaporizhia"](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/new-dam-aids-cooling-water-conservation-at-zaporizhia/); [WNN, "Groundwater extraction begins at Zaporizhzhia"](https://world-nuclear-news.org/Articles/Groundwater-extraction-begins-at-Zaporizhzhia) (all via search summary; not fetched individually)
- In 2023 the pond and the ZTPP discharge channel were the plant's remaining main water sources. — same search results
- UNCG argued in 2023 that the plant could be supplied from an adjacent Dnipro channel. — [UWEC](https://uwecworkgroup.info/is-it-time-to-restore-velykyi-luh/)
- Ermak's drone video (early August 2023) shows the main Dnipro channel and side channels near Nova Kakhovka. — [novakahovka.city](https://novakahovka.city/articles/303964/kahovske-vodoshovische-peretvorilosya-na-merezhu-protokiv) (not near the ZNPP)

### Inferences
- **The cooling pond as microcosm**: a reservoir inside the drained reservoir, held up by pumps and a new dam that inspectors may not see. It mirrors the whole story (water and power held hostage, a hidden structure), at the plant's own scale, and is measurable from Sentinel-2 water masks (the project's `stitch`/water method). A strong candidate because the project can make its own evidence (dated water extents) without visiting.

### Gaps
- No exact date or coordinates of the isolation dam from a primary IAEA page in my fetches. The IAEA Update numbers (around 290–310) should be read directly.
