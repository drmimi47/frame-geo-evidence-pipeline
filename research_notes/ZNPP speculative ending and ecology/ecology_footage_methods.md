# Plants and animals in "Powerlines": footage leads, datasets and methods (researched 4 November 2026)

Scope: flora and fauna media and data for the drained Kakhovka bed between Enerhodar/Kamianka-Dniprovska and Nikopol/Marhanets (the crossing of Dniprovska 750 kV and Ferosplavna-1 330 kV), the ZNPP cooling pond and the Great Meadow; and artistic/forensic methods that treat plants and animals as witnesses or clocks. Video metadata below was read with the project's own yt-dlp (`.venv/bin/yt-dlp -j`, no download) on 4 November 2026; licence "standard" means yt-dlp reported no Creative Commons licence (standard YouTube licence: tracing/reference only, no reuse of pixels without permission). iNaturalist and GBIF were queried through their public APIs the same day. Nothing was ingested into `library/`.

## Footage: geolocatable media of the willow forest, birds, wildlife, the cooling pond and the bed (2023-2026)

### Takeaway
Plenty of 2024-2026 YouTube footage of the regrown willow forest exists, and the best of it comes from Suspilne Dnipro's Nikopol-district expeditions with ecologist Vadym Maniuk. It is filmed on the right (Nikopol) bank shore, the bank the crossing lands on. I found nothing showing birds on the crossing towers or wires, nothing current on the cooling pond's wildlife, and no fish-kill footage near the crossing. The most striking animal footage, rose pelicans on the bed in August 2026, is military drone reconnaissance with no public URL, so it should not be used.

### Inferences
- **Suspilne Dnipro's two films (DGIFNa_viuc, ZUXsmHCUVg4) are the strongest candidates.** They are filmed in Nikopol district, on the crossing's own landfall bank, and made by a public broadcaster with named scientists. They are likely to include drone shots of the forest edge. Each needs a frame-by-frame check for skyline features (the ZNPP's cooling towers and units across the bed, the 750 kV towers) to place it. Matching the forest edge and channels to the corridor's Sentinel-2 passes could pin it within about 100 m. That would work as a sequence of stills; an orbit for a Gaussian splat has not been seen yet and needs checking.
- **Safety checks on every frame.** Military drone footage (the 128th brigade's pelicans, and the crews' shots in the Suspilne film) is reconnaissance near the front. Any frame used must be checked against the safety rule: no positions, nothing finer than about 3 km for any damaged line, no Dniprovska substation.
- **Use RID's videos with care.** RID is a Zaporizhzhia channel, so VV5Nju1dIsE and FOEzXnV482E are likely upstream, near Zaporizhzhia and Dniprorudne, or on the left bank. BdU3IBVoBSk ("the widest stretch") may be nearer the Nikopol–Enerhodar reach, but this is unconfirmed. They are the Great Meadow but perhaps not the crossing.
- **Vasyliuk and Kuzemko's nightingale recordings are the best lead for real sound.** They have the forest's own sound and drone noise in it. Ask the Ukrainian Nature Protection Group (UNCG) for the raw recording and its place and date, which would keep it on the evidence side rather than synthesized.
- **The pelicans as a "counter-record".** The pelicans, absent about 150 years and back by 2025-2026, are the single most cinematic counter-record event. They can only be used as a sourced caption, not as footage, unless the brigade's video is published with a URL and checked.

- No footage found of birds perched on or nesting in the crossing towers or wires (storks, cormorants, raptors). The Ukrainian search on ЛЕП/опори returned nothing for this area. No source says birds use the caissons either.
- No cooling-pond wildlife survey or footage from 2023-2026 was found, and no fish-kill footage near Enerhodar since 2023.
- Telegram, X, Instagram and TikTok were not searched directly; their search isn't reachable from this tool. Channels to check by hand: the Nikopol city channels, "Марганець онлайн", Suspilne Dnipro's Telegram, the 128th brigade's channels, and Maniuk's Facebook.

- The Frontliner video's exact place is not given in its metadata.

## Datasets: iNaturalist, GBIF, eBird, xeno-canto, Movebank, Sentinel-2 vegetation studies, phenology

### Takeaway
Citizen-science records cluster in Nikopol town and on the Marhanets shore. They cover the towns, not the bed, so the bed itself is almost empty in the databases. The Marhanets shore holds a real, citable bird-sound record from 2023-2026 (iNaturalist user `uabird` and xeno-canto recordist Ekaterina Kormina), including a thrush nightingale, Savi's warbler and waders. These are real recordings that could replace or underlie a synthesized biophony. No peer-reviewed Sentinel-2 study of the willow regrowth was found, only a GitHub method project and figures quoted in the press.

### Inferences
- **The Marhanets records stand in for the crossing's north bank.** They come from the shore of the same reach, about 10-15 km upstream of Nikopol. The waders recorded by `uabird` at about 47.69 N (stilt, Temminck's stint, spotted redshank) indicate shallow wet ground: mudflats or ponds. Where exactly needs checking against the Sentinel-2 water masks before any claim.
- **Every "who lives here" claim needs its distance.** The bed between the lines is effectively unsurveyed by citizen science because of shelling and mines. The film can say exactly that: the observations stop at the shore. A map of observation points crowding the shore and never entering the bed is itself a forensic image of access.
- **The forest-height clock is available now.** The press series (0 in June 2023, about 2 m in 2024, about 7 m in 2025, about 9-10 m in 2026) has sources, but it is press-reported and from unknown locations. The project's own ICESat-2 tracks (already used for `bed.py`) may measure canopy height over the bed directly. ATL08 is ICESat-2's land and canopy product, so it is a possible measured "forest clock" for the crossing itself. This is an inference to test, not checked here.

### Gaps
- No Movebank tracking study crossing the area was checked (not searched in this session).
- Phenology was not found: no date of willow leaf-out on the bed. The project could derive it from its own Sentinel-2 NDVI time series over the bed, which would be derived data, not a source claim.
- eBird's own hotspot pages and media were not opened. The licences for xeno-canto files are unchecked (xeno-canto files are usually some CC BY-NC-SA or CC BY-NC-ND variant; check each).

## Methods: plants and animals as witnesses or measures of time

### Takeaway
Two precedents are closest to "Powerlines". Forensic Architecture reads vegetation as the recording surface of an event: leaves as evidence of herbicide spraying, and fire and drainage patterns as evidence of ecocide. Susan Schuppli's "material witness" holds that matter registers events and that the practices making it testify must be shown. Chernobyl gives the counter-intuitive precedent: wildlife abundant once people leave. Ursula Biemann's video essays offer a register for the forest and sound as voices without anthropomorphism.

### Cited Findings
- **Forensic Architecture, herbicide spraying in Gaza.** The plants are the instrument. Soft-stemmed plants withered almost at once and harder-stemmed ones later, and Samaneh Moafi describes leaves keeping "spotted marks where they were hit by toxic particles". Fluid-dynamics modelling showed the wind carrying glyphosate onto the farms. The evidence was "threaded" from vegetation, farmer testimony and environmental elements — [Hyperallergic](https://hyperallergic.com/518205/forensic-architecture-center-for-contemporary-nature-plasticity-of-the-planet/); [Arts of the Working Class, Moafi](https://artsoftheworkingclass.org/text/samaneh-moafi); [FA, Ecocide in Gaza](https://forensic-architecture.org/investigation/ecocide-in-gaza)
- **FA, "Ecocide in Indonesia" (2016-17).** The 2015 Kalimantan/Sumatra fires burned over 21,000 km² of forest and peat. Fieldwork plus satellite analysis and pattern recognition showed that drainage and monocropping were direct causes. The film is about 9.5 minutes and was commissioned by FIBGAR (Baltasar Garzón) — [FA case page](https://www.forensic-architecture.org/case/ecocide-indonesia/); [Ars Electronica 2018](https://webarchive.ars.electronica.art/festival/2018/error/en/index.html%3Fp=8769.html); [BAK](https://bakonline.org/en/making+public/program/propositions+7+5+sense/)

The book is structured like a trial; its cases include Fukushima radioactivity off the Canadian coast — [Goldsmiths Research Online](https://research.gold.ac.uk/28197); [MIT Press Bookstore](https://mitpressbookstore.mit.edu/book/9780262043571)

  - *Forest Law* (2014, with Paulo Tavares, about 38 min) follows the legal effort to define the Amazon as a living entity, a legal subject, against oil and mining — [Video Data Bank](https://vdb.org/node/9987); [UNAM Gaceta](https://www.gaceta.unam.mx/no-podemos-imaginar-la-inteligencia-de-la-naturaleza-porque-no-habla-nuestra-lengua-ursula-biemann/)

- **Chernobyl, wildlife.** Deryabina et al. 2015 (*Current Biology* 25:R824-R826, doi 10.1016/j.cub.2015.08.017) found elk, roe deer, red deer and wild boar in numbers similar to uncontaminated reserves, and wolves more than 7 times as abundant. ### Inferences (concrete ideas for the film, each kept within the evidence rules)
1. **The forest as a clock since 6 June 2023.** In the 3D crossing, the bed's vegetation rises with the date: grass, then shrub, then forest. The heights would come from measurement (ICESat-2 ATL08 canopy over the crossing, if the tracks have it) or from the sourced press series (about 2 m in 2024, about 7 m in 2025, about 9-10 m in 2026, labelled with the source and "elsewhere on the bed"). The trees are drawn as representative, like the towers. One held moment could set the tree height beside the towers' published heights: by 2026 the forest stands at about a quarter of a 330 kV tower. Representative, said so.
2. **The record and the counter-record.** Under the line record (lit, cut, dotted), a second, quieter ribbon would track living things returning, each mark a dated, sourced observation:
   - first shoots (autumn 2023, TSN)
   - waxwings at Marhanets (Nov 2023, eBird)
   - bluethroat (Apr 2023, xeno-canto)
   - the thrush nightingale (Apr 2025, XC1016141)
   - cormorants ×12 and purple herons (Aug 2025, eBird)
   - rose pelicans back after about 150 years (Aug 2026, Inshe.tv / Maniuk)
   
   The power record goes dark while the living record fills. It fits the unrolled film's grammar (the time axis left to right) and Schuppli's idea that matter registers events.
3. **Nightingales against the Hum.** The Hum is synthesized and said to be. A real recording (Kormina's thrush nightingale at Marhanets, 16 April 2025, cc licence to check, or UNCG's "hundreds of nightingales" with drone noise) would be mixed under it, credited and dated as a recording. The biophony would hold steady during line cuts and complete losses, when the Hum falls silent. This draws on Krause's idea of a soundscape's biophony; his writing was not checked this session. It is the one honest place where "observed" sound can enter.
4. **Birds as the only beings that touch the live wires.** No source found yet. Keep it as a question, not a claim: never draw birds on the conductors without a photograph or video of birds on these lines. If one turns up (storks often use towers), it must be geolocated only to a general region (≥3 km), never to a tower.
5. **Access as the image.** Plot every citizen-science point (iNat, eBird, xeno-canto) on the zoom-out. They hug Nikopol and Marhanets and stop at the shore; the bed between the two lines stays empty. The caption would read: "the observations stop where the shelling starts." Forensic Architecture's way: the absence of data as evidence, with sources per point.
6. **The cooling pond.** It shrank by more than 105,000 m² in June-August 2025 (Greenpeace). It held stocked fish kept to control algae (2015 stocking video, pre-war, as context only), with fish-eating birds already noted as a cause of death. It is the reactor's own small ecosystem against the self-seeded forest across the water ("the forest or the reactor"). There is no wildlife footage from 2023-2026, so it could be stills of passes only.
7. **The Chernobyl precedent as one sourced line.** "Without people, wildlife at Chernobyl matched nearby reserves (Deryabina et al. 2015)". It is a parallel, not a prediction for the Great Meadow, and must be said as context from sources.
8. **Gaussian splat candidates.** Only a drone orbit over one place would do, for example the forest edge in the Suspilne films if a crew circles it. Treat it like the ice pools: the shape from footage, the size assumed, and never joined across cuts. It needs viewing first.
