# Staging sourced speculation at the end of "Powerlines": methods, precedents and candidate angles

Researched 4 November 2026. Web sources are cited inline. Project facts come from the project's own notes
(`NEXT.md`, `docs/znpp-heart-plan.txt`) and are cited as such: they were checked there, not again here.
Where a search came back empty, that is said under Gaps. Nothing here is a fact about the plant unless a
source is given.

## 1. Speculative design and film: how studios mark speculation on screen

### Takeaway
The studios that work closest to research (Superflux, Liam Young, Territorial Agency) ground their futures in
named research and published models. They do not mark the speculation inside the frame with dashes or labels.
They mark it outside the frame, with a stated date, a project statement ("not a prediction"), the exhibition
context, and a change of register: mythic, sublime, or domestic props. The forensic studios (Forensic
Architecture, CST Kyiv) stay almost entirely in the past tense. So no precedent combines a forensic evidence
grammar with a future chapter, and "Powerlines" would have to invent that join. Its own conventions (dotted
means unconfirmed, light means power, "said so") are the strongest tools it has.

### Cited Findings
**Superflux (Anab Jain, Jon Ardern)**
- *Mitigation of Shock* (London flat set in 2050, CCCB Barcelona; later a Singapore version at the ArtScience
  Museum's "2219" show). The studio's own statement: "Mitigation of Shock is not a prediction, nor a render." Its
  aim is "actionable hope", not a forecast — [Superflux](https://superflux.in/index.php/work/mitigation-of-shock/); [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2019/12/31/superflux-mitigation-of-shock-climate-change-future-imagined/amp/)
- The grounding: climate projections for the UK and Northern Europe (food insecurity, extreme weather, resource
  scarcity), interviews with experts from NASA, the UK Met Office and Forum for the Future, and working prototypes
  (fogponics, Arduino-run growing). 2050 was chosen because "it sits within many current and well respected
  climate models" and because "by 2050 many of today's children will be their parents age" — [Superflux](https://superflux.in/index.php/work/mitigation-of-shock/)
- The speculation is carried by props: "discarded newspapers and a radio show", food computers "improvised from
  the detritus of a technological utopia that never quite arrived", recipes for "mealworm burgers and fox stew".
  The aim is to make a hyperobject "tangible, relatable and specific" — [Superflux](https://superflux.in/index.php/work/mitigation-of-shock/); [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/design/superflux-design-profile)
- The later works move to a mythic register on purpose. *Invocation for Hope* (MAK, Vienna Biennale 2021) is a
  forest of fire-blackened pines around a living pool, "an imagined vision of a post-Anthropocene landscape".
  *Refuge for Resurgence* (Venice 2021) sets a table for other species, with taxidermy and found objects standing
  in for them. Jain calls this "mythopoetic" — [Dezeen, Invocation](https://www.dezeen.com/2021/06/04/superflux-invocation-for-hope-forest/amp/); [Dezeen, Refuge](https://www.dezeen.com/2021/05/19/superflux-refuge-for-resurgence-venice-architecture-biennale/)

**Liam Young**
- *Planet City* is "a fiction" of one city for the whole Earth, run on renewables. Young stresses that it uses no
  new technology. It was built with a "global think tank of advisers and collaborators", and Young describes it as
  taking "research that's often lost inside the pages of peer-reviewed journals" — [NGV](https://ngv.vic.gov.au/liam-young-planet-city); [Artillery](https://artillerymag.com/planet-city-at-sci-arc/)
- *The Great Endeavor* (Venice Biennale 2023, with the social scientist Holly Jean Buck): carbon-removal
  infrastructure "equivalent in size to that of the entire global fossil fuel industry". Young: "built entirely from
  scientific papers"; "data dramatisation as opposed to data visualisation"; "I don't think it's a cautionary
  tale. I think it's a roadmap". He frames shots in "the language of the sublime painters" — [Koozarch interview](https://koozarch.com/interviews/the-great-endeavour-carbon-removal-infrastructure-at-a-planetary-scale); [La Biennale](https://www.labiennale.org/en/architecture/2023/dangerous-liaisons/liam-young)
- The listed run times disagree: 27 min, 9:23 and 10 min across venues, perhaps different cuts — [Labocine](https://www.labocine.com/films/the-great-endeavour); [Bundeskunsthalle](https://bundeskunsthalle.de/en/medienkunstraum); [Tribeca](https://tribecafilm.com/films/great-endeavour-2024)

**Territorial Agency (John Palmesino, Ann-Sofi Rönnskog)**
- *Oceans in Transformation* (TBA21–Academy, Ocean Space Venice 2020–21; STARTS Prize 2021): large dynamic
  composites of remote-sensing images that leave the gaps in knowledge and the areas with no data visible. They
  show "a potential evolving story without taking it for granted" — [STARTS Prize](https://starts-prize.aec.at/de/?p=3587); [Ars Electronica](https://ars.electronica.art/newdigitaldeal/?p=8405)
- *Museum of Oil* (with Greenpeace, at ZKM "Reset Modernity!", 2016) "puts the oil industry in the museum", which
  frames a still-running industry as already historical. That is a proposal staged as a museum — [ZKM](https://www.zkm.de/en/node/27313); [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com/185937720)

**Dunne & Raby / design fiction**
- *Speculative Everything* sets speculation apart from forecasting, which extrapolates trends and "has repeatedly
  been wrong". Speculative design asks "what if" questions to open debate about the futures people want and do not
  want — [MIT Press](https://mitpressbookstore.mit.edu/book/9780262548687); [Near Future Laboratory](https://nearfuturelaboratory.com/blog/2024/12/what-is-speculative-design)
- *United Micro Kingdoms* (Design Museum, 2013): England split into four counties, each a political model,
  presented as a design fiction meant to feed discussion of policy — [RIBAJ](https://ribaj.com/culture/united-micro-kingdoms-a-design-fiction); [Icon](https://www.iconeye.com/opinion/united-micro-kingdoms)
- The "diegetic prototype" (David Kirby, *Lab Coats in Hollywood*): an object that works as real inside the
  fiction. Kirby notes that film depictions persuade audiences that a technology is needed, viable and benign.
  Bruce Sterling defines design fiction as "the deliberate use of diegetic prototypes to suspend disbelief about
  change" — [Wikipedia, Design fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_fiction); [Near Future Laboratory](https://nearfuturelaboratory.com/essays/2024/12/what-is-speculative-design/)

**The futures cone**
- Voros's cone builds on Henchey (1978) and Hancock & Bezold (1994). Probable = extrapolated from trends, often
  quantitative. Plausible = consistent with how the world is understood to work. Possible = imaginable.
  Preferable = normative (one reading: a filter across the other zones, not a zone). Later versions add a
  "projected" baseline (business as usual) and a "preposterous" outer band — [The Voroscope](https://thevoroscope.com/2017/02/24/the-futures-cone-use-and-history/); [Think Insights](https://thinkinsights.net/index.php/strategy/future-cone)

**Forensic Architecture / CST Kyiv**
- Commentators describe FA as working "in reverse": it studies sites and events "not in order to imagine a built
  future". One essay on speculative animation groups FA with the backward-looking practices and Larissa Sansour and
  Liam Young with the forward-looking ones — [Cattabriga 2021, via search](https://cris.unibo.it/retrieve/b2a5f833-9904-4faa-8d5d-17a52f8e9e10/Cattabriga_2021_Design%20Tools%20for%20Alternative%20Narratives.pdf); [Wallpaper via Everand](https://www.everand.com/article/582901004/Can-Architectural-Analysis-Hold-Power-To-Account)
- CST (Kyiv, director Maksym Rokmaniko) paused its other work after February 2022 to investigate Russian attacks.
  Its first investigation with FA (10 June 2022) was the Kyiv TV Tower strike — [Hyperallergic](https://hyperallergic.com/740401/using-spatial-reconstruction-to-investigate-russias-war-crimes/)

### Inferences
- There are two families, and the film should pick one for its ending rather than blend them. (a) **Research
  fiction** (Young, Superflux): the future is shown whole and immersive, and the label lives outside the frame (a
  date, a statement, the context). (b) **Forensic and data** (FA, Territorial Agency, graphics desks): the frame
  itself carries the marks of what is known, uncertain or absent. "Powerlines" already runs on (b), and its evidence
  rules (inferred never shown as observed, imagery never generated) forbid (a)'s rendered futures. The ending
  should be (b) carried into the future tense: drawn, dashed, labelled. It can borrow (a)'s register (sublime
  framing, a held domestic-scale detail) without its rendered imagery.
- Territorial Agency's habit of leaving no-data areas visible fits the film's hatching (night lights with no
  good-quality value, the bed's inferred heights). The future can be drawn the same way: the unknown shown as an
  area, not hidden.
- Young's "data dramatisation" and Superflux's "not a prediction, nor a render" make a useful pair for one card
  or line of the film's statement: the ending dramatises sourced numbers and predicts nothing.
- The futures cone is a ready grammar for the ending's shapes. "Projected" = the record's rates carried forward
  (dashed). "Plausible" = the published proposals (ghost lines). "Preferable" = said as whose preference (the 20
  points, the ecologists), never the film's own.

### Gaps
- I found no source on how Planet City or The Great Endeavor mark speculation on screen (title cards, narration
  tense, captions). Only the interview framing above.
- I found no Forensic Architecture or CST project framed as a projection or counterfactual. Searches for FA and
  CST work on the Kakhovka dam found other investigators (NYT, SBU, RFE/RL, University of Houston InSAR), not
  FA/CST.
- Not researched for lack of budget: Office for Political Innovation (Andrés Jaque), Columbia C4SR / Laura
  Kurgan, Lawrence Abu Hamdan and Studio Drift. I found no sourced forward-looking material from them.

## 2. Data-driven projection in documentary graphics

### Takeaway
The documented conventions are few and consistent. Solid becomes dashed at the last observed value, with the
join itself dashed. Uncertainty is shown as shaded bands or fans built from past forecast errors. The research
on reading them says the shape is routinely misread (the hurricane cone is read as storm size), that summary
bands are misread more than ensembles of possible outcomes, and that animated outcomes (hypothetical outcome
plots, HOPs) help, though fast motion makes them anxious. Central banks have been moving from one fan to named
scenarios that tell the story behind the risk. All of this argues for a few named scenarios with their sources
stated, not one smooth fan.

### Cited Findings
- **Solid to dashed at the last actual value.** SAP's design system: projected values dashed, the line switching
  at the last actual point, the connecting segment dashed. Flourish: copy the last actual value into the first
  projection row so the lines meet. Datawrapper offers per-series dashing — [Flourish help](https://flourish-kiln.helpscoutdocs.com/article/397-how-to-create-a-line-chart-with-projections); [Datawrapper Academy](https://www.datawrapper.de/academy/how-to-make-bar-line-chart-combinations-in-datawrapper); [SAP Fiori](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-web/v1-139/ui-elements/chart-vizframe/chart-semantic-pattern/usage)
- A "Today" marker on the axis marks the boundary between record and projection — [LightningChart example](https://lightningchart.com/blog/javascript/dashed-line-chart/)
- The line attributes most used for uncertainty are dashing, luminance, wave amplitude and width (IEEE VIS 2023) — [Perception of Line Attributes](https://virtual.ieeevis.org/year/2023/paper_v-full-1655.html)
- IPCC practice (AR5 draft review): coloured lines per dataset, assessed uncertainty as coloured shading, a
  legend explaining both. The AR6 WGI Visual Style Guide has a section "Representing uncertainty/confidence" (its
  contents not read here). A 2015 review found IPCC figures far more complex than standard presentation graphics — [AR5 SYR comments](https://archive.ipcc.ch/pdf/assessment-report/ar5/syr/drafts/Comments_Topic1_AR5_SYR.pdf); [AR6 WGI style guide](https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2022/09/IPCC_AR6_WGI_VisualStyleGuide_2022.pdf); [EGU 2015](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU2015/EGU2015-13440.pdf?pdf)
- **Fan charts.** The Bank of England's fan charts date from the mid-to-late 1990s and were widely copied. The
  2024 Bernanke review found them hard to read and weak at conveying the stories behind the risks. The Bank began
  publishing explicit scenarios in November 2025. A 2026 BoE staff experiment found fan charts understood and better
  than point forecasts at conveying uncertainty, and recommends fans *with* narrative — [BoE working paper 2026](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/anchors-aweigh-the-effect-of-communicating-forecast-uncertainty); [CNBC on the Bernanke review](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/04/10/bernanke-review-how-the-former-fed-boss-could-shake-up-the-bank-of-england.html); [NIESR DP583](https://niesr.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DP583-Communicating-uncertainty-in-macroeconomic-forecasts.pdf)
- **The hurricane cone.** It was added to the NHC site in 2002. It is widely misread as the impact area, and its
  width as the storm's size. Many assume places outside it are safe. Explaining the convention changed people's
  stated beliefs but did little to their estimates. Summary displays were misread more than ensemble displays, but
  ensembles bias judgments where a line passes over the viewer's point of interest — [NOAA literature review 2019](https://www.aoml.noaa.gov/general/lib/lib1/nhclib/Bibliographies/Cone%20of%20Uncertainty%20Literature%20Review_4_10_19%20%281%29%20%281%29.pdf); [Colorado State 2021](https://natsci.source.colostate.edu/2021/07/12/); [APA spotlight](https://www.apa.org/pubs/highlights/spotlight/issue-155); [IEEE VIS 2020, Millet, Cairo et al.](https://virtual.ieeevis.org/year/2020/paper_w-comm-1023.html)
- **Hypothetical outcome plots** (Hullman, Resnick, Adar 2015): animated draws of possible outcomes beat error bars
  and violin plots for judging reliability, most of all for comparisons. A 2018 study found they helped untrained
  viewers judge trends. The NYT 2016 election "needle" is a HOP-like display: its constant motion made viewers
  anxious and was read as the forecast itself swinging — [Hullman lab](https://www.hullmanlab.northwestern.edu/paper%20discrete-outcome/2015/01/01/hops.html); [MU Collective](https://mucollective.northwestern.edu/project/hops); [Northwestern](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/computer-science/news-events/news/articles/2019/how-data-visualizations-can-clarify-and-confound-uncertainty.html)

### Inferences
- For a film, the cone's lesson is the key one: a band's width will be read as the size of the thing. A widening
  band of "possible cuts" around the record could read as "more damage". Named scenarios (a few, each with its
  source on screen) are safer than one smooth fan, and fit the BoE's move to scenarios with stories.
- HOPs fit the record's grammar well: replay the record's *own* past intervals, resampled, as a few faint possible
  continuations flickering forward. That is a projection from measured rates in which every flicker is a real past
  interval moved forward in time. But pace them slowly: the needle's lesson is that fast motion reads as alarm, and
  the film's rule is "a margin wearing thin, not hours to disaster".
- The project already has the right switch: "dotted means not confirmed". Extend it with one rule, *dashed means
  not yet* (projected). Dotted (unconfirmed past) and dashed (future) must not look alike. Dots for the past, long
  dashes for the future, or vary luminance instead (the IEEE VIS list).
- A "today" mark is a natural held moment. The date counter stops at the last reported state (11 September 2026 in
  the zoom-out) and changes style, e.g. stops ticking and the year turns hollow or bracketed. Every value past it
  is then "projected" or "proposed", never a date stepping on as if reported.

### Gaps
- I found no published style rules from the NYT, FT, Bloomberg or Reuters graphics desks on labelling projections
  or "if current trends continue" lines. Only tool guidance and academic studies. The AR6 style guide's uncertainty
  section was not read in full.

## 3. Counterfactual and "ghost" techniques: the reservoir that would return, the proposed line, the restart

### Takeaway
The film can draw a sourced future without generating imagery. Lay ghost geometry (outlines, levels, lines) from
published proposals and plans over its own observed passes, labelled by kind (proposal, plan, draft, claim) and by
who made it. The Kakhovka question is a ready-made, sourced pair of futures: a 2025 peer-reviewed scenario study
compares refilling the reservoir with restoring the Great Meadow, and an April 2025 field survey documents the
willow-poplar forest that refilling would drown. The peace drafts tie the plant's restart to the dam. The film can
end on that fork as an open question, without choosing a branch.

### Cited Findings
- **Kakhovka scenarios (peer-reviewed).** Pichura, Potravka, Stoiko and Dudych, "Scenarios for the Functioning of
  the Kakhovka Reservoir Territory", *Journal of Landscape Ecology* (2025), compares rebuilding the dam and refilling
  the reservoir, a natural ecosystem with the Great Meadow restored, and a third scenario (only the abstract was
  read) — [Journal of Landscape Ecology](https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/jlecol-2025-0023); [DOAJ record](https://library.kab.ac.ug/Record/doaj-art-ac953c6827614698920eb4116109b343)
- **The new forest (field survey).** Tutova et al. 2025 (*Studia Biologica*): April 2025 surveys found spontaneous
  afforestation of the former bed by *Salix × rubens* and *Populus nigra*. They recommend nature-based solutions
  over technical restoration of the reservoir — [Studia Biologica](https://publications.lnu.edu.ua/journals/index.php/biology/article/download/7356/1790)
- Magas (*J. Landscape Ecology*, 2026): satellite analysis shows the bed as "a rapidly reorganising post-reservoir
  landscape mosaic" — [J. Landscape Ecology 2026](https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/jlecol-2026-0039)
- **Advocacy on both sides (flag as positions).** The Ukrainian Nature Conservation Group argues for reviving
  Velykyi Luh instead of the reservoir. Bankwatch argues rebuilding is not the best option. Farmers, the state and
  energy companies favour rebuilding (irrigation, navigation; the reservoir gave about 22% of Ukraine's freshwater
  fish catch). Sources call the debate polarised — [UNCG](https://uncg.org.ua/en/velykyi-luh-or-kakhovka-reservoir/); [Bankwatch 2024](https://bankwatch.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2024_05_Why-is-rebuilding-the-Kakhovka-dam-not-the-best-option-for-Ukraine-1.pdf); [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/25195); [Yale e360](https://e360.yale.edu/features/ukraine-war-wilding)
- One report cites a 2025 study estimating that 80% of the ecosystem functions lost to the dam could return within
  five years. Its co-author warns that refilling would drown "this young forest" (secondary, unverified) — [e-mc2 / Guardian-derived](https://e-mc2.gr/el/node/29880)
- **The rebuild, as planned.** In July 2023 the Cabinet approved an experimental project: design first, then
  construction once the territory is retaken, with about €114 million allocated and Ukrhydroenergo as client. A
  2025 report: at least seven years to rebuild, of which 2–3 years to fill the reservoir — [Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/amp/rubric-economy/3737389-government-approves-kakhovka-hpp-reconstruction-project.html); [International Construction](https://www.international-construction.com/news/-114m-fund-to-restore-ukraine-s-destroyed-kakhovka-dam/8031181.article); [Ukrainska Pravda 2025](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/06/06/7515988/)
- **The paper's level proposal** (project notes): Vyshnevskyi & Shevchuk 2024, p. 286, propose rebuilding to the
  dead-storage level of 12.7 m, then about 15.5 m. The project's notes already plan "a ghost line labelled a
  proposal" — project `NEXT.md` (section on the cooling pond; the paper is T&F, reference only, never on screen)
- **Peace drafts tie the plant to the dam.** 28-point draft (Nov 2025), point 19: ZNPP "launched under the
  supervision of the IAEA", output split "50:50" between Russia and Ukraine — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/11/21/trumps-28-point-ukraine-plan-in-full-what-it-means-could-it-work).
  The 20-point framework (Dec 2025), point 12: control of the ZNPP and restoration of the Kakhovka HPP. Zelensky
  said the dam must be rebuilt for the plant's safe operation. The US proposed a three-way 33% split; Ukraine
  offered a 50/50 US-Ukraine venture; no consensus was reached — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/zelensky-reveals-20-point-peace-plan-draft-backed-by-ukraine-us/); [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/66845); [Ukrinform](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-polytics/4072955-draft-peace-agreement-zelensky-outlines-20-points-and-reveals-details.html).
  (The project's "point 12" is the 20-point plan's; the 28-point draft has it as 19. Say which draft on screen.)
- Experts told the Kyiv Independent that a jointly controlled restart would be practically impossible in the
  plant's current condition. Grossi: the plant would need a "special status" — [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/splitting-europes-largest-nuclear-plant-between-russia-ukraine-wont-work-experts-say/); [Ukrainska Pravda](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/11/25/8008945)
- **The restart's preconditions.** The IAEA: cooling water and off-site power must be fully resolved before any
  restart. Wells cool the shut-down units but not running ones. A large pumping station was reportedly built to
  draw river water. Russia reportedly built about 80 km of high-voltage line toward Mariupol (Greenpeace Ukraine,
  May 2025). The Russian-appointed director claimed a restart by mid-2027 if the war ends soon. Ukraine holds any
  operation without its regulator's licence illegal. An IAEA official said there was no sign of restart
  preparations — [NEI Magazine](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/plans-underway-to-restart-zaporizhia-npp/); [Electricity Forum](https://electricityforum.com/news/russia-builds-power-lines-to-reactivate-zaporizhzhia-plant); [UWC / Greenpeace Ukraine](https://www.ukrainianworldcongress.org/greenpeace-ukraine-russia-builds-new-power-line-for-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-plant-launch/); [Aaj / Reuters-derived](https://english.aaj.tv/news/330450106); [Yahoo / Reuters](https://www.yahoo.com/news/no-sign-preparations-restart-zaporizhzhia-145951200.html)

### Inferences
- **The ghost grammar** (my proposal, fitting the project's rules): observed things in the film's lit or solid
  style. A *proposal* (an official plan or paper: the 12.7 m level, the rebuilt dam) as a thin outline with no
  fill, labelled "PROPOSAL · <who>, <year>". A *draft* or *claim* (peace points, Rosatom's restart date) as text
  only, quoted, never drawn as geometry. A *projection* (the record's rates carried forward) as dashed. Never a
  rendered reservoir or forest. Water returning would be a contour at the proposed level drawn on the observed
  pass, the land below it hatched ("would be under water at 12.7 m"), never a water texture.
- The refill contour can be computed from the film's own inferred bed (the waterline method, `crossing3d.bed`).
  It is then "inferred × proposed" and must say both: e.g. "where the bed lies below 12.7 m, by our inferred
  heights". This is the strongest single image the research supports: the proposal's line over the regrown forest,
  sourced by the field survey and the passes' own NDVI.
- The plant–dam link in the peace drafts gives the ending its fork without the film inventing one. A restart
  (under any party) needs the water. The water drowns the meadow. The drafts say "rebuild". The ecologists say
  "don't". End on the fork, not a branch.
- Rhyme with the opening: the first card shows the full reservoir (the 2021 pass); the last can show the same
  outline as a ghost over the 2025/2026 pass, labelled as a proposal. The film then ends where it began, now
  explicitly conditional.

### Gaps
- The Pichura et al. paper's third scenario and its findings were not readable (abstract only). Get the full text
  before using it on screen.
- The "80% of functions within five years" figure comes only through a secondary report. Find the original study.
- I found no source on how many metres of the bed the forest occupies below 12.7 m or 15.5 m. That would come from
  the project's own NDVI and bed work (inferred).

## 4. Candidate speculative angles for "Powerlines" (evaluated)

### Takeaway
The angles that fit the film's evidence rules best are those already half-built from its data. (1) The record
carried forward at its own rates (a projection). (2) The forest-or-reservoir fork with the proposal's ghost
contour (a proposal over observed imagery). (3) The restart's preconditions as an unmet checklist (claims shown as
claims). Angles that need imagined events (a meltdown, a dike failure) or unsourced national numbers should be cut
or held to one line of text. My recommendation: a coda in the futures-cone order, "if this goes on" (projected),
then "if it restarts" (a proposal and claims), ending on the meadow as an open question rather than a scenario.

### Cited Findings (inputs the angles rest on)
- The plant was about a fifth of Ukraine's electricity before the war. The figure appears mostly in secondary
  sources (6 × 1,000 MW gross, or 5,700 MW net). Use an Energoatom or Ukrenergo figure on screen — [NS Energy](https://www.nsenergybusiness.com/?p=308957); [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/world/europe/276277/how-important-is-the-zaporizhzhia-nuclear-power); [Ukraine government](https://www.kmu.gov.ua/en/news/prodovzhennya-terminiv-ekspluataciyi-zaporizkoyi-aes-zmicnit-energetichnu-bezpeku-krayini-denis-shmigal)
- Project-held facts (checked in the project's notes, not re-checked here): 26 complete losses of off-site power
  by August 2026 (IAEA); a month on diesel, 23 September–23 October 2025; diesel at the 10-day minimum in
  September 2026; about 20 generators; pond levels 16.67 m (June 2023), 13.54 m (August 2025), 12.27 m (October
  2026, HUR, unverified) against the IAEA's 12 m threshold; cooling water about 250 m³/h from wells now, up to
  80,000 m³/h running; Russian lines about 80 km Berdiansk–Mariupol and 86 km Melitopol–Henichesk; seven repair
  truces since October 2025 — project `NEXT.md` (N1, B3, the pond section), `docs/znpp-heart-plan.txt` (SPECULATIVE PART)
- The restart preconditions, peace points and Kakhovka scenarios: see section 3.

### The angles

1. **The long cold shutdown: "if this goes on"** (probable or projected)
   - *Data:* the record's own intervals (cuts, repairs, complete losses per year), the diesel days, the pond's fall
     per year from the dated levels, staff counts from IAEA updates.
   - *Look:* the unrolled bars or the zoom-out record go on past the last reported date in dashes. A "today" mark,
     then a few slow HOP-style continuations resampled from the record's real intervals. The pond's line descends,
     dashed, to the 12 m threshold, with the crossing date given as a range.
   - *Honesty risk:* medium. Extrapolating rates assumes the war's tempo holds. A crossing date for 12 m may read as

   - *Verdict:* strong. It fits the film's thesis ("what the lines carry is time"), and it is the projection the

### Inferences
- **Recommended coda** (about 60–90 s, ordered by the futures cone):
  (a) the last observed state held, the date stopped ("today");
  (b) "if this goes on": the record and the pond line dashed forward at the measured rates, labelled "projection";
  (c) "if it restarts": the checklist of preconditions with their sources, the peace drafts quoted with which draft
      and point (28-point no. 19, 20-point no. 12);
  (d) the fork: the 12.7 m proposal's contour over the regrown Great Meadow, labelled as a proposal and as inferred
      heights, held in silence. Then the closing card, which rhymes with the first.
- **On-screen marking:** one consistent rule set, stated once (the "method" card at the start could carry it):
  - observed: solid, lit.
  - unconfirmed past: dotted (existing).
  - projected: long dashes, after a "today" mark.
  - proposed: outline only, "PROPOSAL · source".
  - claimed: text only, quoted and attributed.
  - Add tense: captions switch to the conditional ("would", "at this rate") after "today".
  - Sound: the Hum could carry the switch too, e.g. the projected stretch silent or a single held tone. That is a
    design choice, said so in film.json.
- Superflux's "not a prediction, nor a render" can become the coda's own statement in the film's voice: "From here
  on nothing is observed. These are the plans and the rates so far."
- Every proposal shown must name who proposes it. The "preferable" futures belong to someone (Ukraine, the US
  draft, the ecologists, Rosatom), never to the film.
