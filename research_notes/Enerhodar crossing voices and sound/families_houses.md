# Families, houses and villages beside the ZNPP's last two lines (Dniprovska 750 kV, Ferosplavna-1 330 kV), 2022–2026

Researched 29 Oct 2026. Fetch status for each source: **[read]** means the page was fetched and read in full. **[snippet]** means I saw only a search-engine summary. **[meta]** means YouTube metadata from yt-dlp (`--skip-download`, nothing downloaded): id, channel, upload date, length, views, and the description where quoted. I did not watch any of the videos, so what they show on screen, and whether the lines or towers appear, is **unverified**.

Overlap: `research_notes/ZNPP microcosm case studies/nikopol_marhanets.md` and `domestic_scale.md` already cover Kling and Harmash (Kyiv Independent, 2022), Korolkova (NPR, 2022), ABC Nikopol (July 2023), Olha Karakusha (Radio Svoboda, 2024), the Nikopol dog shelter (Olena Voshun), France 24 Observers and the TikTok accounts. I don't repeat them below unless I add something new.

Ethics, for every name below: these are private people named as the outlet published them. Many have relatives in occupied territory (Harmash's mother and sister in Vodiane; Enerhodar residents still inside). Do not add house numbers, streets, coordinates or positions relative to military sites. Use names on screen only as already published, and better still contact the outlet or the person for consent. Enerhodar residents quoted under changed names (for example "Maria Mikhailovna") must stay anonymous.

## 1. Villages on the north bank along the routes (Maryivka, Dobra Nadiia, Marhanets, Chervonohryhorivka, Vyshchetarasivka, Myrove and nearby): life beside the lines or towers, the ceasefire repairs, demining, damage to homes

### Takeaway
I found **no published story about a named household in Maryivka or Dobra Nadiia**, and no account of villagers living beside the crossing towers or noticing the IAEA-brokered truces. Coverage of the repairs is entirely institutional: IAEA, Energoatom, Ukrenergo and wire services. The human material that exists is at the level of the town of Marhanets and the hromada: shelled homes, cuts to local power lines, FPV drones. Most of it is Suspilne Dnipro video reports with named correspondents.

### Inferences
- Coverage of the repair zone (about 3 km from the plant's perimeter, i.e. on the occupied south bank, or on the dry bed) is institutional because journalists cannot reach it. A truce story told from a village ("the night it went quiet") would have to be original reporting, for example by asking Suspilne Dnipro's Marhanets correspondents or the Marhanets city council.
- The closest documented "house + line" pairing on the north bank is Marhanets: shelling that hit homes and local distribution lines in the same strike (the Ukrinform gas-pipeline-and-lines item; the January 2023 village with 500+ homes cut off). These are 6–10 kV/0.4 kV local lines, **not** Ferosplavna-1 or Dniprovska, and the film must not conflate them.

### Gaps
- Maryivka and Dobra Nadiia: no reports found in any language, whether on shelling, the garden cooperative, towers on plots or demining. Searches for "Мар'ївка Марганецька громада обстріл" and "Добра Надія" returned only Marhanets-town results. Next sources to try: the Marhanets city council site, t.me/s/dnipropetrovskaODA (Lysak names villages daily), Nikopol RMA Telegram, and the Marhanets channel t.me/s/typicalmarganets.
- No account from villagers of the ceasefire windows (Oct–Nov 2025, Jan–Feb 2026, Jun and Sep 2026).
- No beekeeper, school, shop or farmer tied to the line's right-of-way was found. (The Videoprizm film below has a chapter called "Шалені бджілки", "Crazy bees", at 23:44. It is unverified whether this is a beekeeper or a volunteer group.)
- The 12 Sept 2023 "seven houses + three local power lines" detail from the brief was not independently re-found. The Ukrinform item above may be it.

## 2. Nikopol families tied to the Ferosplavna substation and the Nikopol Ferroalloy Plant (NZF)

### Takeaway
I found **no worker or family story**. NZF appears only in trade and business coverage (GMK Center, Interfax, LB.ua). The January 2026 stoppage is attributed to the power deficit, and an earlier November 2023 suspension to shelling of the energy system.

### Cited Findings
- NZF and other ferroalloy plants have stood idle since 19 January 2026 because of the electricity deficit, and NZF's output fell from 10,000 t to 3,500 t, making production unprofitable [snippet; the exact page was not identified in the summary] — [GMK Center](https://gmk.center/?p=120401); [GMK Center (UA)](https://gmk.center/ua/news/nzf-prizupinyaie-virobnictvo-ferosplavnoi-produkcii-v-zimovij-period/)

- Owners are listed as Ihor Kolomoisky and Viktor Pinchuk, with a 2024 loss of UAH 3.0 bn [snippet] — [Forbes Ukraine](https://forbes.ua/profile/nikopolskiy-zavod-ferosplaviv-250)
- For the November 2023 suspension and the dispute over it (shelling versus the owner's pressure), see sibling notes: [LB.ua](https://lb.ua/economics/2023/11/07/583251_nikopolskiy_zavod_ferosplaviv.html); [Interfax-Ukraine](https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/economic/948380.html)

### Inferences
- The line's name comes from a substation that fed a ferroalloy works that is now idle for lack of power. That irony is documentable from these trade sources, but no worker's voice for it was found.

### Gaps
- No interviews with NZF workers, their families, or the union. Next leads: the NZF trade union, nikopolnews.net, Suspilne Dnipro, and Nikopol RMA statements on layoffs.

## 3. Enerhodar residents (occupied, or left): the town's blackouts when the plant lost its lines, heating, watching across the water

### Takeaway
The strongest material is from exiled mayor Dmytro Orlov and from relayed social-media chatter, not from named residents. Four named or pseudonymous voices exist:
- "Maria Mikhailovna" (pseudonym, about 70, still in the city; Novaya Gazeta Europe, Nov 2025)
- the music teachers Nataliia (69) and Yevhen (70), who left in Jan 2025
- chess teacher Volodymyr Sukhanov (67), who left
- former plant head Oleg Dudar (Reuters, Aug 2025)

None of them talks about the Sept–Oct 2025 plant blackout. The town-level blackout reports are concentrated in Aug–Oct 2022, July 2024 and July 2026 (weeks without power in the 4th microdistrict, water for about 30 minutes a day, cooking on grills).

### Cited Findings
- **July 2026, the worst documented town crisis.** The 4th microdistrict had no electricity "for several weeks", other districts had power a few hours a day, and water ran "only for a few minutes, about half an hour a day". There is no gas network, so people cook on mangals (grills) and homemade stoves and search for firewood. Orlov: people are "effectively returning to the 17th century". Just over 10,000 people remained in early 2026. The occupiers blame "problems with electricity supply and unstable operation of pumping equipment"; the plant's lines are not named as the cause [read] — [5 канал, Viktoriia Voloshyna, 28 Jul 2026](https://www.5.ua/regiony/tymchasovo-okupovanyi-enerhodar-kilka-misiatsiv-bez-svitla-ta-vody-380648.html) (citing Orlov to Suspilne)
- **July 2024.** "Last week" residents went several days without water, power, mobile or internet. Shops ran on generators, cafés grilled meat on the pavement, pharmacies were hard to find in the heat, and only cash worked. Sources are Orlov's Telegram and residents' comments in local chats; no resident is named, and the outlet did not verify [read] — [ZPRZ.CITY, Svitlana Oliinyk, 23 Jul 2024](https://zprz.city/news/view/blekauti-vidsutnist-gotivki-zakriti-apteki-zhittyam-v-energodari-nezadovoleni-navit-kolaboranti)
- **"Maria Mikhailovna"** (name and patronymic changed; nearly 70; a former nuclear-industry worker who still lives in Enerhodar; interviewed by Zoom). On heating after the units were mothballed: courtyard boilers on mazut or diesel in winter 2023, then steam heat from the thermal plant. She said: "От холода люди не страдали, света не было периодически, но недолго" ("People did not suffer from the cold; there was no power at times, but not for long") and "все чувствуют, что живут на пороховой бочке" ("everyone feels they live on a powder keg"). Nothing on the Sept–Oct 2025 line loss or on the view across the water [read] — [Novaya Gazeta Europe, Olga Musafirova, 11 Nov 2025](https://novayagazeta.eu/amp/articles/2025/11/11/eto-byl-gorod-intellektualov)

### Inferences
- From the published record, the plant losing its last line (for example 23 Sept – 23 Oct 2025) and the town losing power are **not shown to be the same event**. Since 2022 the town has been fed largely through the occupied grid and the thermal plant (Maria Mikhailovna's steam heat). The film should not imply that Enerhodar went dark each time a line was cut unless a source says so for that date. The 2022 events (Aug–Sept 2022, when the plant's own lines and the town's substations were shelled together) are the clearest overlap.
- Anyone quoted from inside Enerhodar is at risk. Use only published pseudonyms and never add identifying detail.

### Gaps
- No diary, Telegram post or exile interview that describes watching the lines across the water, or the town during the month-long blackout of Sept–Oct 2025, was found. ## 6. Is there a documented story of "a house near the power line repaired / destroyed / evacuated"?

### Takeaway
**No.** After searching Ukrainian, English and Russian sources, I found no published story that ties a specific house or family to the Dniprovska or Ferosplavna-1 towers or right-of-way, or to the ceasefire repair zone. There are near-misses:
- Marhanets strikes that damaged homes and local power lines together (local distribution lines, not the ZNPP's)
- Olha Onyshchuk's lost family home in Nikopol and her winter without electricity (RFE/RL, 2023)
- the destroyed house in Marhanets in Vikna's December 2022 report
