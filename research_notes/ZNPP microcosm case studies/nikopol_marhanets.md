## 1. Which 4–6 microcosms are strongest? (synthesis of the questions below)

### Takeaway
There are six concrete candidates with real media. Ranked by how closely each mirrors the film's story and how it can be examined forensically:
(A) the line on the horizon: Ferosplavna-1's towers across the drained bed, seen from Nikopol, struck by a drone in June 2026;
(B) the ninth-floor balcony: a Nikopol pensioner watching launches from behind the reactors;
(C) the Grad launchers beside Unit 6, filmed from the right bank on 2–3 September 2022 and geolocated by CIT;
(D) the netted road into Nikopol: a city under fishing nets, and the minibus hit there on 26 June 2026;
(E) the shoreline from a window: a Nikopol journalist's photo sequence of the water falling (8–25 June 2023), plus residents' TikToks of the drained shore with the plant on the horizon;
(F) the furnaces stopped at the Nikopol Ferroalloy Plant, the factory the backup line is named after (November 2023).
Two further cases are strong but graphic: the Marhanets miners' bus (23 April 2025) and the underground school.

**(A) The line on the horizon: Ferosplavna-1 / the Nikopol substation**
- On the night of 2–3 June 2026, Ferosplavna-1 tripped at 23:44. Energoatom said a Russian drone launched from the opposite bank of the Dnipro hit the "Nikopolska" substation, and IAEA staff confirmed it. The line was the plant's only connection, so the plant blacked out and ran on diesel generators. Energoatom counted this as the 17th outage since the occupation and the 5th of 2026 — [Focus.ua](https://focus.ua/uk/voennye-novosti/756416-situaciya-na-zaes-rosiyani-obirvali-liniyu-ferosplavna-1-z-nikopolya-detali)

### Inferences
- **(A) is the closest mirror** of the film: the same short-range drones that hunt Nikopol's residents reach the substation that keeps the plant's reactors cooled. The plant's lifeline is held hostage by the plant's own occupiers. It also connects to the project's existing Ferosplavna-1 crossing work and its record. It is geolocatable through OSM towers and substations; the ABC bus-stop photo and Energoatom's opposite-bank photo could be matched to the horizon.
- **(B) offers a witness position:** a fixed, high vantage (a ninth-floor balcony) looking across water and sand at the six domes. It could be reconstructed from satellite imagery plus OSM building heights as a viewshed from a single window. The people are named in public reporting, but contacting them goes beyond these notes.
- **(C) is the most "forensic"** in Forensic Architecture's sense: the frame was already geolocated by CIT from the TPP chimneys, and the same chimneys and the camera's southward bearing could be re-solved against the project's georeferenced passes. Whether the launchers were inside the fence remains unresolved, and the film must say so.
- **(D) is the spatial counterpart** of the IAEA's protection zone that was never agreed. The plant got no zone, but the city built its own: a lattice of nets over 45 km and more of roads. It is the most "architectural" object and the best candidate for a drone or vehicle video moving through a netted corridor (splat potential), but I found no specific orbiting video.
- **(E) offers a sequence of stills from one window** (Afonin, 8–25 June 2023), which matches the film's dated-pass logic; satellite passes could be cross-dated against it. The TikToks give handheld shoreline footage with the ZNPP on the horizon, and some may have enough camera movement for structure-from-motion. This is unverified until the clips are downloaded.
- **(F) is a name-object:** "Ferosplavna" is literally the ferroalloy plant. Its furnaces stopped because the grid was unsafe, the same fragility that leaves the reactors on diesel. It is a factory waiting on power, opposite a power plant waiting on one line.
- **Splat potential overall:** none of the media I found is confirmed to orbit a place. The likeliest sources are residents' TikToks of the drained shore and drone footage of netted roads (not yet found). The Truth Hounds and Russian drone-unit footage is top-down and approaching, not orbiting, and is graphic.
- **Ethics:**
  - The bus strikes (Marhanets 2025, Nikopol 2026) and the drone-unit videos show identifiable victims and death. Use them only as located events, never as imagery of people.
  - The residents in (B) and (E) are named, so their consent would matter for any portrait.
  - Children appear in the underground school and in the 26 June 2026 bus strike.
- **Under the project's scope rule**, any YouTube video ingested must be published 2022–2026 and pass the Ukraine scope gate. TikTok and Telegram media fall outside the current pipeline.

## 2. Shelling of Nikopol/Marhanets from the ZNPP site and Enerhodar

### Takeaway
Ukraine has said from July 2022 onward that Russian MLRS and artillery fire on Nikopol and Marhanets from the plant's grounds and Enerhodar. The best open-source piece is The Insider/CIT video of 2–3 September 2022: MLRS firing near a power unit, filmed from the right bank and geolocated by the TPP chimneys. It is not proof the launchers were inside the fence.

## 3. Drone "human safari" in Nikopol: streets, buses, nets, emptying

### Takeaway
This is well documented: hundreds of drone attacks a month, repeated strikes on buses and minibuses (Marhanets 2025; Nikopol and Chervonohryhorivka 2026), and nets over 45 km and more of roads. Specific street names were not found in the sources I could read.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Filming the ZNPP from Nikopol (vantage points, the fire, the plant at night or dark)

### Takeaway
The plant is visible from Nikopol with the naked eye. Published views come from a ninth-floor balcony, a street end, a bus stop and a bridge over the dry bed. The 11 August 2024 cooling-tower fire was filmed. The Nikopol district head said Russians burned tyres in the tower, and Russia blamed a Ukrainian drone.
