# Films, media and sound works with natural sound from the drained Kakhovka bed, nearest first to the Enerhodar crossing (research notes, 4–5 November 2026)

Method and limits, read first:
- **Distances.** They are from the crossing's approximate centre, about 47.552 N, 34.626 E. That point is 3 km east and 4.5 km north of the ZNPP, per `config/conductive-seam.yaml` `crossing.centre_km: [3, 4.5]`. Each place is measured at its GeoNames point in `src/image_evidence/data/ua_places.tsv.gz`, so the figures are town centres, not filming spots. Filming spots are almost never given.
- **YouTube metadata.** It was read with the project's yt-dlp (`-j --skip-download`, plus top comments) for 64 videos. After about 70 requests YouTube began answering "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot", and per-video reads stopped. They were not worked around. Search listings (`--flat-playlist`) still worked, giving titles and durations only.
- **No audio was sampled.** Nothing was downloaded and `raw/` was not created. Every statement about what is heard therefore comes from the uploader's description, title, comments or a press text, never from listening. A short audio check of the top candidates (§1) is still to do once YouTube allows it.
- **Licences.** "Standard" means yt-dlp reported no Creative Commons licence. Such footage is for reference and tracing only: reuse needs the rights-holder's permission.
- **Library.** Nothing was ingested into `library/` or `libraries/`.
- **Overlap.** Related notes already exist in `research_notes/ZNPP speculative ending and ecology/ecology_footage_methods.md` (iNaturalist/xeno-canto bird sounds at Marhanets) and `research_notes/Enerhodar crossing voices and sound/war_sound_nature.md`. This file adds the media list.

## YouTube: walks, drone flights and hikes on the bed with natural sound (nearest the crossing first)

### Takeaway
No video found was filmed on the bed at or under the crossing. The nearest post-breach material is amateur and short:
- Chervonohryhorivka's community channel, about 10 km.
- A Nikopol resident's walk on the bed, Nikopol about 18 km.
- A Nikopol plavni clip from Novopavlivka bay, about 15 km.
- Olexandr L's Nikopol plavni clips, about 18 km.

All of these are on the north (Ukrainian-held) bank. Professional pieces whose soundtracks may carry the forest are Suspilne Dnipro's series with ecologist Vadym Maniuk (Nikopol district, mostly undated spots) and TSN.Tyzhden's UNCG nightingale report. Their natural sound is cut with narration and, often, music. Drones are audible or mentioned in several.

The richest steady supply of bed ambience is the channel **@NovaPriroda_UA** ("КАХОВСЬКЕ ВОДОСХОВИЩЕ ● ВЕЛИКИЙ ЛУГ"): about 108 long videos and 81 shorts, 2023–2026, mostly wordless walks. Its titles place it at **Mar'ianske, about 53 km west** (downstream), not near the crossing.

### Inferences
- The crossing's own reach, between Marhanets/Maryivka and Enerhodar, has no public nature footage after June 2023. The north shore there is a closed border-guard zone opposite the ZNPP. The south shore is behind a 500 m access ban (bfm.ru, below). The nearest material is about 10–18 km along the north bank. On screen, any of it would have to be called "the bed near Nikopol" or "near Chervonohryhorivka", never "at the crossing".
- For pure ambience the most promising leads are Suspilne's Yyf9SZViW4I (sound design praised), NovaPriroda's wordless walks (far away, but long and unnarrated), and the UNCG nightingale recording behind TSN's piece (downstream, with drones).
- All professional pieces mix their sound with narration, and several add music. The raw field audio would have to be requested from Suspilne Dnipro, UNCG or NovaPriroda's owner.

## News, documentaries, radio and podcast pieces filmed or recorded on the bed

### Takeaway
Ukrainian broadcasters carry nearly all of it: Suspilne Dnipro, TSN/1+1, RFE/RL, DW Ukrainian, Frontliner, URSA. International press filmed upstream at Khortytsia (Irish Times, Guardian) or with Maniuk near Nikopol (Guardian photos, July 2024). Only two press texts describe natural sound:
- TSN (nightingales with drones).
- The Irish Times on Khortytsia ("small birds twitter among the young willows", with "muffled booms of artillery").

## Sound artists and field recordists on the bed or the region since 2022, and work near high-voltage lines

### Takeaway
No sound artist is documented recording on the bed near the crossing. Works on Kakhovka are either visual or memory-based, or were recorded upstream (Khortytsia) or downstream (Kamianska Sich). The only openly licensed local bird recordings are iNaturalist user `uabird`'s (cc-by-nc). They are at and north of Marhanets town (10–16 km from the crossing), i.e. gardens, ravines and ponds inland, **not the bed**. No work recorded under or near Ukrainian high-voltage lines as a sound-art piece was found (the project's own proxies are in `research_notes/Line sound proxies/`).

### Cited Findings
- **iNaturalist sound observations** within 25 km of the crossing, since 6 June 2023: 61 in all, every one by `uabird`, cc-by-nc.
  - Nearest, at about 10.5 km: Marhanets, 47.647 N, 34.624 E. Among them a Eurasian Scops-Owl on 22 June 2026 at 01:56, a Red-breasted Flycatcher on 26 May 2026 at 04:39, and Common Rosefinch in May 2024 and 2025.
